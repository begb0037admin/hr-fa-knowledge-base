My Account

My Account page is a user profile page. Only Application or Account Administrators have access to the My Account page. 

To Manage My Account:

  1. Click the drop-down arrow next to your name in the upper-right corner.

    The current account opens.

  2. Fill in the fields or make the desired changes.

  3. You can reset the password only if you sign in as an Application Administrator or Account Administrator. Vault Search Only and General Users have no access to the My Account page, and  an Account Administrator must reset your password.
  4. Click Save.
