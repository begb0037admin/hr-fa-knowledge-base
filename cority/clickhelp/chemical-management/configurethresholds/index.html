Configure Thresholds

You can set inventory thresholds in Regulator. You can also upload a spreadsheet of thresholds using a spreadsheet template.

To add a threshold use one of two tabs:

  • Thresholds by Ingredient/Numbers 
  • Thresholds by Physical Container Inventory

Thresholds by Ingredient/Numbers: To add a threshold, you must specify a CAS number and up to two threshold values and units of measure.

Thresholds by Physical and Chemical Characteristics: To add a threshold, you must specify a unique identifier (ID number or Custom Field) and up to two threshold values and units of measure.

When the product inventory volume reaches the threshold value:

  • Regulator sets a warning flag for the product in the Physical and Chemical Characteristics Report.
  • Your Administrator can configure notifications for you to receive when the container or CAS values reach/ fall below the minimum threshold or reach/ exceed the maximum threshold.  See Customize the Add Inventory Form.

To add a threshold:

  1. Click Regulator Main Menu (). The Regulator navigation pane displays. Select Thresholds from the Configurations list.

  2. Select one of two tabs: Thresholds by Ingredient/Numbers or Thresholds by Physical Container Inventory.
  3. Select location from the drop-down list.

  4. Click Add a Threshold.

  5. The Add a Threshold window displays:

  6. Enter the threshold information in the window.
  7. Click Save, or click Reset to clear the information.

To upload a spreadsheet of thresholds:

  1. Click the Threshold Upload Template link to download the template.
  2. Open the spreadsheet and enter the threshold values. Save your spreadsheet.
  3. Click Choose File and select the spreadsheet, then click Upload.
  4. To view the current thresholds, you can click the Threshold List link to view a spreadsheet of threshold settings.
