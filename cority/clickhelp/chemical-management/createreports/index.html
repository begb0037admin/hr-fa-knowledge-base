Create Reports

Vault supports the following major report types:

Chemical Characteristics—Displays the percentage of ingredients in the products assigned to the current location and the chemical and physical properties of the ingredients.

Parametric Report—Searches for products by virtually any chemical characteristic, including product information, composition or ingredients, emergency overview text, physical and chemical properties, hazard ratings, CLP class, DSD/DPD class (risk or safety phrases), or transportation class.

Personal Protection—Provides NFPA and HMIS ratings for your product library if rated by the manufacturer.

Regulatory Analysis—Indicates whether any of the chemicals appear on a specific regulatory list that was preloaded by your administrator.

Product List—Presents the list of chemicals for your location and can be used to comply with the OSHA Hazardous Communication Standard requirement for a comprehensive chemical list by location.

To create a report:

  1. Click the folder icon next to the Vault header to view available reports.

  2. Select the report you want to run.

    The report form is displayed. Complete the required information as presented.

  3. Click Run Report to generate the report.

After generating the report you can download it in Excel format.
