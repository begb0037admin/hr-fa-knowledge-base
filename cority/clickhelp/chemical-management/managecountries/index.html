Manage Countries

In the Manage Countries page, you can specify the documents and languages settings for the distribution.

The Documents and Languages settings determines what document type(s) and what language(s) SDS to distribute based on the Country parameter in the feed file.

To manage country information:

  1. Select the folder icon in the Distribution header and select Manage Countries from the Configurations menu.

    The Manage Doc Types and Languages by Country page displays.

  2. Click Add Country. The Add Country window displays:

  3. Choose a Country from the drop-list.
  4. Choose the document types to allow.
  5. Choose the languages to allow.
  6. Click Save.
