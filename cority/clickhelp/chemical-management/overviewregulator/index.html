Regulator

Regulator works with Vault and Gatekeeper as an inventory tool. Only products already in Vault are available as inventory in Regulator. If your account includes Gatekeeper, and you attempt to add inventory to a location that is also a Gatekeeper location, Regulator routes your request to the Gatekeeper request form. After the product is approved and in Vault, you can add it as inventory in Regulator.

Regulator supports two types of account users:

Application Administrator: An administrator manages Regulator locations and users, customizes the Regulator inventory grids, configures thresholds for inventory, and makes inventory transactions.

General Users can search and view inventory, manage inventory transactions and run reports.
