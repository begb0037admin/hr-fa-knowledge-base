Uploading Inventory from a Spreadsheet

You can also add, use, waste, dispose, transfer, ship, or correct inventory from a spreadsheet. You can download a spreadsheet template, then enter your inventory information into the spreadsheet and upload it to Regulator.

Alternatively, you can make inventory transactions from the user interface. For more information, see Using Inventory.

To upload inventory

  1. Click Regulator Main Menu (). The Regulator navigation pane displays. Select Upload Inventory from the Actions list.

  2. Click the Download Batch Template link to download the template for the inventory list.
  3. Click Batch Upload Inventory Instructions Sheet to download help for the spreadsheet, including the allowable values.
  4. Open the template file and enter your inventory information. Be careful to enter only allowable values, as indicated in the instruction sheet.

  5. Click Upload Inventory to upload your completed spreadsheet. Your batch appears at the top of the batch table.

When you upload the spreadsheet, Regulator begins processing the uploaded inventory. The Status column in the table of batches first indicates Submitted, followed by Processing. The final status may be either Complete or Exception. If Exception, click the Download to XLS button to view a spreadsheet that includes details about the errors.

Note:  When uploading inventory, Regulator interprets the Product Identifier column in the spreadsheet as representing document IDs. Optionally, if you have custom codes configured in Vault, you can set Regulator to interpret the column as a custom code. To do this, click Configurations and choose Customizations. Then, click the Product Identifier drop-list to view the available values.
