Change Locations

The current location is displayed in the top right corner of the page. This is the location that will be used for any searches you initiate.

When you search from the top level location, all SDSs and related product documents associated with your company display in your search results. In the example screen below, if your location is ABC Company, the results include documents located in the sub-locations, including the city level. However, if your location is a city, your default search results only include documents assigned to that location.

To change the current location:

  1. Click the Current Location button in the top left to view the locations for your company.

  2. Click the symbol to expand any location and select the desired location.
