View and Edit Thresholds Flag Grids

You can view and edit the chemical and physical characteristics for existing inventory.

To view or edit Thresholds Flag Grids:

  1. Click Regulator Main Menu (). The Regulator navigation pane displays. Select Thresholds Flag Grids from the Reports list.

  1. Under the Actions column, click the gear icon for a product and choose Chemical Characteristics.

  2. Edit the characteristics as necessary.

    You can add ingredients by clicking the + button, and adjust percentages as needed.

  3. Click Save to save your changes.
