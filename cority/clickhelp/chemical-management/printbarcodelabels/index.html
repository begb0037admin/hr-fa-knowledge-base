Print Barcode Labels

You can print barcode labels for your inventory from the Search Inventory page. Regulator supports small barcode labels known as "2-up" format.

To print barcode labels:

  1. From the Search Inventory results grid, click the gear icon in the Action column for the product you want and choose Labels.

  2. In the Labels window, click Print small barcode labels, with or without the expiration date.

    Regulator launches a PDF viewer in the browser.

  3. Follow the directions for printing in the PDF viewer.

Note:  For small barcode label printing, use the Landscape format and the label size 30333 1/2 inch x 1 inch (also known as "2-up").
