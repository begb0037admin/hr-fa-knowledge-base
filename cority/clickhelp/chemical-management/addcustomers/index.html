Add Your Customers

Adding customers allows users to record shipment of containers to clients and remove them from the Regulator inventory.

You can add your customers to Regulator through the user interface or using a spreadsheet provided to you.

To add a customer:

  1. Click Regulator Main Menu (). The Regulator navigation pane displays. Select Customers from the Configurations list.

  2. Click Add a Customer. The Add a Customer window displays:

  3. Enter the information about the customer. The Customer Name, ID, and Location are mandatory.
  4. Click Save.

To upload a spreadsheet of customer information:

  1. Click the Customer Upload Template link to download the spreadsheet.
  2. Open the file with a spreadsheet application and fill in the customer information.
  3. Save your spreadsheet.
  4. Click Browse and specify your customer spreadsheet.
  5. Click Upload. Regulator uploads the spreadsheet.
