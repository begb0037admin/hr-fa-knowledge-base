Change Your Language

If your company has subscribed to multiple languages, you can change the language displayed by selecting it from the language list.

To change the language:

  1. Click the dropdown arrow next to your name top right.

    Your current language is shown in the list.

  2. Click the current language selection, then select the language you want from the list.

    The page will now display in the chosen language.
