Email Documents

You can email an SDS document to one or more recipients.

To email an SDS document:

  1. From the search results, click the gear icon in the Action column.

  2. From the Action palette, select Email SDS. The Email SDS window opens.

  3. In the To: field, enter the address of the recipient.

    Note:  You can enter multiple addresses using commas to separate them.

  4. Enter the message text.
  5. (Optional) Check the Copy me checkbox to receive a copy of your email.
  6. Click Send. Vault sends the document as an attachment to the email.
