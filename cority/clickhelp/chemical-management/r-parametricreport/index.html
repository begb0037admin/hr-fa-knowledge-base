Parametric Report for Regulator

The Parametric report searches for products by virtually any chemical characteristic, including product information, composition or ingredients, emergency overview text, physical and chemical properties, hazard ratings, CLP class, DSD/DPD class (risk or safety phrases), or transportation class. 

Note:  The Parametric Report in Regulator is identical to the Parametric Report in Vault, except that the Regulator version includes certain inventory information fields in the Product Information Section.

To create a Parametric Report:

  1. Click Regulator Main Menu () or Vault Main Menu (). The navigation pane displays. Select Parametric from the Reports list.

  2. Enter the information and check the fields you want displayed in the report.

    Expand a section by clicking on the section head to see the available fields from the SDS.

  3. At the bottom of the page, select the Location to search and click Go. You can search Current Location or Current Location and Sub-Location.

    To save the report as a custom report, click Save As and enter a report name.

  4. Click Run Report.

    The report displays. Click Download XLS to download the report in Excel format.
