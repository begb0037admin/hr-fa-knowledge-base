Regulatory Analysis Report

The Regulatory Analysis report allows you to report on the chemicals at a location that are subject to a specific regulatory list. The regulatory lists are preloaded by your administrator.

To create a Regulatory Analysis Report:

  1. Click Vault Main Menu (). The Vault navigation pane displays. Select Regulatory Analysis from the Reports list.

  2. Choose a regulatory list from the Select Type list.

  3. Click Run Report.

    The report displays. Click Download XLS to download the report in Excel format.
