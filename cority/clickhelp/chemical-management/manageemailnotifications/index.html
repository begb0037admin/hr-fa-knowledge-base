Manage Email Notifications

You can adjust the time that Gatekeeper sends out email reminders about pending requests.

To manage email notification timing:

  1. Click Gatekeeper Main Menu (). The navigation pane displays. Select Email Management from the Configurations list.

  2. In the pop-up window, Click the drop-lists to adjust the number of days before Gatekeeper sends a reminder email to the approver.

  3. Click Save.
