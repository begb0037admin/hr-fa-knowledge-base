To sign in:

  1. If you are accessing the Vault from a workstation with the Actio icon on the desktop, just tap the icon and you will be automatically signed in. Otherwise, access the Vault sign-in page from one of the following options:
  • Click the link to Vault from your corporate intranet site.
  • Point your browser to the following url: https://ww1.actiocms.com

  1. When the sign-in page is displayed, enter your username and password, and click Sign In.

If you forgot your password:

Enter your email address and click Forgot Your Password?

Forgotten password request: This option enables users to reset their passwords by clicking on a "forgot your password" link on the sign-in page. An email with a temporary password is sent to the email on the user profile. 

If your user role is Vault Search Only, you need to contact your administrator to reset your password.
