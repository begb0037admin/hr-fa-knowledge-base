Search for Products to Add

You can search for a product from the Basic Search page or the Advanced Search page. Vault displays the results in three tab:

  • Current Location—Products already located in your Vault and is limited to the current location.
  • Company (name)—Products already located in your Vault, including all your locations.
  • Master Library—Products matching your search from the Master Library.

To search for and add a product to your Vault:

  1. In the Basic Search or the Advanced Search page, enter the product information to search for in the product fields.

    Note:   Quick Find does not search the Master Library.

  2. Click the icon in the Action column to display the Action palette.

  3. Select either Add to Library to add the product SDS to your Vault (displayed if allowed).
