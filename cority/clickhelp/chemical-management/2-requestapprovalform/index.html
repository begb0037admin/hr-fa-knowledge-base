Request and Approval Forms

Creating request and approval forms are the first steps to creating an approval workflow. Request forms display to Gatekeeper users when they request a product. Approval forms display to Gatekeeper approvers and provide an opportunity to add more information to the request and either approve, approve with conditions, or decline the request.
