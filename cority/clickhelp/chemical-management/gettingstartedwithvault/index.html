Setting Up Vault

As a Vault administrator, the following tasks will help you get started setting up your Vault.

  1. First, create Vault Locations and specify the document types and languages to allow, and email addresses to notify when Actio revises an SDS in the location.

  2. Click the dropdown arrow next to your name top right. Select Administration from the menu.

  3. In the Administration menu, select Location Management.

  4. Next, create Vault user accounts, specifying their location privileges and roles. From the Administration menu, selectUser Management.
  5. Search for products to add to your Vault. From the Search page, you can search for a chemical by product name and choose the product from the Actio Master Library. Click the Vault tab and enter search criteria.
  6. (Optional) To request products not in the Master Library, click Document Administration and choose Add New Product. Your Actio Customer Services Manager (CSM) reviews your request and contacts you if more information is needed.

If you need help with adding products to your Vault, please contact Customer Service.
