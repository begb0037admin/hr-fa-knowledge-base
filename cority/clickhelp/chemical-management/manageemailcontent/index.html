Manage Email Content

You can configure Gatekeeper to send customized email based on the answers to questions in the request or approval forms. 

For example, you can ask a requestor if they have reviewed the SDS document for the product and are aware of the hazards and safety concerns of the product.  If they answer no, Gatekeeper can send an email with custom content.

To manage email content:

  1. From the Gatekeeper dashboard, click Configuration and choose Email Content Manager.

  2. Choose a Form Type, either Request Form or Approval Form.
  3. Choose a Form from the list of existing forms.

  4. Choose a Question from the drop-list.  In our example, our request form has three questions available as triggers to your custom email.  We chose “Have you received training in the proper use and handling of this chemical?”.

  5. For Answer, choose an answer that triggers the email.  In our example, the No answer is specified.

  6. Click Email and enter the heading and body content of the email:

  7. Specify a Notification Type.
  8. Specify a Notification Role.
  9. (Optional) Click the + button to add another row for another decision path.  For example, you could trigger different email content for a Yes answer in the above example.
  10. Click Go to save your changes.
