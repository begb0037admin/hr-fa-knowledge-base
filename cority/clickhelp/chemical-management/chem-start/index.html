Chemical Management

Release Notes

Cority's integrated suite of Chemical Management applications help you manage the chemicals used in your facilities and keep up-to-date Safety Data Sheets for all products.

SDS Vault is a centralized materials information management system that provides on-demand access to Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and substance-level data to help you comply with worldwide regulatory demands. Our IntelligenTEXT™ technology organizes and manages a manufacturer’s SDS data in defined fields that are structured down to the lowest levels, giving you unlimited search, analysis and reporting capabilities.

Gatekeeper integrates with Vault as a chemical approval and product screening tool.  Gatekeeper administrators create product request forms and approval forms, authorizes users to request products, and specifies approvers that approve product requests.

Regulator is an online substance inventory tracking and regulatory reporting system. Regulator allows customers to track individual containers of any substance within their enterprise.

Distribution is a tool to automatically distribute SDS documents, managing email notifications about SDS updates and keeping your customers and employees informed and up-to-date.
