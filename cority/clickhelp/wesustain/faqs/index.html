Frequently-Asked Questions

General Questions

Q: I cannot log in.

A: Make sure that you are in the correct portal – you can identify it if the company's own login page is shown. If you are in the correct portal, use the “Forgot your password?" function. If you are not on the company's login page, ask a colleague or Support for the correct URL. Support can also reset your password for you.

Q: I forgot my password.

A: On the login page there is a button labelled Forgot your password? After entering your username you will receive a link via email. Open the link and create a new password.

Q: Who do I contact if I want to change my email address?

A: Contact Support by clicking on blue button in the upper right corner of the login page or in the system.

Q: Who do I contact for content-related questions?

A: If you have any questions, please contact the contact person of your company.

Q: Who do I contact for technical questions or problems?

A: Contact Support.

Q:  The language is always wrong when I log in. How can I change and save the language settings?

A: You can change the language settings under My Account (click your name in the top menu bar). On the Preferences tab on the right side you will find an entry for “System language”. The date and decimal format adapts automatically to the set system language.


Q: I received an error page while using the software. The following symbol appears:

A: If you received an error page with the above symbol, contact Support with a brief description of how you received the error.

Content-Related Questions

Q: I set the number of decimal places to '0'. Why do I still see 2 decimal places?

A: The indicators are always displayed in edit mode with all decimal places. The specified number of decimal places is displayed as soon as the indicator is released.

Q: I see an indicator in the Data Management, but I cannot enter any data.

A: This can have several causes:

  • When you open the Data Management, you will see all the top-level indicators of the organizational model (the company as a whole). In most cases however, the values ​​are recorded at lower levels and automatically added up. To see the levels at which the indicator is captured and displayed, open it in Standards & KPIs.
  • The indicator has already been released and cannot longer be edited.
  • Whether values ​​for an indicator can be entered depends on the rights of the user. A user may only have the right to see the indicator and not to edit it.

Q: How can I create a formula?

A: First select the desired indicators. Then you can drag and drop them into the formula field. The operators (+, -, *, /) are added via the keyboard. The validity of the formula is checked and you will be notified if the formula is correct.

Note: Behind the  icon are hidden example formulas.

Q: I cannot access all modules of the software and cannot make any changes.

A: Your user rights do not allow it. Contact the administrator in your company or contact Support.

Q: I accidentally deleted a contact from a stakeholder and now I can't add it again. The system issues a message that the contact already exists or still exists.

A: You can re-add the deleted contact as follows:

  1. Click  Add stakeholder/contact in the top right-hand corner.
  2. Select “Add contact to a stakeholder”.
  3. In the next step, click Add existing contact.
  4. Select the stakeholder to which you want to add the contact.
  5. In the last step, select the deleted contact and add it back to the stakeholder.

Q: The identifiers I have assigned to the indicators are not shown on the left side.

A: The display of the identifiers must be activated explicitly in the product settings. To do so, on the Settings > Options tab, in the KPIs and Data Management section, select the Show identifier column in Data Management and Standard & KPIs checkbox.
