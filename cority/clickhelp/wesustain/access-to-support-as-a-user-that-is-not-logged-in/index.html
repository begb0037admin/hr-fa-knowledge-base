Access to Support – User Not Logged In

If you are not logged into your portal and have a request of a general nature, click the Help link on the login page. 

1Click Help. The Support window opens.


1Since you are not currently logged into the portal, you will have to enter an email address so that the Support team can reply to you.
2Enter your Message in this text field.
3Attach a file, e.g. a screenshot (max. size 5MB).
4Click Send to send the message, or click Close or x to cancel the process.
