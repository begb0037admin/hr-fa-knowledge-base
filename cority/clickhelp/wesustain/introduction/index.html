Introduction

The WeSustain Enterprise Sustainability Management (ESM) provides you with a complete solution for software-based sustainability management. It integrates all relevant aspects, such as performance indicators, reporting, and stakeholder management, as well as operational areas such as CO2 and energy management. In addition, external stakeholders can be integrated easily. Especially for the complex issue of sustainability and decentralized organizational structures, WeSustain offers an efficient, reliable, transparent, and powerful solution with intuitive operation.

WeSustain ESM is divided into four areas; these can be used independently of one another, but automatic data exchange takes place between each area.


The SETUP area supports you in establishing your individual organizational and process structure for sustainability management and in systematizing your KPIs.

The DATA area supports the documentation and control of KPIs. Here employees of different specialist departments can enter performance indicators from their department, follow discussions and manage contacts with other users.

The PERFORMANCE area enables the efficient execution and documentation of analyses. Here you can define priorities, formulate effective implementation strategies and manage your sustainability performance. Furthermore, this area brings completeness, clarity, and conformity to the production of reports.

The STAKEHOLDER area offers analysis tools, systematic methods for stakeholder dialogue and standardized processes for improving the company reputation.
