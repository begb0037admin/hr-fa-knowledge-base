SSO (Single Sign-on) Authentication

If you are using the SSO authentication method, the login screen will have Proceed to Login and Guest Log In options. If you click Proceed to Login, you will be directed to your organization’s login page.

  1. Enter your login credentials in your organization’s login page.
  2. Read the Privacy Agreement. When you are finished, click Accept to indicate you read and understand the agreement.

If you decline the Privacy Agreement, a message will display asking you to close the browser.
