Discussions

At many points in the portal, you can use Discussions to engage with other users to exchange information on questions in specific areas, or to discuss best practices and possible procedures. You can: 

  • create discussions yourself
  • reply to other users' posts
  • follow discussions that interest you
  • invite other users to your discussions.

The discussions are displayed at the location where they were created.

1Click  Create Discussion to open a window with two text fields (Title and Content). Click Save to create the discussion, or Cancel or x to cancel the process.
2For existing discussions that you would like to follow, click Follow. This causes the discussion and all its posts to appear under News Feed on your Dashboard. Discussions that you created or have participated in are followed automatically. Click Unfollow to unfollow a discussion.
3To invite users to the discussion, click Invite/uninvite User. A window displays all users; click  in the Action column beside the user(s) you want to invite. Click Close or x to cancel the process.
4To delete any discussions and discussion posts that you created yourself, click Delete below your post.
5To add a post to a discussion, enter your comment in the blue text field below each discussion and click Reply. Your post then appears in a green area below the main post. Older posts shift downwards when a new post is added so that the most recent is always shown first.
