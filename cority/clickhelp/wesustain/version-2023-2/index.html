Version 2023.2

Release Date: July 23rd (first group deployment) and July 30th (second group deployment)

WeSustain version 2023.2 provides the following features:

  • In the Benchmark Module, users can create internal benchmark values and external benchmark values (industry standard values) and can now compare a company’s performance internally or against other organizations.
  • The Scenarios & Target Module can be used for ‘What If Projections’ to gain meaningful predictive analytics by considering forecasts, targets, and the impact of corrective actions. This can enable your organization to make more responsible resource-use decisions, navigate through future sustainability obstacles, increase organizational resilience, and drive more effective communication across teams. 
  • Target Values can now be automatically aggregated on higher levels of organizational units.

    This Functionality is optional. Users can still manually define target values for higher-level organizational units. 

  • In the Collections & Approval module, sub yearly KPI’s can now be assigned to users with a target date and have sub yearly reminders sent to their emails. This allows users to have more granular and transparent means to manage and keep track of data collection processes and deadlines.  
  • In the Collections & Approval module, users can now view all KPIs for up to the past two years that require an action. The desired time periods of the current and/or past year can be selected in settings.
