DATA

The DATA area includes the following modules:

  • Data Management allows you to enter the data for the previously defined indicators (KPIs); they can also be checked and approved by the manager(s).
  • Collections & Approval allows users and managers to view, edit, and approve KPIs that are assigned to them.
  • Goals & Actions allows you to define your goals and responsibilities in sustainability management and comprehensively review and control the status of the achievement of goals at any time.
