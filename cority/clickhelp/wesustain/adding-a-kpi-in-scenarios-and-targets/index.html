Adding a KPI in Scenarios and Targets

To Add a KPI


 


1On the left navigation panel, click Data.
2Click Scenarios & Targets.
3Click the Add KPI tab. Choose KPI window opens.

 


4On the Choose KPI window, select a KPI.
5Click Select.



6The added KPI displays in the KPI list.
