Access to Support – User Logged In

If you are already logged into the portal, follow the procedure below to access the Support area.


1Click Help. The Demo Portal Support window opens.
2Enter your Message in this text field.
3Attach a file, e.g. a screenshot (max. size 5MB).
4Click Send to send the message, or click Close or x to cancel the process.

Our support team processes your request in accordance with the Service Level Agreement arranged with your company.
