Comparing a KPI with a Benchmark in a Chart

To compare a KPI with a benchmark in a chart



1On the left navigation panel, click Data.
2On the Data menu click Data Management. The Data Management module opens.
3Click a KPI where a benchmark is set.
4Information on the KPI will display to the right.
5Click the Data Tab.
6The graph displays the KPI values and Benchmark values.
