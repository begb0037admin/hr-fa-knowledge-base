Analytics

The Analytics module enables you to analyse collected data directly in WeSustain. Data from Data Management can be filtered based on specific criteria and displayed in tabular form in Analytics.

To meet the different needs and tasks of each user, there are two role profiles for this module:

  • Data Editors have unrestricted access to Analytics and can access all the data contained in the system via this module regardless of whether or not they have restricted rights in other modules.
  • Data Viewers have limited access to Analytics. Users with this role can only load and execute queries that were approved and given to them by editors.

 Data Viewers see all results of the queries that are set public, regardless of their rights.
