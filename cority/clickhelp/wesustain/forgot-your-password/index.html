Forgot Password

If you have forgotten your password, click Forgot Password on the Portal Login page. 


1Click Forgot Password. The reset password screen displays on the Portal Login page. 


1Enter your WeSustain Username (not your first or last name).
2Click Send. You will receive an email with information on the rest of the procedure.
3If you reached this page by mistake and still know your password, click  icon to return to the login page.

After you have sent us your username, you will receive an email with a link that will bring you to the following screen:


1Enter your New password here and then enter it again for security reasons.
2Click Save to save the new password.

After the new password is saved, you will be returned to your portal's login page where you can log in with your new password. If you have further difficulties, contact the administrator at your company.
