Aggregating KPI Targets

Aggregating target values allows companies to see what their accumulated total target value for a given year is for their organizational unit.

 

1On the left navigation panel, click Data.
2Click Scenarios & Targets.
3Select either a numeric or formula KPI.
4Information on the KPI will display to the right.
5Click the Targets tab.
6Select a higher-level organizational unit.
7Click Aggregate targets. Note: Aggregated targets can only be set when on the lower organizational units targets have been inputted.

 


8On the Aggregate Targets prompt, click OK.



9The aggregated target values of the higher-level organizational unit appear.
