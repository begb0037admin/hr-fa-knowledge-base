STAKEHOLDER

The STAKEHOLDER area allows efficient management of internal and external stakeholder relationships. You can maintain extensive contact databases and retain an overview of all activities and documents. In this manner, you create a basis for standardized and group-wide communication with essential stakeholders.

This area comprises the following modules:

  • Materiality Matrices allows you to create matrices to compare factors in two dimensions.
  • Donation Management allows you to document and manage all donation requests, as well as assign them to stakeholders.
  • Stakeholders allows you to organize your contacts with stakeholders and to structure the stakeholders in groups. Under Stakeholders you can file information on each individual stakeholder. As well as contact details, you can also store assessments and expectations for criteria that you can define freely (e.g. "Proximity to the topic of sustainability" or "Influence on the company" etc.). You can also create user-defined groups on the Groups tab. You can then use these to clearly group your stakeholders.
