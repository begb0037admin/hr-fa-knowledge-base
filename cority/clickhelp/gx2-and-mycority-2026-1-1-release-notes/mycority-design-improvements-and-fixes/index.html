myCority Design Improvements and Fixes

The table below lists the design improvements and fixes we have made in the myCority 2026.1.1 release. The items are organized by component, and the components are arranged in alphabetical order (generally).

Item #Component(s)Description
CCC-26334Environmental

On an Inspection/Survey/Observation with a questionnaire, if in myCority the user assigns an action to a multi-select questionnaire question then clicks back and adds an attachment and saves, then adds a comment and saves, that will no longer create any additional Findings in GX2.

 

CCC-27285
Ergonomics - Risk Assessment

For an employee who is in a ‘Group and Team’ that is assigned multiple Risk profiles, those profiles will now be assigned successfully to that employee within Demographics. That will enable the employee to open any assigned assessments.


CCC-27477Ergonomics – Risk Assessment

When applicable, the office ergonomics assessment SCORM will now load successfully in myCority.

 

CCC-25812IH – Risk Assessment

For Risk Assessment in myCority, when the status of the record is ‘Done’ or ‘Approved’, the ‘Clone and Inactivate’ option is now available.

 

CCC-26402IH – Risk Assessment

In myCority, for IH Risk Assessment, a SEGs sublist that should not be appearing no longer does.

 

CCC-26393
IH and Safety - Risk Assessment

When applicable, in myCority, the Exposure Group Employee form (for creating or updating) will now persist to open in the ML-000377 layout even if the user's role(s) are liked to custom and the ML-001002 and ML-001001 layouts.


CCC-25305Importing

When the Import Utility is used to create myCority users, that process will no longer select the Portal check box for any new or existing GX2 users.

 

CCC-27475Patient Engagement

When applicable, a Management Referral completed in and submitted from myCority will now be both retained as is in myCority and reflected as expected in GX2.

 

CCC-27186Platform Administrator

The ‘Prohibit guest users in myCority’ system setting is now selected by default for new customer sites, and remains editable.

 

CCC-26367Platform End-User

An issue that was requiring a hard browser refresh to log into myCority has been resolved.

 

CCC-22808Reporting – Metrics

An issue that was causing an assigned campaign with metrics, when opened in myCority, to open without any metrics has been resolved.

 

CCC-25740Reporting – Metrics

An issue that was preventing the saving of a Campaign metric using myCority has been resolved.

 

CCC-27180Safety – Incident

For Safety Incident Investigations in myCority, wherever applicable, site security now applies to the ‘Job’ field.

 

CCC-25990Safety – Inspections

When an Inspection questionnaire is submitted using myCority, a corresponding Create Record BRA configured for the ‘Safety Incident’ entity in a business rule configured for the ‘Questionnaire Response Header’ entity will now function properly.

 

CCC-26372Safety and Quality – Incident

Safety or Quality ‘Incident’ display rules that should render certain fields as mandatory will now do so in myCority.
