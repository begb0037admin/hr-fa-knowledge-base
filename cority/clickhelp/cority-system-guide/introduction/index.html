Introduction

Core (GX2) is an EHSQ software platform designed for managing and reporting on occupational health, safety and environmental data, as well as assisting to maintain legislative compliance in these areas.

The application is typically used by medical professionals, industrial hygienists, safety managers, and case managers at large companies.

The application is primarily an employee-centric system, in that most of the data in the application is linked to a specific employee.
