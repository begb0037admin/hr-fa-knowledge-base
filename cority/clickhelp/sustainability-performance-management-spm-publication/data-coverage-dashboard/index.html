Data Coverage Dashboard

The data coverage dashboard enables clients to see how many months of their data is from each estimation type, and how much data is missing.

Data coverage is the total months during a period classified by a particular estimation category. Coverage classifications are at a datasource level unless the node is closed with no data during this time period, in which case it is excluded from analysis.
