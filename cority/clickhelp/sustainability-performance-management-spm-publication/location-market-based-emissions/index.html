Location & Market-Based Emissions

Environment calculates location-based emissions as standard. The location-based emissions factor that is used is defined by the consumption type, methodology and country of the data source as well as the content of the uploaded data. Location-based emissions represent the average emissions for consumption in a given region.
Note  Location-based emissions can be recorded directly for Value Chain data sources in the Input form.

Market-based emissions calculations can be used to reflect supplier specific emissions.

Market-based emissions are calculated according to the following priorities:

  1. Using Emissions factors defined in admin settings
  2. Using emissions factors uploaded in the inputs section
  3. Using residual mix emissions factors
  4. Location-based emissions factors

Admin Defined Emissions Factors

Please see the Environment – Customisation – Emissions Factors section for further detail on how to upload and manage these factors.

Inputs-Defined Emissions Factors

Market-based emissions factors can be uploaded for all consumption or business activity uploads. This is possible through the input form, multi-node upload or via SFTP.

Residual Mix Emissions Factors

Residual mix emissions factors represent the emissions intensity for a region’s electricity grid after removing REGOs and other forms of renewable energy certification. Residual mix emissions factors are available out of the box for European countries through the RE-DISS publication, Australia through publications by NGA, and Canada & United States through the Green-e publication.

Location-Based Emissions Factors

Where none of the calculations above are possible, which occurs when an emissions factor is not available, market-based emissions factors are assumed to be the same as location-based emissions.
