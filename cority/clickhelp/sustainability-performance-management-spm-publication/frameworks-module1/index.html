Frameworks Module

The Frameworks module enables you to collect and report data for sustainability frameworks such as GRI, CDP, SASB or for other internal company KPIs. Selecting this module from the welcome page will enable you to either respond to the questionnaires, approve responses or to analyze your data in the Dashboards and reports.
