Customized Pins

Most screens within SPM can be pinned to the homepage - just look for the ‘Pin to Start’ link on any page. Once this is clicked it is possible to give the screen a customized title and one of three images to use as the pin icon.

These Pins are found at the bottom of the homepage. Once you have created pins, they provide shortcuts to your most accessed screens.
