Energy Compliance Dashboard

The Energy Compliance dashboard can be found under ‘Management > Energy Compliance Dashboard’ drop down menu and provides users with an overview of all energy data that should be included in an energy audit.

Data can be viewed as ‘Consumption’ or ‘Cost’ by selecting the appropriate tab. On each tab the data is displayed by energy type and fuel type. The ‘Settings’ box allows users to select the required view, node, and time period to analyze the data. It is possible to toggle between ‘Consumption’ and ‘Cost’ data using the tabs above the graphs.

On the ‘Consumption’ dashboard, all energy is displayed in kWh. Where energy is uploaded in other units (e.g. liters of fuel), the consumption is converted to kWh so comparisons can be made across different sources of energy consumption.­
