Environment Module

The Environment module enables you to measure and report resource consumption and GHG emissions at any organisational level. Selecting this module from the welcome page will enable you to either Upload data, analyse your data in the Dashboards, Variance Tracker or Initiatives Savings, manage your Targets and Compliance related activities and Report your emissions.
