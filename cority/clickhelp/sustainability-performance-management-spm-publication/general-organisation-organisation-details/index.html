Organisation – Organisation Details

Organisation Details is the area within Admin where the information about your company is held. This includes your company contact details, who the main point of contact is for your company, and where your company logo can be imported into your site.

The default language can also be set in organization details. This language applies to all users unless the user has otherwise specified using the language dropdown available at the top right of the screen.
