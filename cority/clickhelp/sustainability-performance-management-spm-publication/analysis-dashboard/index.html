Analysis Dashboard

GHG emissions and consumption are displayed in dashboards that enable you to analyze performance over time and identify any gaps or anomalies in your data. From the ‘Dashboards’ dropdown menu, select ‘Analysis’ which will display carbon emissions by data source, scope, consumption and cost.

The counter at the top-left of the screen displays the total tons of CO­2­e per Unity by Period. The pie chart and associated table splits this into data sources, and the bar graph displays your emissions over time with a dynamic View-specific baseline and trend line. Below the bar chart is a table containing the specific numbers for each time period and the overall totals.

At the top-right there is the option to ‘Toggle Legends’ on and off, similarly each item within a legend can be hidden / revealed by clicking on the legend entry. Different tabs can be selected at the top left to analyze emissions (tCO2e), emissions by scope (scope), consumption or cost data.

If you wish to drill-down into either a specific emission type (pie-chart), or specific time period (bar graph), you can click on that item within its respective graph, and it will load the new display. Alternatively, you can change the parameters of the data you are analyzing by clicking ‘Settings’.
