CCC-3276: Three Surveillance tabs (sublists) from the Demographics module are now available in the Medical Chart module

  • The following sublists from Demographics > Surveillance can now be added to custom Medical Charts layouts:
    • SEGs Linked to Employee Job
    • SEGs Related To Individual Employee
    • Agents Related to Employee Job
  • The sublists are the same in structure and functionality as, and will always remain in sync with, their equivalents in the Demographics module.

Impact to Existing Users:

  • A physician recording encounters can now see the full history of SEG enrollment in the Medical Chart, instead of having to navigate to the Demographics module.

Additional Configuration Considerations:

  • Beyond what is described above, no additional configuration is required.
