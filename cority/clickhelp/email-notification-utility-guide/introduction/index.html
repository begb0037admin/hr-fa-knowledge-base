Introduction

To enhance Cority’s notification functionality, the Cority Email Notification Utility is designed to send reminders and/or notification emails for time-sensitive records such as Investigation, Corrective Action, Equipment Calibration, and Scheduling.

The Email Notification Utility is embedded into Cority. To start the automated email sending process, Cority needs to receive a signal from the Email Notification Utility client. The Email Notification Utility client works with Microsoft Windows’s Task Scheduler to provide you with full control of the automated email sending process start time.

Note: The Email Notification Utility is a very data-intensive application. The processing of the email notifications is a long and resource-intensive process. Please be advised that it is recommended to run Email Notification Utility during off hours as it may degrade the performance of the Cority application.

Please refer to the System Guide, 4.17 Email Notification Utility Settings for additional information about the Email Notification Utility settings screen.
