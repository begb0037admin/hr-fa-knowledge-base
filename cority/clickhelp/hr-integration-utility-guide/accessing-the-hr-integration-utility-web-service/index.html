Accessing the HR Integration Utility Web Service

You can determine the URL of your HR Integration Utility web service by looking at your login URL. For example, if your login URL is https://example.com/gx/security/login.rails, your HR Integration Utility Web Service URL is https://example.com/gx/WebService/MEIPService.svc.
