Contracts

Use the Contracts section to view and manage all of the contracts uploaded into Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS). Click on ‘New Contract’ to search for a supplier to add a new contract record against and click on a current contract title to manage the existing record. You can assign multiple labels to multiple suppliers by selecting the tick box next to each supplier and then using the Assign Labels drop down.

The fields under the ‘Contract Details’ tab of an existing / new contract is customisable for Buyers; please get in contact with Cority Support to discuss the configuration process. Relevant documents (e.g. signed contract copies) can be uploaded in the ‘Contract Files’ tab, users can be given read and / or right access to the contract information in the ‘Access Rights’ tab and notifications around termination and notice period dates can be managed in the ‘Notifications’ tab.
