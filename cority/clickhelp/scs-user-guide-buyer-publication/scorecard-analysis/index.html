Scorecard Analysis

In the scorecard analysis section, select the scorecard for which you want to display your Suppliers’ results. If you want to score specific groups of Suppliers, select the relevant groups or labels in ‘Change Settings’. To view the percentage score for each question of your scorecard, click the Scorecard Performance expander. To view your Top 10 and Bottom 10 performing Suppliers, click the Supplier Scores expander.
