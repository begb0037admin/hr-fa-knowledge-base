Welcome to Cority’s Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS). This solution will enable you to collect and review your Suppliers’ information, contract details and supporting files through a secure and private online solution. Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) can satisfy many different requirements; enabling Buyers to monitor compliance, identify potential areas of risk and undertake in-depth Supplier analysis.

For Buyers, Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) is a powerful information management tool that facilitates the collection and analysis of Supplier information. With customisable features including questionnaires, contract management, scorecards, risk flagging, risk management, audits, group comparisons and benchmarks, Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) improves the efficiency of managing important Supplier information.
