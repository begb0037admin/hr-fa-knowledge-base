Supplier Compliance

In order for the Supplier compliance dashboard to function properly you will need to have uploaded your master list of suppliers (see Supplier Master List) and enabled the self-assessment functionality either manually (see Self-Assessments) or through automatically through the master list upload.

This dashboard enables you to track the compliance of your Suppliers. The graph and table shows how many of your target Suppliers have registered on Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) and how many of those have completed all of their required questionnaires.

You can amend the time frame you are looking at to suit your reporting needs. The table can be filtered and exported, making the process of contacting the appropriate Suppliers more efficient.

The default settings mean that Suppliers are only shown as ‘published’ if they have completed all of their required questionnaires, and none of these questionnaires are yet required to be resubmitted. If you wish to change the compliance rule – the frequency with which a questionnaire is required to be updated, then go to Admin Settings > Buyer Code.

Below the Compliance graph there is an exportable table of Suppliers that can be searched and filtered on various parameters.
