Supplier Management > Supplier Master List

The Supplier master list enables you to track the Suppliers that you are targeting to join Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) through the compliance and questionnaire dashboards. Click ‘Upload  new  suppliers’ and use the template provided to add Suppliers to the master list.

When a Supplier registers on Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) they become automatically ‘linked’ to your master list and their self-assessment questionnaire completion starts being tracked (if required to complete them). Once a Supplier is linked the contact information used to register that Supplier will be added to that of your master list. You can edit, delete and archive any of your Suppliers on the master list.

When uploading the supplier master list, you should populate the Buyer Code column if the supplier is required to complete self-assessment questionnaires. This way their progress can be tracked through the dashboards. Those suppliers that are not loaded with a Buyer Code, will be stored as ‘Master List Only’ suppliers, with Buyers able to store documents and contracts against them.

Should you wish to automatically group or label your suppliers then this information can be included on the master list. If loading to multiple groups of labels then the names should be separated by a comma. The group and label names will have to be created prior to the loading of the master list.
