Risk Management – Supplier Profile

By clicking on an individual supplier record you can see all of the individual supplier risk flags, set up actions by risk, and access and sign off actions.

Clicking ‘Responses’ on a risk will show you the history of supplier responses to this question. Clicking ‘History’ will show you the risk history of this question.

The ‘Ongoing Alert’ button enables you to choose if you would like to be alerted to the same risk in future supplier responses. For example, if a supplier has provided the required policy document in response to the Action, then you would want a risk flag to be raised if they did not provide the document in future questionnaire versions. However, if they provided evidence that they did not need the document, then you would accept the same response in future and not want a risk flag triggered.
