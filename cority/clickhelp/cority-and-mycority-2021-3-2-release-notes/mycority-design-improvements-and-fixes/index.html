myCority Design Improvements and Fixes

The table below lists the design improvements and fixes we have made in the myCority 2021.3.2 release. The items are organized by module and the modules are arranged in alphabetical order.

Item #Component(s)ReportedDescription
CCC-6693Appointments, SchedulingN/A

In myCority 'Appointments', on a Schedule Entry layout, if the values for both Provider and Schedule Activity are displayed as radio buttons, values can now be changed and the layout saved without causing any unexpected issues.

 

CCC-6822Platform – AdministratorN/A

In GX2, when a custom layout gets updated, the change(s) are now reflected more quickly in myCority.
