Questionnaires

Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) uses a combination of questionnaires and uploaded supporting files for the collection of all relevant data.

A list of questionnaires that are required by your selected buyers are displayed for each reporting period in this section. There is also a list of Optional questionnaires that you can choose to complete by selecting them from the drop-down menu.

It is important to note that Required Questionnaires will only display once you have selected the appropriate Buyer from the list using their Buyer code (see above). As a result Buyer specific questionnaires will not be visible until you have completed this process.

Reporting periods

Your Reporting Period is determined by the Reporting End Date you entered when you registered. You can add a new Reporting Period for any year before or after your current reporting period by clicking ‘Manage Periods’. You can toggle between your reporting periods to view and edit questionnaires by using the View drop down menu.

You should always complete your latest reporting year in order to maintain an up to date profile.

Required questionnaires

The list of questionnaires you see in this section is determined by the Buyers you have selected and the Questionnaires that they require you to complete. You can hover your mouse over the icon next to each questionnaire to see which of your Buyers requires it.

Optional questionnaires

In this section, you can choose from the questionnaires that are optional. These include questionnaires to enable you to calculate your carbon, waste and water footprints (refer to the Data Checklist in the reference library to find out what data you will need to complete these questionnaires). Select a questionnaire from the drop-down menu and click ‘Add’ to add it to your reporting period.

Answering questionnaires

Click on the questionnaire name to launch the wizard and start entering your information. Every step of the questionnaires includes a help button for each question to provide further explanation of the information required. You can save your responses at any time and come back to them later. In order for your buyers to view your completed questionnaires, you need to publish each questionnaire with the ‘Publish’ button on the final step. Buyers are not able to see your data until you have published.

Once you have published a questionnaire it becomes locked. You may unlock a questionnaire at any time if you wish to edit the information. You must then publish it again in order for your Buyer(s) to be able to view it.
