Public Profile

This section allows you to set-up a public profile which can be searched by registered Buyers in Supplier Portal. You can decide whether to make your profile available to Buyers by clicking ‘Visible’ / ‘Hidden’. By making your profile visible you will allow Buyers who you are not currently matched with to view and make contact with your company. Click ‘Edit Profile’ to create or make any changes.
