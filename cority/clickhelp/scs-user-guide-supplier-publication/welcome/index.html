Welcome

The Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) solution enables you to enter your organisation’s business data, company information and supporting files and share it with your buyers to help you fulfil requests for information and demonstrate compliance.

As a member of Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) you will be able to upload data and supporting files and publish it to multiple selected buying organisations. You are therefore in complete control of who sees your data as you choose which buyers you share it with. This will save you time and effort and ensure your data and information is presented in a consistent, accurate way.
