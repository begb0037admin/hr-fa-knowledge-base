Buyers

Use this section of Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS) to find and select the buyers to which you want to publish your data. You will need a unique Buyer Code for each buyer that you wish to add to your ‘Selected Buyers’ list.

Find and select your buyers, or invite new buyers to join to Supply-Chain Sustainability (SCS), through the Select Buyer function. Once you have found your buyer in the list, enter their Buyer Code, and click ‘Select’ to add your buyer. Click ‘Save’ once the buyer is added.

After selecting a buyer, the company name will be displayed in your Selected Buyers list with a status of “Accepted”. By clicking on the ‘View supporting documents’ link by their name, you will be able to view and download the documents they have made available to their suppliers. This option is only available if the buyer has made documents available to suppliers.
