CCC-9100: Using the Appointments module, a medical practitioner can now provide Telehealth care for employees

Opt-In

  • This enables your organization to expand its medical care to employees who cannot physically travel to your clinics.
  • To support this, the Appointments module has integrated with Zoom.
  • In order to use this functionality, your organization will require the new Cority ‘Remote Expert’ license key, which is not included with the ‘Occupational Health’ license key.
  • This feature set allows those booking Telehealth meetings to do so exclusively within the Appointments module. They will not need to log into Outlook or Zoom to book the meetings or generate the Zoom links.
  • Note: The Zoom meetings will not occur within GX2, but will be created and scheduled through the Appointments module, represented on the calendar within the Appointments module, and the link to the meeting will be provided in the appointment itself on the Appointments module calendar.
  • There are three new system settings:
    • Zoom Credentials Email
    • Zoom OAuth Client ID
    • Zoom OAuth Client Secret
      • The latter two are global settings that must be configured for OAuth authentication between Zoom and GX2.
      • The third is present in System Settings and in My Settings. Leave the one in System Settings empty (blank) and provide your (the logged-in / scheduling user’s) email address for the one in My Settings. If an email address is not provided for that setting and you are creating a Telehealth schedule entry in the Appointments module, the email address on your User record will be used instead. If no email address is provided there either, the email address on the Employee record associated with your User record will be used. If no email address is provided for any of those, a notification will be displayed asking you to provide one before creating any Telehealth schedule entries.
  • Additional configuration through Zoom Marketplace is required for connection authentication between Zoom and the GX2 Appointments module. If you are required to perform this configuration on your own, ask your Cority contact for instructions or assistance.
  • Once all of the above is properly configured, you will see a new ‘Telehealth Appointments – Zoom Connected’ toggle at the bottom right of the Appointments module screen. Note: If either of the ‘Zoom OAuth Client ID’ or ‘Zoom OAuth Client Secret’ system settings is not configured, the toggle will be hidden.
  • To start creating Telehealth meetings, click the toggle on. The Zoom ‘Sigh In’ dialog box will open.
    • Use the Zoom ‘Sign In’ dialog box To connect to your Zoom account.
    • Note: Only people who will be creating, modifying, and cancelling meetings require a Zoom account. We recommend your organization purchase your corporate zoom account(s) because the free account has additional functional limitations. See the Zoom platform website for information on Zoom account limitations. For example, a scheduling user with a purchased Zoom account can perform only 100 API calls (meeting-related actions like booking, modifying, cancelling) per day.
    • Once connected to your Zoom account, the ‘Zoom Connected’ toggle in the Appointments module will remain on.
  • The workflow for creating an appointment (schedule entry) in the Appointments module remains the same, except that you will now select the new Telehealth check box on the Schedule Entry layout. When you have populated the necessary fields on the Schedule Entry layout and click Save, GX2 will request that Zoom create a Zoom meeting and send its link. That link will then populate in the new ‘Employee Zoom Meeting Link’ field on the schedule entry. Note: Before creating any Telehealth meetings in the Appointments module, you will first have to add both the ‘Telehealth’ and ‘Employee Zoom Meeting Link’ fields to your Schedule Entry layout.
    • Once the schedule entry is saved, the link will remain with it (tied to the scheduling user, the invited employee, and the schedule entry) for use when it is time for the meeting to begin. The Scheduling User (creator) will be the meeting host, and the activity you selected on the schedule entry will be the subject of the Zoom meeting when it starts.
    • Note: Zoom does not permit the value for the duration of a Zoom meeting to be zero, so if you do not enter a value greater than zero for the duration of your schedule entry, you will be notified you must change that.
  • For use in letter templates (to be used in emails), the ‘Employee Zoom Meeting Link’ field has been made available as a merge field. That is, while creating the schedule entry, if you have previously created a letter template using that merge field, you can select ‘Use Email Template’ for the ‘Send Email’ field value. When you save the schedule entry, the employee will be sent an email containing the meeting ICS file (for accepting, declining, etc. the invitation) and the Zoom meeting ID and link. If the employee accepts the invitation, the meeting (with Zoom link included) will then be added to his/her Outlook (or other integrated) calendar.
    • The meeting can also be emailed to supervisors or other stakeholders.
  • Note: For more information on configuring and working with the Appointments module (including creating, modifying, and cancelling schedule entries), see the GX2 User Guide.
  • If you move the meeting on the calendar, or open, reschedule, and save it, you will be asked if you want to send an updated invitation email with new Zoom meeting link to the employee.
  • To remove the Telehealth (Zoom) meeting component from the schedule entry, simply open the entry, deselect the Telehealth check box, and click Save. The Zoom meeting will be cancelled and the Zoom link will be removed from the schedule entry, but the schedule entry itself will be retained.
  • If you cancel the meeting (schedule entry) altogether while the Telehealth check box is still selected, the both the schedule entry and the Zoom meeting will be cancelled.
  • In GX2 Administrator tools, there is a new ‘Meetings Log’ that lists every meeting in the GX2 system, and for every meeting displays Created Date, Meeting Date, Created By, Meeting Link, etc. For each meeting, it also distinguishes between the creator and host, if the creator configured the meeting host to be someone else.
  • This Telehealth functionality is supported by the Appointments module’s ‘Reassign Multiple Appointments’ action. Support by the ‘Cancel Multiple Appointments’ action will be added in an upcoming release.
