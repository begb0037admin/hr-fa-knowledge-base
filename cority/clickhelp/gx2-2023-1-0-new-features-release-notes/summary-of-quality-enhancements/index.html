Summary of Quality Enhancements

The table below lists the smaller design improvements we have made in the Quality Cloud. The items are organized by component and the components are arranged in alphabetical order.

ItemDescription
CCC-10947

For writing business rules for Quality Non-conformance 'Disposition' records, the Disposition entity is now included in the Business Rules 'Entity' drop-down list.

 

CCC-10393 and 10143

A Management of Change user can now link any applicable 'Safety' items to a 'Quality' Management of Change record so that the change request is fully documented.

To support this, the following options have been added to the default Actions menu on Management of Change layouts:

  • Add Related Safety Audit
  • Add Related Safety Finding and Action
  • Add Related Safety Inspection
  • Add Related Safety Incident
  • Add Related Safety Action Plan
  • Add Related Safety Management of Change

Note: A user whose role is not granted the Safety cloud will still be able to link the Safety records, but not open them.

For information on working with the Safety or Quality cloud, or the Management of Change module, see the GX2 User Guide.
