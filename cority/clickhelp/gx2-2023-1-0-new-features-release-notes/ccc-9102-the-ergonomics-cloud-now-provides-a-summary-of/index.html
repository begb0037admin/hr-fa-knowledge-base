CCC-9102: The Ergonomics cloud now provides a summary of an employee’s personal risk

Opt-In

  • This will help ergonomists attend to people who are at risk.
  • The ‘Manage Employee Risk’ screen now has two tabs: Risk Inventories and Employee Summary.
    • Risk Inventories retains all of the information from the original ‘Manage Employee Risk’ screen.
    • The new Employee Summary tab has four sections: Key Activities Completed, Recent Notes, Recent Email, and HR Profile.
      • The first three are list views with relevant columns, and using a picklist each can be configured to show 3, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, or 200 records at a time. The default is 5.
      • The ‘Key Activities Completed’ section tracks log-ins, desktop actions, self-assessments (with status), related training (started/completed), changes to risk (improved or worsened), resolution of issues, etc.
      • The 'Recent Notes' section lists the 5 most recent notes that were written about the employee using the notes feature located in the 'Manage Employee Risk' list view.
      • The 'Recent Emails' section lists the 5 most recent emails sent to the employee from the Personal Risk or Online Training (for personal risk) modules.
      • HR Profile has two columns and will draw pertinent information (personal, contact, work-specific, GDDLOFB, etc.) about the employee from the Demographics module.
