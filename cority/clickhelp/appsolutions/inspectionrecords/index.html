Inspection Records

The completed Inspections, Forms, Equipment Inspected, and resulting Corrective Actions are represented in the Management System by task instances and related Process Workflows that the App automatically creates and associates to the appropriate objects to document the inspection and inspection results.

The Process Workflows are:

  • Inspection (EIA-Inspection): This is the parent workflow that represents the inspection.
  • Inspection Section (EIA-InspectionSection): Child workflows of the Inspection, one for the General section and one for each inspection point included in the inspection.
  • Corrective Actions (EIA-CorrectiveAction): Child workflows of the Inspection Section for which each corrective action is created.

The process workflows should not be edited. The InspectionApp needs these workflows in order to function.

Inspection

The Inspection itself is represented by the EIA-Inspection Workflow AND the specific task instance of the Inspection Task from which it was generated, the Source Task. The inspection workflow captures all of the inspection points included in the inspection, the status, and the inspector. This inspection workflow is also referenced on the Source Task, and triggers the update of its Source Task completion status.

The Task and the Workflow are tied together by a document associated with the task that represents the inspection type and whose name is then used to create the correct workflow according to the selected inspection type. The document has no other use in the app.

Findings

Findings are created during an inspection based on a response to a questions. One more responses on a question may cause a finding to be created (if defined) or findings may be created ad hoc. Corrective Actions can be added to findings during the Follow-up to the inspection.

Inspection Form

The Inspection is broken into several sections, one for general questions and one for each inspection point. Each section of the inspection form is represented as a child workflow of the Inspection, of type EIA-InspectionSection. This Inspection Section workflow is populated by the app with all of the question IDs, questions, answers, and comments (including any triggered sub-questions) provided during the inspection and is associated with the relevant inspection point.

For the Inspection Point section this workflow is only associated with the related inspection point.

Corrective Actions

Corrective Actions can be created as follow-up to an inspection. These Corrective Actions are represented as EIA-CorrectiveAction Workflows which are child workflows of the EIA-InspectionSection workflow and appear on the Corrective Action Calendar based on the workflow's due date.
