Review Corrective Action

If you receive an email notification of a corrective action review assigned to you, you can follow the link in the email to go directly to the corrective action review form.

If you do not have an email notification, you can find Corrective Actions to view or complete in the IncidentApp by various methods. See Find Corrective Actions.

To review a Corrective Action:

  1. In the Corrective Action completion form, review the Actual Action taken.

  2. Choose one of the two options:

    • Accept and Close Action: The corrective action will be approved and closed.
    • Request Revision: The corrective action will be sent back to the assignee. You can edit the Recommended Action first with your instructions before initiating this option.

If you click Save, your edits will be saved, but no action will be taken and the corrective action will remain open at the review step.
