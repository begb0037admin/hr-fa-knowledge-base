Moving a Text Box on a Portal Page

To Move a Text Box on a Portal Page

  1. In the Portal, navigate to a Portal Page with the Text Box you want to move, and then click the Page Settings  icon.

  2. Scroll to the Text Box you want to move on a Portal page.

  3. Click anywhere on the Text Box except the bottom right, and drag the Text Box to anywhere on the Portal Page.
