Investigate Incident

After an incident has been created and details provide, it may be sent to an investigation team for further investigation. The Investigation is an optional step of the incident workflow.

To complete the investigation:

  1. Open the main incident form.

    The Current Step (Investigation) is indicated at the top of the form and in the Process Bar, if enabled.

  2. Complete the fields provided for the investigation.

  3. Click Save to save your changes.

Other actions you may take (depending on the action buttons available) include:

  • Submit for Review: Sends to manager for approval. This is an optional next step of the incident workflow.
  • Request Additional Details: Sends the workflow back to the Collect Incident Details step for more information.
  • Log Lost/Restricted Time: Create a log for lost or restricted time and log status changes.
  • Enter Root Cause: Creates a root cause analysis form to complete.
  • Log Action: Create a corrective action.
