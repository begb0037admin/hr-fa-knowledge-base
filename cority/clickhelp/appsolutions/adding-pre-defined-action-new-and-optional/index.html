Adding Pre-defined Action: New and Optional

Creating a new action that is optional, provides users with more options to choose from in terms of next actions to take based on their specific situation or process.

  1. On the Predefined Action window, in the source section, click New Action and Optional. 


    1. Click the Select Field Action drop-down textbox and click a Field Action.
      Additional fields display in the Add Predefined Action window. These fields will vary depending on the Field Action selected. 
    2. Type in the Predefined Action name, in the Predefined Action Name text box.
    3. Fill in all required fields.
    4. Click Save.
