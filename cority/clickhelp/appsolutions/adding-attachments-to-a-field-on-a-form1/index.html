Adding Attachments to a Field on a Form

By adding attachments (documents or pictures) to a specific field on a form, a user can include more information with a response.

Add the attachment feature to a field

  1. From the main menu dropdown, select Designer to open Forms Designer.
  2. To select a specific workflow step, select a Form Type, and then select the desired Workflow Step.
  3. Click the Fields ribbon.

The Fields ribbon will expand with a list of field types and two sections: Header Fields and Field Sections. 

  1. Click the ˅ icon next to the desired field.


  1.  To the left of the field, click the + icon. 


The field's section will expand.

  1. At the right in the Field Attachments section, toggle on Field Attachments. 
    The Field Attachments section will expand, displaying the Attachment Label field. 


  1. By default, the Attachment Label field value is Add Attachment.
    To change the value of the field, click within the Attachment Label field and type a field name.


  1. At the top right, click Save. 

Add attachments to a field on a form

  1. Under the field, click Add Attachment.  

This could be labelled Add Document, depending on what is specified within Forms Designer.  



The Choose File window will open.

  1. Click browse.



The File Explorer window will open.

  1. Select an attachment, and then click Open.
  1. Click Add.
    The attachment will upload. 


The system tracks and maintains the connection between the field and the attachment.

  1. Once attached, to view the attachment at any time, click it.
    1. If the attachment is a document, its status while downloading will display at the bottom. Once downloaded, the document can be opened.
    2. If the attachment is an image, the image will open in your browser automatically.
