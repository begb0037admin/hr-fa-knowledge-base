Map View

The Map view displays the facilities in the Search list by map location. Each facility is represented by a bar on the map. The bar indicates the number of open vs. closed inspections or corrective actions. You can switch between displaying Inspections or Corrective Actions on the top bar.

  • Blue indicates Open.
  • Green indicates Completed.

Expand the map to full screen with the "expand" button. This hides the navigation panel, the facility details panel at the top and the facility sidebar list.

Select Inspections or CAs to show the activity type you want to view.

Search by date range using the date filter at the top.

Zoom in or out for regional views. Click and hold on the map and move the cursor to shift focus.

Select the bar for a facility on the map to view the inspections for that facility.
