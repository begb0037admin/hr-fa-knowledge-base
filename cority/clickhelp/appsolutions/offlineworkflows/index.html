Completing Workflows Offline

You can view and complete workflows offline, and upload changes once you have reconnected. Before working offline, you need to bookmark the app in your browser so you can access it later.

To bookmark the Portal App for working offline:

  1. Go to the Portal in your browser.
    If you have access to other apps and functions, open the app in its own tab using the pop-out button on the sliding menu.

  2. Add the page to your browser bookmarks.

If you close your browser and re-open it later, use the bookmark to go to the page. You will then be able to access any data that you have added to your offline cache. When internet connection is restored, the system will automatically switch to online mode and request sign-on.

These instructions apply to working offline any custom app using the WAB framework.

To complete work offline: 

  1. Select Access Offline to download current items to work with offline.

  2. Select Download Now.
    The progress indicator will show that the download is in process.

  3. When the download is complete, you can disconnect or safely move out of connection range.
    The Working Offline button indicates the disconnected status.

  4. When you are ready to work, use your app bookmark to open the app, f you have closed the browser or navigated away.
  5. Complete your work as usual and save any edits.
    The changes will be saved to your cache.

  6. Once you are reconnected, the Upload Available button will be available. 

  7. Click Upload Now to upload your changes immediately or click Review Offline Data to view the changes, then click Upload.
