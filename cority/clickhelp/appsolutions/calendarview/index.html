Compliance Calendar View

The Calendar view of a workflow panel or task panel gives you a convenient view of compliance activities by day, week or month.

The calendar will show either workflows or tasks, depending on the panel type, but not both.

To access the calendar view:

  1. Select the calendar icon on the panel bar at the top.

    The Calendar has Day, Week and Month Views.

  2. Select the period you want to see.

Use the arrows next to the Date selections to navigate to next or previous period.

Click the dropdown on the date indicator on the left to change the date range.

In the Day and Week views, you can scroll to see more events. Use the plus/minus signs to expand or contract items.

To see scheduled items for a week, select Week. Scroll to see multiple events. Use the plus/minus signs to the left to expand/contract an item, or click Expand All/Contract All.

In the Monthly view, select a day in the calendar to see scheduled items for that day listed to the right of the calendar. Use the plus/minus signs to expand/contract an item. You can also select a symbol in the calendar to expand the item summary on the right, or hover over the symbol to see the name.

Click the View link at the bottom of the summary to go to the form to view or complete an item.

Calendar Keys

The symbols for each item indicate its status: Complete, Open or Overdue.
