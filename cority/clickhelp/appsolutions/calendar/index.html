Using the Calendar

When you first log in to the InspectionApp is the Calendar. The name of the selected facility is shown at the top of the calendar, which shows the inspections that are scheduled, in progress, or completed, as well as the corrective actions for the facility.

Select the facility you want to view from the facility sidebar.

If the facility sidebar is not displayed, click the sidebar button to display it.

Select Inspections or CAs to show the activity type you want to view.

Expand the calendar to full screen or collapse with the "expand" button. This hides the navigation panel, the facility details panel at the top and the facility sidebar list.

Click on the Inspection or Corrective Action shown in the calendar to view a pop-up with more details. You can then view or edit the inspection or corrective action details by clicking the Go to ... button in the pop-up.

To change calendar display, you can select the month, week or day button. Use the arrows to go forward or backward in the calendar.
