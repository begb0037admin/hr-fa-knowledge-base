InspectionApp Overview

Release Summary

With the InspectionApp you can easily create and monitor inspections for multiple Inspection Points and related corrective actions.

Users can:

  • Create and complete an inspection
  • Work offline to complete an inspection in the field
  • Assign corrective actions
  • Respond to and document a corrective action
  • Conduct a follow-up review of corrective actions
  • View dashboard summaries and key statistics for inspections and corrective actions

This InspectionApp User Guide describes the process and functions for the global InspectionApp as initially provided. Your implementation may differ in some respects, chiefly in the content of the forms used. Other customizations in the workflow process may have been done to accommodate your organization's specific business practices.

Browser Support & License Information

Apps are best viewed on an HTML5 compliant browser. Supported browsers are:

  • Chrome (desktop, tablet, Android)
  • iOS Safari (Mac, iPad, iPhone)
  • Edge (desktop, tablet, Android)
