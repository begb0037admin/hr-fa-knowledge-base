Permissions

Users of the App are system users who are associated with the workflow roles appropriate to the functions they need to perform. Users may be members of more than one role.

Some typical roles that may be set up initially for the App are listed below. You can create other roles as needed for each step (or process) in the workflow.

WAB-Process Submitter: The users who can create a primary App Item.

WAB-ProcessLead: The users who can update, view, remove, edit the fields for any of the forms.

WAB-ProcessReviewer: Users who need to manage (create, edit, approve...) the items.

WAB-ActionAssignee: The users who can be assigned to actions.

WAB-ActionReviewer The users who review actions.

Creator: The person who creates the form/record. This user can generally view/edit all fields for the records they create. This role is automatically assigned.

Assignee/Previous Assignee: The person to whom the actions/steps are assigned. This role is automatically assigned.
