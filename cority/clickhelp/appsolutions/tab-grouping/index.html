Tab Grouping

Tab Grouping allows you to group child or grandchild workflows into a single tab, based on workflow type. The tabs will display on the left navigation menu, and will provide quick access to all of the items related to a workflow. 

Each tab can be expanded to display the child workflows for that group. The columns displayed in the list of child workflows will reflect the headers that have been selected for the respective forms. 


To enable Tab Grouping:

  1.  Open the Form Designer and select the 'Grouped' Display Tabs option.
  2. Select your desired form properties and click Save.
