Complete Corrective Action

If you receive an email notification of a corrective action assigned to you, you can follow the link in the email to go directly to the corrective action completion form. This will depend on how notifications have been set up for your system.

If you do not have an email notification, you can find Corrective Actions to view or complete in the IncidentApp by various methods. See Find Corrective Actions.

To complete a Corrective Action:

  1. In the Corrective Action completion form, enter the Actual Action taken.

  2. Click Submit Action for Review button at the bottom of the screen.

    If you click Save your edits will be saved, but the action will not be marked complete.

  3. Select the assignee for the review. Enter a few characters of the name, then select the name from the matches offered.

  4. Click Assign.
