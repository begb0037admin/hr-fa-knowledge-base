Edit Workflow Column Display

You can choose to show or hide columns in the workflow table.

:  You can use this feature to filter for the columns you want before downloading data to Excel. Only those columns that are visible in the table will then be downloaded.

To choose columns to show or hide:

  1. Select the Filter button on the right side of the panel header.
    The column names are shown.

  2. Uncheck columns to remove them from the panel display.

  3. When you are done, click OK.

You can also re-order columns in the table view by dragging and dropping.

To re-order the columns in the table view:

  1. Select the column header and hold the cursor over it until the move icon appears.

  2. Click and hold, then drag and drop to the new position.
