Conducting an Inspection

You can easily create inspections for multiple Inspection Points and monitor progress with related corrective actions. The steps involved in the inspection process include: 

  • Create an inspection
  • Complete an inspection
  • Create findings and corrective actions
  • Document completion of a corrective action
  • Conduct a follow-up review of the inspection
  • View inspections in progress or complete
  • Create new Inspection Points
