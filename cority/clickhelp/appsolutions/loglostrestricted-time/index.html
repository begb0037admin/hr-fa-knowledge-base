Log Lost or Restricted Time

For OSHA Recordable Injury or Illness incidents you can log Lost Time or Restricted Days.

To log lost or restricted time:

  1. On the initial incident form, check the OSHA Recordable box.

    The Log Lost|Restricted Time form will be added to the form set.

  2. Select Log Lost|Restricted Time from the buttons at the top.

  3. In the Lost Time Summary form, click New.

  4. In the pop-up window, enter the effective date (the date that lost or restricted time began or there was a status change).
  5. Select the Medical Status from the dropdown list (Lost Time, Restricted Time, or Full Duty)

  6. Enter an Explanation of Status (change).

  7. Click Save.

    You will be returned to the form and the new record will be shown in the Lost/Restricted Time table. You can add another row for a status change, such as date of return to full duty.

  8. To edit an entry, select the gear icon next to the record and select Edit.
  9. To delete an entry, select the gear icon next to the record and select Remove.

The user must have the appropriate permission to delete an entry.
