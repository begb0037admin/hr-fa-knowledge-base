Download Charts to Image or PDF

You can download charts to image or pdf, for use in reporting, presentations, or for other purposes.

To download charts as an image or PDF:

  1. Got to a panel with a chart displayed. 

  2. Click the download button.


    1.  To download a chart as an image, click Chart to Image. 
    2. To download a chart as a pdf, click Chart to PDF.
