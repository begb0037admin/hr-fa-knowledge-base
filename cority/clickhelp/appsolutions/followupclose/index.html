Inspection Follow up and Close

After the inspection form has been completed and any corrective actions created, you can follow up on the inspection and check whether corrective actions have been completed.

When all corrective actions are complete, you can close the inspection.

To follow up on an inspection:

  1. Select the inspection from the Calendar or from the search screen.

  2. In the inspection summary dialog, select Go to this Inspection.

    The inspection will open on the Follow-up step. The corrective actions will be displayed.

    The corrective actions are listed, with the status of each corrective action (open or closed) indicated. If all corrective actions are complete, the inspection can be closed.

  3. To close the inspection, click Close Inspection.

  4. Click Yes to confirm.
    If there are open corrective actions that required an action, a message will be displayed informing you that you need to complete the actions on the open corrective actions prior to closing.

Closing the inspection will attempt to close all the corrective actions that are still open. If the Actual Action Taken field is required and is not complete, closing the inspection will not be allowed. You will need to complete the corrective actions first.
