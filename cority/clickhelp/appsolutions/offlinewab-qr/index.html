Quick Reference for Working Offline

These instructions apply to working offline in the Incident App or any custom app using the Workflow App Builder framework.

To complete work offline:

  1. Select Work Offline to download current items to work with offline.

  2. Select Download Now.

    The progress indicator will show that the download is in process.

  3. When the download is complete, you can disconnect or safely move out of connection range.
    The Work Offline button indicates the disconnected status.

  4. When you are ready to work, use your app bookmark to open the app, if you have closed the browser or navigated away.
  5. Complete your work as usual and save any edits. The changes will be saved to your cache.

  6. Once you are reconnected, click Upload Available.

  7. Click Upload Now to upload your changes immediately or click Review Offline Data to view the changes, then click Upload.
