Natural Joins

WorkflowTypeVersionStep

Example: Get workflow steps, step action, status and form template for workflow types.

select

t.Name as WorkflowType,

s.Name as Step,

a.Name as Action,

s.StatusName,

s.FormTemplate.Name as Form

from WorkflowType t

join WorkflowTypeVersionStep s on t

join WorkflowTypeVersionStepAction a on s

Permissions:  Manage Workflows right is required to query this table.
