Natural Joins

Requirement

Example: Get Requirement Templates and associated requirements

select

r.name as Requirement,

rt.Name as RequirementTemplate,

rt.DriverAuthorityType,

rt.DriverAgency,

rt.Media

from Requirement r

join RequirementTemplate rt on r

order by rt.Name

Permissions:  Manage Requirement Templates right is required to query this table.
