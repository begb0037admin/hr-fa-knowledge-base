Date Manipulation

DATEADD

Add specific <number> of <type> to <date>

<type> can be: "yyyy", "quarter", "qq", "q", "mm", "m", "dayofyear", "dy", "y", "dd", "d", "week", "wk", "ww", "weekday", "dw", "hour", "hh", "minute", "mi", "n", "second", "ss", "s", "millisecond", "ms"

Syntax

DATEADD(type, integer, date expression)

Example: Add 365 days to createdOn of facility

Select

DATEADD(dd, 365, CreatedOn)

from Facility

ROUND
