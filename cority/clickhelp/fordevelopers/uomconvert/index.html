UoMConvert

Note: The 'UoMConvert' function is only available for the Chemical Management EQL.

Subheading

Syntax

UOMCONVERT (Amount, UOMFrom, UOMTo, [SpecificGravity])

Parameters

Amount- amount of source unit of measure

UOMFrom - source unit of measure

UOMTo - destination unit of measure

SpecificGravity – Optional, specific gravity to convert between Volume and Mass

Example:

select top 1

UOMCONVERT (1, 'Milligrams','Pounds')

from Container c
