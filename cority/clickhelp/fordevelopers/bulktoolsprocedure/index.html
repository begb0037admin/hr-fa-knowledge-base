Procedure for Using Bulk Tools

The general procedures for using the Bulk Tools:

  • Obtain the Excel template files for the tools you need. Read the instructions for preparing the files and creating any necessary template objects in the system.

  • Prepare your upload files according to the instructions for the tool.

    Column heads are color-coded, with red indicating required fields. The required format for each field is also indicated in the column head. The last worksheet in the file may also contain instructions and tips for usage.

  • Break large files into smaller files for testing.

    To assist, you can use the file-splitter tool (Prepare Upload Files Tool). We recommend your job files be limited to no more than one hour when run on production. You can estimate the time after running your tests in the Sandbox environment.

  • Upload, test and debug a sample of your job files in the Sandbox environment.
  • After testing and debugging in Sandbox, submit job files to your EMS Services representative of Customer Services to schedule your bulk upload job in the Production environment.

When scheduling your bulk uploads to Production, consider running on off hours and weekends to minimize impact to users. Also, take into consideration other scheduled operations in the system so as to avoid conflicts. Discuss timing with your EMS representative if you are unsure.

Note:  Save all your bulk tool build documents in a secure location. Modifications or corrections that may need to be made later will be much easier if you can retrieve your original files.
