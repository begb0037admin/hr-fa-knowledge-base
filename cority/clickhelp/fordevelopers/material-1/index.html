Example: Get material and associated Material Activity Template and fields.

select

m.name,

m.description,

m.MaterialActivityTemplate.Name,

m.MaterialActivityTemplate.CustomField.Name

from Material m

where m.name like '121361'

Permissions:  Manage Materials and Activities right is required to query this table. Manage Material Templates is required to query MaterialActivityTemplate fields. Manage Material Templates and Manage Custom Fields rights are required to query MaterialActivityTemplate fields.
