Summary of Occupational Health Enhancements

The table below lists the smaller design improvements we have made in the Occupational Health cloud. The items are organized by component and the components are arranged in alphabetical order. Descriptions of any larger features follow the table.

ItemComponent(s)Description
CCC-11581Audiometric

In the Audiometric module, 'Significant Threshold Shift' has been added to the 'Hearing Classification' tab.

This impacts the Alberta audiometric standard only.

It is defined as a change in hearing threshold relative to a reference audiometric test that indicates one of the following:

  • An average shift of greater than or equal to 10 dB in either ear (30 dB or more combined) at 2,000, 3,000, and 4,000 Hz.
  • A shift of greater than or equal to 15 dB at 3,000 or 4,000 Hz.

 

CCC-9717CorAnalytics

CorAnalytics has been enhanced. It can now report on data from the following modules:

  • Immunization
  • Case
  • Audiometric

 

CCC-10904Immunization

On the Vaccine (or Immunization) Inventory list, when inventory is low and the color red is used as an indicator, that no longer adversely affects the loading of the list.
