CCC-11365 and 9951: An administrator or support person can now log into myCority as another user, and view the software, view data, and make changes as that user

  • This makes it easier to trouble-shoot for users.
  • To use this feature, the role of the impersonating user must be granted the new User.ImpersonatemyCorityUser security action, which is located under the Admin module.
  • To impersonate a user, navigate to Administrator > Users, click the user's name, and then on the user's record click Actions > View myCority Application As This User. An entry for this action will be added to the Login Log. Important: The 'View myCority Application As This User' action must first be added to a custom version of the User layout.
  • Note: The impersonated user's role must be granted the 'General Security' security module.
  • While you are impersonating a user, a notification will display across the top of the screen to remind you, and all record history will be recorded under your (the admin) user name. For example, all saves (Modified By, Created By, etc.) will be recorded under the impersonating user's name.
  • All changes made though will be logged under the impersonated user's name.
  • The impersonated user's security configuration and system settings will be respected while the impersonating user is navigating and/or making changes.
  • A ‘Back to Your Account’ button will be provided at the top right of the screen. This will take the impersonating user back to his or her GX2 account and enable the impersonated user to login again.
  • Medical (O.H.) records will not be accessible to the impersonating user.
  • The following functionality is not supported while impersonating a user: ePrescribing, refilling ePrescriptions, and Translations Mode.
  • Important Security Note: For clients who must adhere to GDPR or other such regulations, this gives the user permission to impersonate any other user, so potentially the permission to view records and data he or she does not have access to otherwise, and view sites outside of his or her security settings. This privilege is best limited to support personnel who require such broad-level access to perform their jobs.
