What is the context of this article?

A Charge Band always has a Total Charge Price List (the full cost of the service). Optionally, you can add an Adjustment Price List if you want to control how costs are split between the third-party payer and the patient.

Adjustment Price List options include:

  • Benefit Maxima – maximum amount the third-party payer will pay.
  • Patient Contribution – maximum amount the patient will pay.

Both options can be a fixed value or a percentage. 

What is the purpose of this article?

This article will explain how to configure a price adjustment list, including:  

  • How to configure a Benefit Maxima Price List type
  • How to configure a Patient Contribution Price List type

How to configure a Benefit Maxima

When Benefit Maxima is used, a third-party payer pays up to the Benefit Maxima amount (or %) and the patient pays the remainder of the Total Charge Price.

For example, when an Employer with Benefit Maxima (Fixed Amount) is configured with a Total Charge Price of £300 and the Benefit Maxima of £150, the Employer is invoiced £150 and the Patient is invoiced £150 (the remainder). 

To configure the Benefit Maxima, go to Admin > Billing rules:

  1. Click a Price List from the list of Billing Rules. 
  2. From the Price type drop-down, choose Benefit Maxima (or Benefit Maxima % of total). 

  3. In the Service Prices list, enter a Benefit Maxima amount or percentage. 
  4. After creating an adjustment Price List, add the new Price List to the Charge Band. For more information, see Adding a Price List to a Charge Band.

How to configure a Patient Contribution amount

When Patient Contribution is used, the patient pays up to the Patient Contribution amount (or %) and the third-party payer pays the remainder of the Total Charge Price.

For example, when an Employer with Patient Contribution (Fixed Amount) is configured with a Total Charge Price of £400 and a Patient Contribution of £50, the Patient is invoiced £50 and the Employer is invoiced £350 (the remainder). 

To configure the Patient Contribution, go to Admin > Billing rules:

  1. Click a Price List from the list of Billing Rules. 
  2. From the Price type drop-down, choose Patient Contributions (or Patient Contribution % of total). 

  3. In the Service Prices list, enter a Patient Contribution amount or percentage. 
  4. After creating an adjustment Price List, add the new Price List to the Charge Band. For more information, see Adding a Price List to a Charge Band.

Scenario: Billing Rules for Employer-Paid Consultations

Test Company is an employer that the Chamber provides services to. For consultations, the total charged price is £750. The employer requires the patient to contribute £50 toward the cost of the appointment.

  1. Define Price Lists.
    • Total Charged Price 
      1. Create a Price List of type Total Charged Price.
      2. Add an item for Consultation priced at £750.
    • Patient Contribution
      1. Create a Price List of type Patient Contribution.
      2. Add an item for Consultation priced at £50.

  2. Configure Billing Rules - Add both Price Lists to the Test Company Charge Band:

    • Patient Contribution Price List rule

    • Total Charged Price list rule

  3. Book an Appointment.
    When an appointment is booked, two invoices are generated:

    • Invoice 1: £700 charged to Test Company (Total Charged Price £750 minus Patient Contribution £50).

    • Invoice 2: £50 charged to Patient.
