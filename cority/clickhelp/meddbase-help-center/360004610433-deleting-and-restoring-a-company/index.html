What is the purpose of the article?

This article will walk you through the steps you will need to take in order to delete a company. Whilst the company will not be permanently deleted from your chamber, it will no longer be an 'active' company. If a company has been deleted in error, or if a company needs to become 'active' again within your chamber, this article will also explain how to restore a deleted company.

  • Deleting a Company
  • Restoring a Company

Deleting a Company

To delete a company, you will first need to find the relevant company by navigating Start Page > Find Company. (More information on how to find a company can be found here: Finding a Company).

Once you have navigated to the company page, at the top left, underneath the breadcrumb trail, you will find the 'Delete' button.


Once you have clicked on the 'Delete' button the following pop up will appear asking you to confirm you wish to delete the company:


Click 'Yes' to confirm you would like to change the status of the company to 'Deleted'.

The status of the company will now be changed to 'Deleted'.

A company cannot be permanently deleted. By deleting a company you are just changing the status of the company.

Restoring a Company

If you have deleted a company in error, or you need to change the status of the company back to active this can be done by following these steps.

You will first need to find the company by using the 'Find Company' function. Start Page > Find Company.

When searching for a company to restore, you will need to ensure the 'Status' field is set to 'Deleted'.


Once you have selected the relevant company, the 'Restore' button can be found at the top left of the screen, underneath the breadcrumb trail.


Once you have clicked on this, the company's status will be changed back to 'Active'.
