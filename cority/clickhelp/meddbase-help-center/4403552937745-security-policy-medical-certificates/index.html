What is the context?

Security Policies govern different aspects of data security in Meddbase. Each policy type (or certificate) governs a set of user permissions.

These permissions determine if or how users will be able to interact with data protected by a given policy or certificate, e.g. Company record protected by a Company Policy; Patient record protected by a Patient Policy.

Many permissions need to be used in conjunction with permissions governed by other certificates e.g. Company Certificate(s) or Document Certificate(s).

What is the purpose of the article?

This article provides a detailed description of user permissions governed by the Medical Certificate(s)*.

*A Medical Certificate can be assigned to an Appointment Type, under Admin > Appointment Admin > Security > Medical Policy Applied, although this is not mandatory.

Meddbase provides the ability to split patient medical record (e.g. OH vs GP) by means of assigning separate Medical Certificates to different Appointment Types, thus protecting the clinical notes, documents etc. captured/created during the respective appointment type. If Medical Certificate(s) are in use, a Meddbase user will have access to the part of the patient's medical record according to their permissions on the respective certificate(s).

Permissions

Permission

Description

View Patient Demographic

Allows users to view Patient Demographic in the Medical Context(s)* related to this Medical Certificate.

(Please note - this permission does not interfere with the View Patient Demographic permission on the Patient Certificate, which governs the ability to view patient demographic data outside a medical context).

View Documents*

Adds users' ability to visit documents associated with the patient within the context of this Medical Certificate.

(Please note - interacting with individual documents requires permissions on the Document Policy assigned to respective documents e.g. View document - to view document list; Download document - to see document preview etc.)

Modify Documents*

Adds users' ability to modify documents associated with the patient within  the context of this Medical Certificate.

(Please note - interacting with individual documents requires permissions on the 

Document Policy

 assigned to respective documents e.g. View document - to view document list; Download document - to see document preview etc.)

View Patient Medical Record

Adds users' ability to view Patient Medical record in the Medical Context(s) related to this Medical Certificate.

Modify Patient Medical Record

Adds users' ability to modify Patient Medical record in the Medical Context(s) related to this Medical Certificate.

 

*A Medical Context groups all medical data, incl. clinical notes, documents, referrals, Pre-placement Questionnaires, Referrals and more, recorded/generated under a particular Medical Certificate under a specific situation.
