What is the purpose of the context?

Meddbase can automatically send email and/or SMS messages. These are send in various scenarios and for a host of reasons.

Click here for more details on SMS communication

Click here for more details on email & SMS communication in Occupational Health

What is the purpose of the article?

This article describes email and SMS messages sent from Meddbase, the trigger, configuration and other details.

Task Checker

Appointment Confirmation email

(Non-Telemedicine)

Generated and sent when an appointment is booked with 'Telemedicine' untickedAppointment saved on the Appointment Setup page as part of a standard booking processAppointment booked confirmation

Either in:

  • Admin > Configuration > Task Checker > Appointment Confirmation > Patient > Email box checked > Email Message (generic)

Or

  • Admin > Configuration > Appointment Admin > Patient confirmation > Confirmation email message (appointment type specific; overrides generic message)

 

 

Yes, via either:

  • (Chamber) 'Email' checkbox unticked in Admin > Configuration > Task Checker > Patient > Email
  • (Per Patient) Patient not giving consent for this contact type
  • (Per Appointment Type) 'Prevent confirmation email' checkbox ticked in Admin > Appointment Admin > New/Edit > Patient confirmation

Patient Portal
Patient portal validation email

Generated and sent when a patient is added to an online Charge Band or when a Meddbase user reset's a patient's portal login

  • Patient added to an 'Online Enabled' Charge Band with the option to 'Allow And Notify' selected.

Or

  • 'Reset Portal Login' clicked on Patient Record > Patient Portal 

 

N/AAdmin > Configuration > Online Portal > Patient Portal templates > Registration email template

Yes. This email will not be sent when the patient's Charge Band:

  • Has not been 'Enabled for Online Booking'
  • Has been 'Enabled for Online Booking' and 'Allow With No Notifications' has been selected.

Referral portal notification email

Generated and sent when there is a change of state of a referral the patient is a manager of.

  • A new message is added to a referral (via the OH portal or in Meddbase)
  • A new reply is added to a message on the referral (via the OH Portal)
  • A referral is rejected / discharged
Referral confirmation   Admin > Configuration > Online Portal > Occupational Health Portal  templates > Notification email template

Yes, the email will not be sent if the patient has not given consent to this type of communication.

  • A referral is rejected / discharged

Online payments 

Online payment confirmation emailGenerated and sent when a patient makes a payment on the Patient Portal or via a payment link.

Patient makes a payment either:

  • Immediately following an appointment booking on the portal
  • From an existing invoice on the portal
  • From a link included in a manual email sent from an invoice in Meddbase 
N/AAdmin > Configuration > Online Portal > Online payments > Payment confirmation email enabled > Confirmation email template

Yes:

  • (Chamber) 'Enable' checkbox unticked in Admin > Configuration > Online Portal > Online payments > Payment confirmation email > Enable
Online payment failed emailGenerated and sent when a recurring online payment fails. 

A recurring payment method is required when a patient is on a Charge Band with a Membership Scheme set up. The recurring payment can be set up in Meddbase vie the patient Record, or by the patient via the Patient Portal.

This email is sent when the payment method fails

N/AAdmin > Configuration > Online Portal > Online payments > Recurring payment failed email enabled > Online payment failed email template

Yes:

  • (Chamber) 'Enable' checkbox unticked in Admin > Configuration > Online Portal > Online payments > Recurring payment failed email > Enable
