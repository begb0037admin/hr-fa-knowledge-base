What is the article's purpose?

  • This article includes e-mail and SMS notifications information, how these notifications are triggered and portal notification information.
  • This article outlines where certain e-mail and SMS notifications for occupational health are setup. It also outlines how the e-mail and SMS notifications are triggered and who receives them.
  • The expected outcome of the article is to instruct the user where to set up notifications and show how they are triggered.

What fields in Meddbase does SMS and Email communication go to?

Managers: All notifications from the referral portal will be sent to the managers Mobile numbers - think of the Work Phone number as an additional field. The system will not read the Work Phone number if the Mobile Number field is empty, so it is vital to that they have a Mobile number registered. 

Employees: Appointment confirmation emails go to the employee's Email field address, not Work Email field.

Portal Notifications

The following actions will trigger a notification in the Occupational Health Portal. Based on the manager's contact options, they will also receive an email/SMS.

  • Referral discharged/rejected (the notification text is generated based on the referral state, e.g.: Referral has been released but the patient refused the medical report
  • A shared collaboration message/reply posted
  • SLA notifications: appointment was not booked/placed within SLA, contact protocol was not followed within the SLA, medical report was not finished within the SLA,
  • A shared document has been deleted/restored
  • An employee download the OH report
  • A referral has been reallocated
