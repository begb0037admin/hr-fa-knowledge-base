What is the context?

The Medication Delivery and Collection feature in Meddbase allows clinicians to make, sign, and send E-Prescriptions electronically to SignatureRx, an online pharmacy that dispenses Meddbase prescriptions and delivers them to patients. In addition to delivery, following recent updates, medication can now also be collected from a local pharmacy should they prefer this to delivery.

The Medication Delivery and Collection feature is fully compliant with the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 providing a legal, safe, and efficient way to prescribe and deliver medication straight to a patient in need, mitigating the need for administratively heavy or paper processes.

The Medication Delivery and Collection feature is an update to the existing Medication Delivery capabilities. With this latest update, patients can choose to have their medication delivered to them as well as choosing to collect medication from their nearest pharmacy.

WARNING
This feature is only available to those in the UK and NorthernIreland.

Which locations are eligible for the Medication Delivery and Collection service?

Next day delivery is available across the United Kingdom and including the Channel Islands and Northern Ireland.

Can medication be delivered to another address other than the patient’s home address?

Signature Pharmacy will deliver the medication to the patient’s chosen address anywhere in the UK.

Meddbase Functionality

ItemQuestionAnswer
1.

How can I add a new clinician to use the Medication Delivery feature?

First, ensure the user has been added as a clinician within Meddbase with all relevant clinical fields completed, including the clinician’s professional registration number.

Secondly, the clinician will need to have their security permission set to allow them to prescribe.

Finally, request their Medication Delivery account set up within Meddbase by contacting our support team.

2.

How do I send a prescription via the Medication Delivery feature?

Sending a prescription by the Medication Delivery feature is quick and easy. For a short video on how to do this click here.

3.

Can a prescription be cancelled or amended after signing?

Once a prescription is electronically signed by an appropriate clinician it cannot be cancelled within Meddbase.

Signature Pharmacy should be contacted as soon as possible if a prescription needs to be cancelled or amended:-

  • Tel: 0330 111 0440,
  • Email: support@signaturerx.co.uk
  • Website: https://help.signaturerx.co.uk/en/
4.

Who can prescribe via the Medication Delivery feature?

Any clinician set up with a clinician account in Meddbase and a Medication Delivery account can prescribe via the feature.

This includes all legal UK prescribers including doctors and independent prescribers.

5.

How can we see the status of a prescription?

Once a prescription is e-signed this will show against the prescription status in Meddbase. For further information regarding dispensing and delivery Signature Pharmacy should be contacted via:-

  • Tel: 0330 111 0440,
  • Email: support@signaturerx.co.uk
  • Website:https://help.signaturerx.co.uk/en/
6.

Can you prescribe multiple items on one prescription and have them delivered?

Yes, multiple items can be prescribed and delivered as part of a single prescription.

7.

Can you process repeat prescriptions via the Medication Delivery feature?

Yes, repeat prescriptions can be e-signed and delivered using the Medication Delivery feature.

8.

What are the Human Medicines Regulations 2012?

 

The Human Medicines Regulations 2012 govern the authorisation, sale and supply of medical products for human use.

The Medication Delivery feature is fully compliant with this legislation meaning prescriptions can be electronically signed and delivered using the feature.

9.

How does Medication Delivery differ from other e-prescription offerings?

 

The Medication Delivery feature provides a fully legal means to electronically prescribe medication safely and securely in compliance with legislative requirements.

The feature is built into the existing Meddbase system meaning there is no need for transcribing of medication, or additional workflow steps, which would introduce clinical risk and inefficiency. Medication Delivery removes the need for pen, paper, fax machines or emails.

10.

How often do prescribers need to authenticate with their Medication Delivery logins to sign prescriptions?

 

Prescribers will need to authenticate using their Medication Delivery logins once per Meddbase session. 

11.

How long does a user stay authenticated after logging into the Prescription Authentication UI?

If the user is active, users will only need to authenticate once per Meddbase user session.

If the user is idle, the user will stay authenticated for the time configured for 'Screensaver timeout' in Admin > Configuration +120 mins.

Manually logging out of Meddbase and logging back in again requires the user to re-authenticate via the Prescription Authentication UI.

 

Patient FAQs

Pharmacy Service Provision

ItemQuestionAnswer
1.

How will my medication be delivered?

Your medication will be delivered either using DPD or Royal Mail for nationwide deliveries and in-house couriers for same-day deliveries. You can discuss the delivery options available and choose the most convenient option directly with Signature Pharmacy.

Delivery is free of charge and you can rest assured that all mediation is discreetly packaged.

2.

How will I receive my prescription?

You will not need to receive the prescription as it will be sent directly from the clinician to Signature Pharmacy for dispensing and dispatch.

3.

Will I receive a paper prescription?

No, the service is totally paperless, maximising the security of your data and minimising hassle as well as environmental impact.

4.

Can I cancel my prescription?

To cancel your prescription, contact Signature Pharmacy via:-

  • Tel: 0330 111 0440,
  • Email: support@signaturerx.co.uk
  • Website: https://help.signaturerx.co.uk/en/

If you do not wish to proceed with medication delivery, you can simply ignore the payment request and your prescription order will not be fulfilled. You would need to contact the prescriber if you want to make alternative arrangements for your prescription.

5.

Can my prescriptions be left with my neighbour if I’m out?

Yes, when your item is dispatched, you can contact the delivery provider and arrange for the medication to be left at your neighbours/a safe place.

6.

How much will medication and delivery cost?

The cost of private prescriptions varies depending on the cost of the medication prescribed. Private prescriptions are not subsidised by the NHS and are chargeable at all pharmacies.

A competitive price is guaranteed throughout partner pharmacy Signature Pharmacy. Regardless of same-day or next-day delivery preference, prescription delivery is free of charge.

7.

How do I pay for my prescription?

Once your prescription is signed by the prescriber, Signature Pharmacy will contact you to arrange payment and confirm your delivery. 

You will receive a link by email and SMS, usually within two working hours. Through this link you can pay for your prescription, choose the delivery method and your preferred delivery address  

8.

How will I get updates on my prescription?

Once your prescription is signed by the prescriber, you will receive an email confirming the prescription has been sent to Signature Pharmacy (subject to your consent to receiving emails from your healthcare provider)

Signature Pharmacy will then contact you to arrange payment and confirm your delivery.

9.

How will I receive updates regarding delivery?

Once payment is received by our partner pharmacy Signature Pharmacy you will receive regular updates by a tracked courier service.

10.

How confidential is the service?

Signature Pharmacy is a fully registered and compliant Pharmacy with the General Pharmaceutical Council. Your data is kept securely within our electronic healthcare record system and accessed by Signature Pharmacy meaning that your data is handled by clinicians on a need-to-know basis.

In addition, your medication will be sent via secure courier in discreet packaging maintaining confidentiality throughout.

11.

Can I have my electronic prescription filled by another pharmacy?

No, but you can ask your clinician for a paper prescription which you can take to the pharmacy of your choice.

12.

Who do I contact if I have an issue with my prescription?

If your query is regarding a dispatched medication please contact the courier service. Otherwise please contact Signature Pharmacy who will be happy to help you with your query.
