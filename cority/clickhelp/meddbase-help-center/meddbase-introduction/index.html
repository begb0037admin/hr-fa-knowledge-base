Welcome to Meddbase

Meddbase is a comprehensive, cloud-based platform that supports the full spectrum of healthcare service delivery. Built on the Meddbase system, the engine brings together Electronic Health Records (EHR/EMR), Occupational Health (EHS), Patient Administration (PAS), and Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) functionality in a single, configurable environment.

Developed with healthcare professionals in mind, Meddbase helps organisations streamline, standardise, and securely manage clinical, administrative, and financial data across their services. The platform is designed to support complex workflows while remaining intuitive and flexible for end users.

Core Functional Areas

Meddbase spans the following key operational areas:

  • Occupational Health – Manage referrals, assessments, surveillance programs, and case reviews
  • Electronic Medical Records (EMR) – Capture and manage detailed clinical histories and documentation
  • Patient Administration (PAS) – Oversee scheduling, patient communications, and document workflows
  • Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) – Handle billing, invoicing, payment tracking, and cost management

Each of these areas is supported by configurable modules that allow for the collection, tracking, and reporting of data in real time. With powerful reporting and permissions tools, users can access exactly what they need, when they need it—within a secure and compliant framework.

Designed for Integration and Flexibility

Whether implemented as a fully integrated solution or as standalone functional areas, the Engine is adaptable to your organisational needs. It enables you to:

  • Connect teams across clinical, operational, and support functions
  • Automate standard processes using customisable business rules
  • Improve communication through integrated alerts, messages, and workflows
  • Monitor performance and compliance with real-time reporting tools
  • Configure roles and permissions to align with governance and access requirements

Meddbase also supports integration with third-party services and clinical systems, ensuring it works seamlessly within a broader technology ecosystem.
