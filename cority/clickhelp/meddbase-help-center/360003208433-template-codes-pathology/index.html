What is the purpose of the article?

This article outlines the different codes that can be used to include Pathology information in templates.

List of template codes for Pathology and a related description

Below is a list of template codes for Pathology and an associated description for each item.

CodeDescription
{Person.X}Refers to the patient the lab request is for; X can be replaced by any codes relating to patients, see the relevant article on Patient Template Codes
{Doctor.X}Refers to the medical person making the pathology request; X can be replaced by any codes relating to medical people, see the relevant article on Medical Person Template Codes
{OurReferenceCode}The unique reference code required by the lab
{OurReferenceBarcode}Reference code in barcode format
{RequestedTests}Tests being requested
{Request.Notes}Notes written by the requester at the time of requesting pathology
{Fields.copy_to_title}Title of the person who results should be copied to
{Fields.copy_to_name}Name of the person who results should be copied to
{Fields.copy_to_address}Address of the person who results should be copied to
{Fields.copy_to_telephone}Telephone of the person who results should be copied to
{Fields.account_to}Check box for who the lab fee should go to
{Fields.clinical_details}Pulls through any written clinic details
{Fields.fasting}Check box for indicating whether the patient is fasting
{Fields.pregnant}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient is pregnant
{Fields.postmen}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient is postmenopause
{Fields.irregbleed}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient has irregular bleeding
{Fields.suscerv}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient has a suspicious cervix
{Fields.hysterectomy}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient has had hysterectomy
{Fields.iucd}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient has an IUCD
{Fields.hrt}Checkbox for indicating whether the patient has had HRT treatment
{Fields.ethnic}Patient's ethnicity
{Fields.drugtherapy}Details of patient's drug therapy
{ShortNow}Date of request
{Now}Time and date of the pathology request
{Appointment.Start}
Appointment date
{Appointment.Time}
Appointment time
{RequestDate}
Request date of pathology order
{RequestTime}
Request time of pathology order

 

Template Codes for Pathology Lab Result Templates

 

CodeDescription
{Person.X}Refers to the patient the lab result is for; X can be replaced by any codes relating to patients, see the relevant article on Patient Template Codes
{Doctor.X}Refers to the medical person making the pathology request; X can be replaced by any codes relating to medical people, see the relevant article on Medical Person Template Codes
{ShortResponseDate}Date of receiving pathology lab result
{ShortResponseTime}Time of receiving pathology lab result
{HL7.First.ORC.3}Lab order number
{Now}Time and date of the pathology request
{Request.Notes}Displays any notes written by the requester at the time of requesting pathology
{Results} Table of pathology results
{HL7.First.PID.8}Displays the gender as when the request was made.

 

Additional Information

 

CodeDescription
