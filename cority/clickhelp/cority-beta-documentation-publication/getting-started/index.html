Cority Early Access

Early Access features are pieces of functionality that are mostly complete but have some remaining development. When these features are made accessible in an environment, they can be evaluated more effectively for suitability across a wider range of workflows. During early access to these features, we encourage you to provide feedback.

When using an Early Access feature, keep in mind the following: 

  • They may not be feature complete.
  • They may not be fully supported throughout Cority One. 
  • They may have known performance or quality issues.

Feedback and support

Please reach out to Cority Support with feedback on early access features or for support using the feature.
