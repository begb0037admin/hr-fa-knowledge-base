View Notification Policies

Notification Policies define the rules for workflow notifications. The notifications are associated with one or more designated Roles as recipients. For instance, a single policy might include two notification schedules: one specifying that the Assignee be notified when a step is assigned, and another specifying that the Supervisor be notified when the step is completed.

A Notification Policy is associated with a Notification Template which define the format of the messages.

To view notification policies:

  1. From the menu, select Setup > Policies under Workflows.

    The existing policies defined in the system are listed. The Roles referenced by the policy and associated workflow types are shown.

  2. Click the policy name, or right click and choose View to see the policy definition.

    Each notification schedule defined in the policy is listed. The Recipient role associated with the notification and the delivery method are also shown.

  3. To view a notification schedule, click anywhere in the row for that schedule.

    The notification properties are displayed for viewing.

  4. Click Back to return to the list of policies.
