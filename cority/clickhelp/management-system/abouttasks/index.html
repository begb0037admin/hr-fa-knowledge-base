About Tasks

Tasks prompt and monitor compliance with regulatory requirements. Usually you will receive a task notification by email to remind you when your tasks are due. A link in the email will take you directly to the task completion page or to the TaskApp for even faster completion.

To access Task Manager:

  1. Select Tasks and Workflows from the menu.

  2. Select Tasks from the pop-out menu to the right, or from the Tasks and Workflows page.

You can keep track of your tasks in several ways:

  • You can view your upcoming task assignments from your Advanced Calculations (EMS) dashboard.
  • You can get an overview of daily, weekly or monthly tasks from the task calendar.
