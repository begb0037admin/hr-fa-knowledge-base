Publish Quick Links

To make system Quick Links visible to users, they must be published to users or groups, or be designated system-wide. Users may use any of the system Quick Links that have been shared to them on a Shared Quick Links panel on their personal dashboards. The links will also be added to the Quick Links menu at the top of the page.

To publish system Quick Links:

  1. From the menu, select Setup > QuickLinks.
  2. Right click a quick link and choose Publish.
  3. Click Add to add users or groups.
  4. Select the users or groups to whom you want to publish the quick link from the selection window. Or, to publish to all users, check the box Publish System-Wide.
  5. Click Save.

When system Quick Links are published to specific users or groups, they become available for users to add to their personal dashboards through the Shared Quick Links panel.

Shared Quick Links (both system wide and specific) will also appear automatically on the Quick Links dropdown menu at the top of the page for users.

Changes to the Quick Links menu at the top of the page will appear in the menu only after a page refresh or new login session.

When using system Quick Links on a Shared Quick Links panel on a distributed dashboard ensure that users also have permission to execute the link action (i.e., enter data or create a workflow) and have permissions to any related objects.
