Materials

Materials for use in Material Activity calculations are defined by their chemical components and their concentrations and by a set of optional value-based and formula-based properties.

Material components include:

  • Chemicals: A material must include at least one chemical with defined concentrations.
  • Material Value Based Properties: A material may include one or more value-based properties. These are numeric or Boolean fields that are properties of the material that may be used in calculations.
  • Material Formula Based Properties: Formula based properties are properties that may be expressed as formulae and calculated according to the material. Formula based properties are of three types: Primary, Secondary and Tertiary. The difference in the types is dependent on when they may be calculated.

Optionally, Material/Activity Templates may be applied to materials to provide custom fields for informational purposes only.

Materials are created as needed per system. No standard materials are provided.
