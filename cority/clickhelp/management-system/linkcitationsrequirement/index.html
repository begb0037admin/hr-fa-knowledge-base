Citation Associations

Citations may be associated with requirements singly or in bulk. To associate a citation with a requirement, you must have Modify permissions on both the citation and the requirement.

To associate citations with requirements:

  1. In the Citations library, find the citations you want to associate.

    Select one or more citations by checking the boxes, then right click and choose Associate. Or, to select multiple citations, check the desired citations, then right click and choose Associate.

    If any links to the citation already exist, the objects currently associated are shown in the grid. In the case of multiple citations, any objects they have in common are shown.

  2. To add object associations, click Add Association.

    The selected citations are shown in the first box. You can add objects you want to associate to the second box.

  3. Click Add to find the objects you want to associate.

  4. In the selection window, browse or use search to find the objects you want to associate. Select the checkboxes and click Select Objects.

    The selected objects are shown in the box on the bottom. You may add additional objects to the selection box, or just save.

  5. Click Save to save associations.

To add/change citation associations with requirements:

  1. In the Applicable Requirements view, find the requirement(s) you want to associate with a citation.

    Use the Requirements Search, if necessary, to find objects under different parent objects.

  2. Select the checkboxes for the requirement(s), right click and choose Properties > Edit.

  3. On the properties page, scroll to the bottom of the form to the Citations section.

    If you have selected multiple requirements, the common properties are shown. Check the Citations box in the Relations section to enable editing.

  4. Click Add in the Citations box.

  5. In the citations selection window, browse the citation folders, or search to find the citation(s) you want.

  6. Select the citation(s) to associate and click Associate.

    You may choose the default (latest) version or a specific version to associate.

    To associate with a different version of a citation, click the plus sign (+) next to the citation to view versions. Then select the version you want.

    The selected citations appear in the Citations box. The version associated is noted.

  7. Click Save.
