Chemical Properties

Chemicals are defined by their name and CAS number. In addition, each chemical has the following set of numeric properties, which may be accessed by calculations:

  • Molecular weight: Molecular weight of the chemical. A non-negative decimal value. If unspecified, the default is 0.
  • Antoine A: Value of the Antoine A constant for calculating vapor pressure with temperature in Centigrade.
  • Antoine B: Value of the Antoine B constant for calculating vapor pressure with temperature in Centigrade.
  • Antoine C: Value of the Antoine C constant for calculating vapor pressure with temperature in Centigrade.
  • Antoine A (K): Value of the Antoine A constant for calculating vapor pressure with temperature in Kelvin.
  • Antoine B (K): Value of the Antoine B constant for calculating vapor pressure with temperature in Kelvin.
  • Vapor pressure at 60F: Vapor pressure of the chemical at 60 deg Fahrenheit.
  • Density at 60F: Density of the chemical at 60 deg Fahrenheit. A non-negative decimal value.
  • DIPPR A ... DIPPR G: Value of the constants A through G for calculating vapor pressure by the DIPPR method.

Regulatory characteristics are represented by a set of Boolean (true/false) properties, which may also be accessed by calculations. The Boolean properties for chemicals in the system are:

  • CERCLAHaz: Is classified as a hazardous chemical under the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) (commonly known as Superfund).
  • RCRAHaz: Is classified as a hazardous chemical under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA).
  • RCRA SVOC: Is classified as an SVOC under the RCRA.
  • RCRA VOC: Is classified as a VOC under the RCRA.
  • CAA VOC: Is classified as a VOC under the Clean Air Act.
  • CAA112r: Is on the list of regulated substances under Section 112(r) of the Clean Air Act.
  • Diisocyanate: Is a diisocyanate chemical.
  • Dioxin: Is a dioxin or dioxin-like chemical.
  • PBT: Is listed as a Persistent Bioaccumulative Toxic (PBT) chemical.
  • PAC: Is on the Protective Action Criteria (PAC) list.
  • EHS: Is listed as an Extremely Hazardous Substance.
  • TRI: Is on the Toxic Release Inventory reporting list.
  • HAP: Is listed as Hazardous Air Pollutant (HAP).
  • Solid: Is a solid.
  • Non-Evaporative: Is non-evaporative
  • Air Toxic Emission Inventory: Is listed on the Air Toxic Emission Inventory.

The properties of the standard chemicals supplied by the software, which are not system-specific, may not be edited. System-specific custom chemicals may be created and edited. When a custom chemical has the same name and CAS number as a standard chemical, the custom chemical will be used whenever it is referenced.
