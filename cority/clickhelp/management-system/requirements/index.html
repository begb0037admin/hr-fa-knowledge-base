Requirements

Requirements are the compliance objects that are set up in the system to allow monitoring and tracking of compliance obligations. The system allows two broad categories of compliance requirements:

  • Non-numeric Requirements: These represent obligations such as operating requirements, equipment standards, and reporting requirements.

    Non-numeric data are the records that document compliance with non-numeric requirements. The data includes the data collector, compliance period (start and end dates), compliance status, date on which the compliance action was performed, and compliance comments.

  • Numeric Requirements: These represent the numeric factors needed to document compliance (includes parameters, variables, and various types of calculations)

    Numeric data are the numeric values of the parameters and variables that are tracked. For example, Title V permits typically contain annual emission limits on a variety of criteria and non-criteria pollutants. Annual emissions data collected or calculated in response to such limits constitute numeric data. Various types of numeric data may be stored in the system for calculation purposes.

The authority and driver information for requirements is stored on Requirement Templates. Each requirement object is associated with a corresponding Requirement Template. The use of Requirement Templates allows reports to be easily generated for all requirements associated with a particular authority/driver template, such as all requirements associated with a particular operating permit.
