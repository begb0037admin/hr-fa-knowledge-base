Workflow Notifications

Workflow notifications are sent out automatically by the system according to the policies for the Workflow Type.

When you receive a workflow notification via email, click the link in the email to sign into the system, view the workflow, and complete the step assignment.

To view the workflow assignment:

  1. Select the link to the workflow in the email notification. The format of the email may be customized for each workflow type.

    The sign in page opens in your web browser.

  2. Sign into the system with your user name and password.

    Once you sign in, the workflow step completion form is displayed in edit mode if you are an assignee to the workflow step. If you received the email for notification purposes only, the Workflow Overview is displayed. You will be able to see the current workflow status and view the progress of the workflow.

For security reasons, if you are already logged into the system, you will be required to sign in again to validate your credentials.
