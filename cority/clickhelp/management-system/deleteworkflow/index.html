Delete a Workflow

To delete a workflow, you must have Delete permission for the workflow, granted through any role you are a member of in the workflow, or have Manage Workflows right.

Deleted workflows cannot be retrieved, and all history of the workflow will be lost.

To delete a workflow:

  1. From the Workflows page (Tasks and Workflows page), select one or more workflows, then right click and choose Delete.

    Or, from the Workflow Overview of a single workflow, click the Delete link at the top of the page.

  2. Click Delete again to confirm.

    The workflow is deleted. If there are any child workflows, they will also be deleted.

To delete one or more child workflows from the Workflow Overview:

  1. In the Child Workflows section on the Workflow Overview, select the checkbox for one or more child workflows you want to delete, then click the Delete link.
  2. Click Delete again to confirm.
