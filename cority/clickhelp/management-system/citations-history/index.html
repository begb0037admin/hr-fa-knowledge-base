Citation History

Whenever a citation is edited, a new version is automatically created. A history of all versions of a citation is maintained.

To view history and versions of a citation:

  • Right click the citation in the Citations library and choose Versions.

    All versions of the citation are displayed.

  • To view a specific version, right click the version and choose Open.

Revert to Previous Citation Version

You may revert to a previous version of a citation and make that the active (or "latest") version.

 You must have Modify permissions on the citation in order to control versions.

To revert to a previous version of a citation:

  1. Right click the version and choose Revert to Version.

    A copy of the citation version will be made and it will become the latest version of reference. All links to "Latest" version will automatically point to this version. Any links made to a specific version will not be changed.
