System Quick Links

System Quick Links are quick links that may be created and managed by an administrator and shared with users to create easy one-button access to common functions or objects with pre-selected filters. With Quick Links you can:

  • Create custom Quick Links menus for different users or groups.
  • Create a collection of role-specific links to place on a system dashboard to distribute to selected user groups.
  • Give users pre-configured links to use as they wish on their personal dashboards.
  • Integrate your system with other sites and resources.

The Manage Quick Links is required to set up system Quick Links. System Quick Links are then published to selected users or groups. Users will see only those links that have been published to them, either directly, via a group, or system-wide.

Quick Links can be internal or external:

  • Internal Quick Links, or "smart links," are used to access objects and functions within the system, with pre-selected filter criteria and actions. Examples are:
    • Link to a data entry form for a pre-selected data set.
    • Link to a log entry form for an Event Log.
    • Link to a cached report.
    • Link to a pre-configured search for specific objects in the system (i.e., a facility, POI, or group of requirements).
  • External Quick Links allow access from Advanced Calculations (EMS) to other resources or web sites, such as:
    • Quick Data
    • Waste Management
    • A company intranet
    • Regulatory agencies
    • Other management systems or related resources
