Material/Activity Fields

Material Activity fields are managed from Material/Activity Field Library. Material Activity fields include the following field types:

 To view Material/Activity Fields, users must have the Manage Materials and Activities user right.

Value Based Material

Constants (numeric or Boolean) that are properties of the material and which may be used in calculations related to a material.

Value Based Activity

Constant (numeric or Boolean) that are properties of the activity and which may used in calculations.

Specific Use Variables

The variable for which data is input and which triggers the calculation, such as the amount of material used.

Formula Based Primary

Material-specific calculations that may be calculated based on properties of the material alone (not dependent on how it is used)

Formula Based Secondary

Material-specific calculations that are dependent on where or how the material is used, and may be calculated once it is known where the material is used. A secondary formula based property may require variables stored on a calculation template applied to a parent object, or meteorological data dependent on the location identified for the parent object.

Formula Based Tertiary

Material-specific calculations that are dependent on factors that may only be available on data entry; thus may only be calculated upon data entry.

Chemical Formula Based Primary

Formula Based Primary calculations for chemical calculations that automatically create values for each chemical in the material.

Chemical Formula Based Secondary

Formula Based Secondary calculations for chemical calculations that automatically create values for each chemical in the material.

Chemical Formula Based Tertiary

Formula Based Tertiary calculations for chemical calculations that automatically create values for each chemical in the material.
