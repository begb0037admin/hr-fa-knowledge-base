Advanced Calculations (EMS) Management System

The Advanced Calculations (EMS) Management System can be used to track all your Environmental and Health & Safety compliance obligations, including air and water, permit management, greenhouse gas emissions, safety and incident management and more. The Advanced Calculations (EMS) cloud database is custom modeled to represent your own facilities, departments, and/or compliance programs.

The User Guide section of this online help contains how-to information on using the management system. For help with Advanced Calculations (EMS) Apps, see Apps.

The Admin Guide section gives detailed information for administrators and advanced users on system configuration, maintenance and the underlying components of the system.

The help link icon on each management system page will take you to a specific help topic for that page.
