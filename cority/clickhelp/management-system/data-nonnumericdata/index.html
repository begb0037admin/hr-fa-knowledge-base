Non-Numeric Data

Non-numeric requirements represent the tasks, operating procedures, and equipment standards to which a company is subject, such as periodic inspections, sampling procedures, or requirement for secondary containment.

Data associated with non-numeric requirements includes:

  • Compliance data entered manually.
  • Completion data for tasks associated with the requirement.
