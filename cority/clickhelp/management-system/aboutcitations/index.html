About Citations

Citations provide a way to keep track of the code regulations a facility is subject to and the specific requirements to which they apply. In Advanced Calculations (EMS), a Citation may represent a regulatory citation, permit condition, or environmental order. Citations are designed to expand on the information contained in Requirement Templates.

For example, each condition in a permit can be represented as a Citation, with the Description containing the full text. The Citation may then associated with Requirements and Event Logs.

Additional functionality for Citations include:

  • Citation fields can be used in searching for objects. For example, you can find all objects of a specific type related to a specific citation.
  • Citation fields can be used in reports for filtering objects and in report output just like other object attributes.

Citations have a many-to-many relationship with Requirements. That is to say, one Citation may relate to multiple Requirements, and one Requirement may relate to multiple Citations.
