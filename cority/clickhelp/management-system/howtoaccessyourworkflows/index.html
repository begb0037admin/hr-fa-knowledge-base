How to Access Workflows

You can access your workflow assignments in any of the following ways:

Email: Workflow assignments and workflow-related notifications are customarily sent to you via email. You can access a workflow assignment by clicking on a link in the email notification. After you sign into the system, the current step completion form will be displayed.

Workflows Panel on Dashboard: The Workflows Panel on the your Desktop (or other dashboard) shows your current workflow assignments. Click the Current Step link to go to the step completion form, or click the workflow name to view the Workflow Overview first.

Inbox: Workflow notifications may also delivered to your Inbox in the Messages page. Click the link to go to the current workflow step. See Inbox.

Tasks and Workflows page: Select Workflows from the Tasks and Workflows page to view and search for workflows by various criteria. See View and Search Workflows .

Calendar: The calendar in the Calendar page shows your daily task and workflow assignments, in daily, weekly or monthly format. An abbreviated form of the calendar may also be shown as a Calendar panel on your dashboard. See Task and Workflow Calendars and Calendar Panel.

Object Calendar: View all tasks and workflows related to a facility, program or piece of equipment from the right-click menu of the object on the system model tree. See Task and Workflow Calendars.
