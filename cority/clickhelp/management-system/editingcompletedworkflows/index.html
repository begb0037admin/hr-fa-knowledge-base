Editing Completed Workflows

Once a workflow is completed, it may not be edited unless it is reopened. However, you may add comments to a completed workflow without reopening it.

To add comments, you must have Add Comments permission for the workflow, granted through any role you are a member of in the workflow, or have Manage Workflows right.

To add comments to a completed workflow:

  1. Open the Workflow Overview from the Workflows page (Tasks & Workflows page).
  2. Select the Comments, History and Documents tab.
  3. Click the Add Comment link at the top of the page.
  4. Enter your comment, then select Save.
