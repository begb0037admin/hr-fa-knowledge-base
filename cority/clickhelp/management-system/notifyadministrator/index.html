Notify Administrator

The Notify link can be used to send an email to your system administrator when you have a problem that requires administrative attention, such as missing objects or data, or inadequate permissions.

To notify your System Admin:

  1. From the main menu, click your profile and select Notify.

  2. In the Notify window, click Add User to add recipients.
    The user selection window shows those users who are Administrators in your system.

  3. Select the Admin user(s) to contact and click Select.

  4. For the notification type, select Inbox or Email (by default, both are selected).

  5. Click Send.
