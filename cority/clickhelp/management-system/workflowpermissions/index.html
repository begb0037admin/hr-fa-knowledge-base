Workflow Permissions

Roles and permissions work together to determine what users may do at any step in the workflow, or for general workflow operations.

Permissions are assigned to each Role in a Workflow Type. Permissions may be assigned at the Workflow level or the Step level.

Workflow Level Permissions define what actions the user can perform on the workflow as a whole. They include workflow operations, such as closing, scheduling, associating objects and documents, and assigning users (other than the default users associated with each Role).

Field Permissions include the rights to view or edit fields on the forms associated with each step. Field permissions may be granted at the step level for all fields on the form, or for each field individually, as needed (i.e., some fields may be view only, while others are editable). Permissions to all fields on all forms in the workflow may also be granted at the workflow level, which is equivalent to assigning the same permissions to all fields at the step level.

General Rules for Workflow Permissions:

  • When a user who is not a default member of a role is assigned to a workflow step, the user is mapped to the role and is automatically granted the permissions of that Role for that step.
  • A user who is assigned to any step on a workflow will have the combination of all the permissions granted to all of the Roles of which he is a member.
