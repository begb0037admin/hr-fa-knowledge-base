Pushing Advanced Calculations (EMS) aggregated data to Core (GX2) 

Core (GX2) has environmental permits as well as regulatory requirements and obligations. To confirm compliance, collection of data is required. Data and calculations collected in Advanced Calculations (EMS) can be pushed into Core (GX2) to ensure compliance is met by Core (GX2) standards.

If you are interested in this function, please contact your CSM or CAP program to initiate configuration and get further instructions.
