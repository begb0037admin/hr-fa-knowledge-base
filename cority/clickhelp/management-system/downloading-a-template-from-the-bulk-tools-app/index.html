Downloading a Template from the Bulk Tools App

To download a Template from the Bulk Tools App

  1. On the Bulk Tools window, click the Upload tab.
  2. Scroll through the Templates section and click the link to an excel template you would like to download.

Links to Instructions can be found in the first column. Links to excel templates can be found in the second column.

  1. Once the excel template is downloaded, fill in all applicable fields.
  2. Save the excel document on your desktop.
