Advanced Calculations (EMS) User Guide

The Advanced Calculations (EMS) platform allows you to consolidate and manage all your compliance-related data and activities across your organization. The system tracks emissions data as well as compliance-related tasks. It enables you to concurrently reference and manage detailed numeric data, giving you complete calculations, analysis, reporting and tracking capabilities.

With the Advanced Calculations (EMS) you can:

  • Streamline and monitor your compliance-related tasks and workflows.

    Create and assign tasks, set schedules, track progress, establish notification protocols, and collect specific information such as time and cost required to complete tasks. Automatic reminders and emails are generated based on the criteria you choose, so you can quickly respond to potential incidents.

    The engine tracks and monitors numeric data and automatically sends out data warnings when either regulatory or internal limits are exceeded so that prompt action can be taken.

  • Maintain audit, incident and inspection logs.

    The engine centralizes required record-keeping and logs, and maintains complete documentation of follow-up tasks and resolution.

  • Generate reports for compliance filings and for business analysis.

    The engine automatically builds, edits and generates local Title V compliance and deviation reports, NPDES Discharge Monitoring Reports, RCRA generator reports and more. You can also generate key performance metrics for a broad range of events and tasks.

  • Coordinate your documentation.

    Store, organize, share, and track multiple versions of compliance documents, such as operating procedures and safety documents, so they are available when and where they are needed.

Advanced Calculations (EMS) can be accessed from anywhere with just an internet browser or properly enabled Internet device.
