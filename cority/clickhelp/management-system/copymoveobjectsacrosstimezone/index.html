Copy/Move Objects Across Time Zones

The time zone is set on the Facility/Program object type. All child objects of a Facility (Unit, POI, Requirement, or Event Log) inherit the time zone of the parent Facility.

When you copy objects to a Facility with a different time zone, they automatically inherit the time zone of the new Facility. All date properties (active dates, script begin dates, custom field value histories, etc.) will have the same local date/time in the new time zone. For example, a calculation with a begin date of 1/1/yyyy 12:00 AM EST will have a begin date of 1/1/yyyy 12:00 AM PST when the requirement is copied to the US Eastern time zone.

When objects are moved, rather than copied, to a facility with a different time zone, all date properties are then adjusted for the new time zone. For example, a calculation with a begin date of 1/1/yyyy 12:00 AM in the US Pacific time zone, when moved to the US Eastern time zone will be 1/1/yyyy 3:00 AM. After moving objects, you should verify that the date/times are appropriate for all requirements.

 Moving requirements to a different time zone is not allowed if data exists for the requirements.
