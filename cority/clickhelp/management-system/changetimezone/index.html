Change Time Zone

The time zone is set on the Facility/Program object type. All child objects of a Facility (Unit, POI, Requirement, or Event Log) inherit the time zone of the parent Facility.

When you copy a Facility, you can change the time zone, if necessary. When the Facility time zone is changed, all date properties will have the same local date/time in the new time zone (i.e., script begin/end dates, custom field value history begin/end dates, etc.). For example, a calculation with a begin date of 1/31/yyyy 12:00 EST will have a begin date of 1/31/yyyy 12:00 PST when the time zone is change to PST.

The time zone may not be changed if the change will cause loss of data. Specifically:

  • If any of the following types of data exist under the facility, the time zone update will not be allowed: numeric data points, events (event instances), non-numeric data.
  • Changing from STD to DST time zone is not allowed because of potential data loss due to the time shift. However, the following time zone changes are allowed, since no potential data loss will occur: DST to STD, DST to DST, and STD to STD.

DST in this case means a time zone that adjusts from standard time to daylight saving over the year. STD is Standard Time Only.

To change a Facility time zone:

  1. Right click the Facility and choose Change > Time Zone.
  2. Select the new time zone from the dropdown list.
  3. Click Save, then Confirm.
