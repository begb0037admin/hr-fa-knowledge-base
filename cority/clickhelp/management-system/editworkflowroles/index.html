Edit Workflow Roles

You can edit a Role to add or change the default memberships or description. You can also copy or rename a Role in Workflow Roles Manager.

 Changing the default memberships from Role Manager will not affect the role memberships on associated workflow types.

  1. From the menu, select Setup > Roles under Workflows. 
  2. Do any of the following:
    1. To edit a role, right click the Role and choose Edit. Edit the fields as desired, then click Save and Confirm.
    2. To copy a Role, right click the Role and choose Copy. Enter a name for the new Role and click Save. 
    3. To rename a Role, right click the Role and choose Rename. Enter the new name and click Save.
