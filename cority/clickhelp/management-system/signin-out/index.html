Sign in and Sign out

To access your system model in Advanced Calculations (EMS), you must sign in with your personal user ID and password. Your administrator will set up a user ID and an initial password for you.

You can access the system in the following ways:

  • Navigate to the sign in page online with your web browser.
  • From an system email, such as a task or workflow notification, you may click the link in the email.

Access Advanced Calculations (EMS) 

To access Advanced Calculations (EMS) from your web browser:

  1. Navigate to the following URL:

    https://www.enviance.com/CustomerLogin/CustomerLogin.aspx

  2. Enter your user name and password.

    A user name and an initial password will be assigned to you by your system administrator.

  3. Select Sign In.

If this is the first time you have signed in with your user name, you will be required to set up your personal security questions first. Select any three security questions from the list and enter your answers. Click Submit to continue.

To sign in from an email task or workflow notification:

  1. Select the link at the bottom of the email to open the sign in page in your web browser.

  2. Enter your username and password and click Sign In.

    If authentication is successful, you will be taken directly to the task or workflow completion page.

For security purposes, if you are already signed into the system, clicking on the email link will require you to re-enter your credentials and sign in again.

Forgot Your Password

If you forget your password, you can use the Forgot my password option on the login page to reset your password.

An email will be sent to the email account specified in your profile with a temporary password. Click on the login link in the email. You will be required to answer one of your security questions before being directed to the Reset Password page to reset your password.

If the forgotten password feature is not enabled in your system, you will receive a message that you must contact your administrator.

Sign Out

For security reasons, you should always sign out of the system and close your web browser after completing your work. To ensure security, users are automatically signed out after a period of inactivity. The session timeout period is set by your system administrator.

To sign out:

  1. From the main menu, select Log Out.
  2. In the confirmation dialog, click OK to sign out now, or Cancel to remain logged in.

Sign In Problems

Some possible reasons why you may not be able to sign in are:

  • The user name and password do not match any existing account. Recheck your user name and password info and try again.
  • Your user account has expired or the account is locked. Contact your system administrator to resolve any problems.

  • Another session is already active with your account. Only one session is allowed per account at one time.

    In this case you will receive the following message: "Another session is currently active using your credentials. Would you like to end the session and log on?"

    If you choose Yes, the active user session will be terminated immediately and you will be signed in with a new session. If you choose No, you will be returned to the sign in page and may sign in under another user name, if desired.

This message may also appear if you were working in the system recently and did not sign out, or followed an email link with an active session in progress. For security reasons, you will need to sign in again.
