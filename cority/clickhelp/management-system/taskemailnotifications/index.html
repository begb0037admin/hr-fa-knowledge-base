Task Email Notifications

Task notifications are sent out automatically by the system according to the task notification schedule that has been set up for the task. Notifications may include task assignments, task reminders, overdue notices, and escalations. They may be sent to the task assignee, the assignor, or to other users for informational purposes.

A task notification will typically include a link to the task completion or view page in Advanced Calculations (EMS) or to the Task Completion App. The default task notifications also gives you the option to click to add the task to your calendar (such as Outlook). If you are the task assignee, you are responsible for closing the task after completing the required task. Until the task is marked complete in the system, you may continue to receive email task notifications, and the task will be considered overdue for reporting purposes.

To sign in and view the task details or complete the task completion form:

  1. Select the link in the email to view the task.

    The sign in page opens in a web browser.

  2. Sign in with your user name and password.

    Once you sign in, if you are the task assignee the task completion page will be displayed, ready for you to complete the task. If you received the email for notification purposes only, a read-only version of this form will show the current task status and information.

For security reasons, if you are already logged into the system, you will be required to sign in again to validate your credentials.
