Pre-Filtered Quick Data Links

In the Quick Data interface you can save Quick Links with saved filter criteria which can then be used on Quick Links panels to allow users to navigate directly to Quick Data with the desired filters applied (i.e., for data entry on pre-selected facility and source).

Quick Data links may be saved as personal links or system links.

Links to Quick Data can only be created as system Quick Links by a user with Manage Quick Links right. They must then be published or used on a distributed dashboard to be available to users.

To create a Quick Link with pre-set filter criteria for Quick Data:

  1. On any Quick Data page, set the filter criteria as desired.
  2. Click Add Quick Link.
  3. Enter a name for the link.
  4. Choose whether to save as a personal link or system link.
  5. Click OK.

Personal links will appear automatically on your My Quick Links panel on a dashboard, and may be managed through the Manage My Quick Links page. See My Quick Links.

System links will appear in the Shared Quick Links library (accessed from Setup) and may be used on a Shared Quick Links panel on a distributed dashboard, or published to users for their own use.
