Global Apps

Global Apps are distributed by Cority for general customer use. They are built and maintained by the development team and can be distributed to or removed from any system on request.

The configuration options for global apps are limited to creating Role memberships for users and groups to grant permissions. Depending on the app design, you may need to configure certain objects in your system that the app requires. The documentation for the app will explain anything that is required.

Once a global app is copied to your system, you can decide whether or not to display the page for the app. It will not be visible to users until you choose to enable it.

Global Apps cannot be downloaded, copied or edited.
