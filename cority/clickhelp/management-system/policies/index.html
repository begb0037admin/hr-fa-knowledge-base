Policies

The policies that apply to this workflow are shown in the Policies tab.

Notification policies are the only type of policy available at this time. (In future other types of policies might include escalation policies, validation policies and security policies.)

Notification policies define who is notified under what conditions in the workflow. The notifications are associated with a schedule and one or more Roles as the recipients. For instance, one policy might specify that users be notified when a step is assigned to them, and if it is not completed after 3 days. Another policy might specify that a supervisor be notified when the step is completed.

Policies applied to the workflow type apply to all instances of this workflow by default. However, policy overrides may be applied to system model objects. Workflow instances associated with a facility (or other object) with a policy override will use that object’s notifications policy. This allows different facilities to have their own notification policies for specific workflow types.

You can view and edit existing policies and create new policies from Policy Manager. See Workflow Policies.

To add a policy to the workflow type:

  1. In the Policies tab of the Workflow Type properties, click Add.

  2. Select the policy to apply from the selection window, and click Select Policies.
  3. To delete a policy, select the listed policy in the Policies tab, then click Delete.
