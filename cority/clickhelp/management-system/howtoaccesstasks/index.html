How to Access Tasks

You can access tasks in the following ways:

  • Email: Task assignments, task reminders, and other types of task notifications are customarily sent to you via email. Click on the link in the email, sign in to the system, and the task completion form will be displayed.
  • Task Completion App: Complete tasks on mobile devices and multiple browsers using the Task Completion App.
  • Tasks Panel on Dashboard: The Tasks Panel on the default Desktop dashboard in the Management System shows your current task assignments. Click the task link to open the task completion form. See Tasks Panel.
  • Calendar: The calendar shows your daily task and workflow assignments, in daily, weekly or monthly format. An abbreviated form of the calendar may also be shown on your dashboard. You may also view all tasks and workflows related to a facility, program or piece of equipment from the right-click menu of the object on the system model tree. See Task and Workflow Calendars.
  • Inbox: Task notifications may be delivered to your Inbox. Select Messages from the main menu to open your Inbox. Click the link to open the task completion form. See Inbox.
  • Task and Workflows Tab: Select Tasks from the Tasks and Workflows page to view and manage all your tasks, and search for tasks by various criteria. See View Tasks.
