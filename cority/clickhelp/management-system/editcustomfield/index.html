Edit Custom Field

To edit a custom field:

  1. In the Custom Field Library, find the field you want to edit, right click and select Edit.

    The properties of the field are displayed for editing.

  2. Modify the properties as desired.

    You cannot change the field type, and once a field is associated with a template, editing is limited to the caption and description.

    Editing the caption may alter the meaning on the forms in which the field is used, so if the field is used on more than one template, you should ensure that the change is appropriate for all uses.

     The number of templates on which a field is used is indicated in the # Ref column. You can determine what templates it is used on by attempting to delete the field. The operation will not be allowed if the field is used, but the warning page will list the associated templates.

  3. Select Save then Confirm.

To rename a custom field:

  1. From the right click popup menu, choose Rename.
  2. Enter a new name for the field and Save.
