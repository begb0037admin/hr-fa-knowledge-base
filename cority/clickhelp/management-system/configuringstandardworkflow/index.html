Configuring Standard Workflow Types

Several pre-defined, standard workflow types are available to be copied to your system on request. You can use these workflows out-of-the-box, or copy them and use them as a starter to create your own workflows. These workflows are fully editable in the Workflow Type Editor.

Before using a pre-defined workflow in your system, you will need to configure it for use by setting up workflow assignees and granting the appropriate permissions. Optionally, you can add notifications.

To view the general workflow configuration:

  1. From the menu, select Setup > Types under Workflows.
  2. Select the Workflow Type (or right click and select View or Edit).

The Process tab shows the steps in the workflow, the form template associated with each step, and the Role assigned to the step.

  • The process diagram gives a visual overview of the steps and possible paths in the workflow.
  • The Steps table shows the details of each step. Select a step row to display further details for that step.
  • Decide which users or groups in your system you want to assign to each step. It is recommended that you define default role memberships via groups for each role. You may leave the group members empty or assign dummy users for testing. Associate the groups with the roles on the Roles tab.
  • To see the step forms and the fields they contain, note the names of the forms and go to Form Templates Manager to view the forms. See Form Templates.

Initiate a Trial Workflow

You may want to initiate a trial workflow to get a feel for the process. 

  1. From Tasks and Workflows > Workflows, or from the Workflows panel on your desktop, select Create New Workflow.
  2. Select the Workflow Type. You may enter a name for the workflow instance or let the system generate a name.
  3. Click Next. On the next page, click Create.
    The form for the first step in the workflow is presented. Take note of the fields on the form. The same form may be used for different steps in the workflow, although different fields may be appropriate for different steps. You can specify which fields are available to view or edit at each step through the permissions given to the step assignee role.
  4. Click one of the Actions shown in the left panel (i.e., "Save and Assign...") to go to the next step in the workflow.
    The Save button below merely saves any form edits and leaves the workflow at the current step. In order to transition to another step, you must select a step action.
    The form may be the same as on the previous step, or it may be different. Note the fields that you may want to control access to on this step.
  5. Continue to step through the workflow, clicking an Action on each step to transition to the next.
  6. If you are a member of the role assigned to the next step, the form for the next step will be presented.
  7. If you are not a member of the role assigned to the next step, the workflow overview will be displayed. Click the link to the current step (flagged) to go to the step form.
  8. After you have stepped through the entire workflow and reviewed the forms, click End Workflow under Operations in the left pane.

To configure a workflow for use in your system:

  1. Assign default users and groups to Roles in the Roles tab. See Role Memberships .
  2. Grant permissions to the Roles in the Permissions tab. Permissions may be granted to the workflow as a whole, to each step, and to specific form fields at each step. See Workflow Permissions.
  3. Optionally, apply notification policies to the workflow in the Policies tab. See Policies.

Additionally, you may edit or change the form templates applied to each workflow step. See Form Templates.

You can customize workflow assignments on a facility basis by applying workflow overrides to each facility (or other system model object). Workflow overrides include:

  • Role Overrides: Specify default users and groups associated with Roles for that facility (or other object). See Role Membership Overrides.
  • Policy Overrides: Change notification policies applied to the workflow. See Policies Overrides.
