Quick Data

Quick Data allows users to view data and perform easy data entry tasks. You can also view reports and documents to which you have been granted Quick Data viewing permission.

What you see and the functions that are available to you depend on the permissions you have been granted in the system.

 Quick Data is supported only in the Internet Explorer browser. For cross-browser support, organizations are encouraged to use the Portal.
