System Model Tree Objects

The basic System Model tree objects in order of hierarchy are: Division, Facility/Program, Unit/Element, Point of Interest (POI).

These objects can be used in many different ways to model your business and environmental compliance requirements.

The System Model can be based on geography and physical entities. Alternatively, it could be based on media and program requirements. A combination of both can be utilized, depending on your specific requirements.

Regardless of how the hierarchical System Model is laid out, the actual compliance requirements (numeric and non-numeric) will be incorporated into the System Model depending on user interaction and reporting requirements.
