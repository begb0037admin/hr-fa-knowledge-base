View Requirements by Citation (Show Dependencies)

You can display all the requirements associated with a specific citation by searching for citation dependencies in the Citations library.

To show requirements by citation:

  1. In the Citations library, find the citation you are interested in.

  2. Right click the citation and choose Show Dependencies.

    All requirements associated with the citation are displayed.
