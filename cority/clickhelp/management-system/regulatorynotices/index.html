Regulatory Notices

You can set up Regulatory Notices to help you keep up-to-date with changes to regulatory citations that affect your compliance activities and implementation. Add specific regulatory citations to your notifications list, making a personal "watch list" to ensure that you are notified whenever any of the citations on your list changes.

You can subscribe to any number of citations through the BNA Cite-It® citation service. (This service is available at an additional cost.) When a regulatory citation on your list changes, you will receive an email notification.

The notifications list in Regulatory Notices is specific to each user. Each user must set up their personal notifications list to receive notifications. Regulatory notices are not system-wide, nor are they applicable to user groups.

View regulatory notices

A citation hyperlink field may also be a property of a Requirement (or other object). Any user with View permission on the object may then click the link from the properties page to view the current text of the citation associated with the object. Citation hyperlinks are independent of Regulatory Notices subscriptions.

To view the regulatory notices on your personal notifications list:

  1. Select Messages > Regulatory Notices.
    The Regulatory Notices to which you have subscribed are displayed.

  2. Click a column head to sort by any column.
  3. Use the Search pane to search for specific citations on your notification list by:
    • Status: Status of the notification request (Failed, Pending, Successful, or Queued)
    • Issued From/To: Active dates of the regulatory notice.

Add new regulatory notice

You can add new regulatory notices to your request list to receive email notification whenever the regulatory citation changes.

To add a new regulatory notice:

  1. Select Messages > Regulatory Notices.

  2. Select New Regulatory Notice.

  3. In the New Regulatory Notice form, click the Search link to search the citation database for the citation to add.

    The BNA web page opens in a new browser window.

  4. Choose whether you want to search federal of state regulations.
    For state regulations, select the state and regulatory category.
  5. Browse or search for the citation you want to link to.
  6. When you find the citation you want to link to, copy the citation number in red.

  7. Paste the citation text into the Regulatory Notice box.
  8. Add an optional description.

  9. Click Save, then Confirm.

  10. Click Back to Manager.
    The citation appears in the Regulatory Notices subscription list with a status of Queued.

    Once the request has been confirmed, the status will change to Successful. When changes occur to this citation, you will receive notification by email.

The status of the notice shown in the Regulatory Notices subscription list is initially Queued, and changes to Pending when the request has been logged. If the request is confirmed, the status changes to Successful. If the request was not successful, the status is Failed; view the error message in the Regulatory Notice Request Log for further information.

Delete regulatory notices

To unsubscribe from regulatory notifications, you can delete notices from your regulatory notice list.

To delete regulatory notices:

  1. Select Messages > Regulatory Notices.
    The Regulatory Notices to which you are subscribed are displayed.
  2. Select the checkbox(es) for one or more citations you want to delete.

  3. Right click and choose Delete from the menu.

  4. Confirm that you want to delete the notice(s) by clicking Delete.

    The notice will be deleted from your list. You will no longer be notified by email when the regulatory citation changes.
