Completing Workflow Assignments

You can access your workflow step assignments in several ways:

  • From a Workflow panel in the Portal app
  • From a Workflows panel on a dashboard in the Management System
  • From the Calendar
  • From an email notification
  • From a notification in your Inbox (in the Messages page)
  • From the Workflows page on the Tasks and Workflows page

To complete a workflow step you simply complete the form associated with the step and choose the appropriate action to move the workflow to the next step. Optionally, you may add comments.

The steps, actions, and the type of information requested will differ according to the workflow type. Depending on the design of the workflow and the permissions you have, you may do one or more of the following:

  • Complete the step form as required by the workflow type and step.
  • Send the workflow to the next step and choose an assignee.
  • Initiate a child workflow.
  • Re-assign the current step to another user.
  • Add comments.
  • Associate a document.
  • End the workflow.
