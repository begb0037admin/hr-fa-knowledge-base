GSE-1691: In Frameworks, users can now create Aggregated Reports.

  • In Frameworks > Reporting > Aggregated Reports, users can click the Configuration button to aggregate Frameworks data into custom reports.
  • In the View Configuration modal box, users can define the report, including the period group, years of data, and question(s) that the report will be based on. Users can also define the view, nodes, and frequency for which they want the data.
  • After clicking Apply, the data will be aggregated according to what was specified in the configuration and will be displayed in the Aggregated Reports table.
