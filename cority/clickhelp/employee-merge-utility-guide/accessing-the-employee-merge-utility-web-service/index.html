Accessing the Employee Merge Utility Web Service

You can determine the URL of the Employee Merge Utility web service by looking at your login URL. For example, if your login URL is https://demo.Cority.com/gx2/security/login.rails, your Employee Merge Utility web service URL is Https://Demo.Cority.com/Gx2/Webservice/Employeenumbermergeservice.Svc.
