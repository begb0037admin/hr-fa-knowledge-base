What is My Dashboard?

If your organization uses myCority, then 'My Dashboard' lets you visit your myCority Dashboard. There you can:

  • View information about your risk level
  • Initiate or continue ergonomic training and assessment
  • Work on issues (or update information) identified in an assessment, discomfort survey, or identified by RSIGuard
  • Request ergonomic evaluations (if available at your organization)
  • View a summary of your RSIGuard statistics (exposure levels, break patterns, etc.)
  • View messages from the ergonomics team and the OES system (Legacy Enviance OES)


Accessing 'My Dashboard'

Your dashboard will open in your default web browser (you may be asked to log in or authenticate via SSO).
