From Start to Implementation: How to implement RSIGuard at your organization

Learn about RSIGuard

You can learn about RSIGuard by reviewing the documentation here. For example, the section Getting Started with RSIGuard includes an overview and introductory tutorial.


Try RSIGuard

Trying RSIGuard can be as simple as downloading and installing the RSIGuard 45-day trial on your and a few co-worker computers. This will give you first-hand experience with RSIGuard. If you want to do a more extensive pilot, see the section Piloting RSIGuard.


Purchase RSIGuard

We're confident you will have a positive experience and recognize the value RSIGuard can bring to your organization to minimize discomfort, boost productivity and morale, and reduce injuries. When you're ready to purchase, here are the choices to make:

  1. Determine how many users will use RSIGuard. Since the purpose of RSIGuard is prevention, we encourage you to take advantage of volume discounts and implement RSIGuard for all users with regular computer usage.
    1. If your license will be for fewer than 500 users, you can purchase a perpetual license. This includes use of the version of RSIGuard available at time of purchase forever.
    2. If your license will be for 500 or more users, you can purchase either a perpetual license or a subscription license. Please inquire about pricing to help you determine which choice is best for your organization.
  2. If you purchase a perpetual license with 50 or more licenses, you have the option to purchase an annual maintenance contract. View details about Maintenance and Support. (RSIGuard Subscriptions already include annual maintenance support.) Click here to review the End User License Agreement (EULA).


When you are ready to purchase RSIGuard:

  • For licenses under 50 copies, you can purchase directly from our website.
  • For licenses of 50 or more copies, please contact sales@rsiguard.com for pricing.


Deploy RSIGuard

After purchase, you'll receive a link to your RSIGuard installation package (either to a private "custom package" download link if you opted for this under your maintenance and support, or a standard download link otherwise). Your IT department can either download RSIGuard and deploy it using a methodology of their choice, or you can have your users use the download link directly if they are permitted to install software.


Enjoy RSIGuard

Most people enjoy RSIGuard, especially when they personalize it to meet their needs and personality. RSIGuard is ergonomics software, and the central idea of ergonomics is that tools (like RSIGuard) should be adjustable to the user (rather than forcing users to adjust to the tools). Visit these RSIGuard Help pages to explore RSIGuard, and reach out to RSIGuard SupportRSIGuard Support if you need assistance.
