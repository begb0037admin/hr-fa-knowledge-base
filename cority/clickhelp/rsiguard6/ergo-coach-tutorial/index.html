ErgoCoach Tools

Do you have  sit-stand desk?

...use 2 or more monitors?

...use a notebook or laptop computer?

...share a workplace with other people?

ErgoCoach offers optimization tools for various work environments. Be sure to configure these during the setup wizard (or via the Settings pages) to enable the features that can help you.

  • You can get advice on your monitor placement based on how RSIGuard sees you use your monitors.
  • The sit-stand coach can remind you when it's a good idea to sit or stand.
  • A notebook computer tool can encourage you to use external devices if you're spending a lot of time without them.
  • Training tools can offer you ongoing information and add extra ForgetMeNot messages that are important for your situation.
