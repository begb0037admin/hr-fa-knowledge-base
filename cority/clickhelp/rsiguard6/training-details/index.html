ErgoCoach Training Methodology and Custom Trainings

RSIGuard comes with 3 ErgoCoach "daily trainings", covering the topics of "Sit Stand Desk", "Multiple Monitors", and "Shared Workstations". Customers with customized versions of RSIGuard can add custom trainings (e.g., how to use a specific model of chair, safety or operational training for a piece of lab equipment, or learning a company-specific practice).

ErgoCoach training is different than typical training modules in that it is delivered in small chunks over the course of multiple days. RSIGuard provides a mechanism to view a little portion of training each day.

This model of training is called "Distributed Training" or "Distributed Practice" because it is distributed over a longer period of time. Traditional "all at once" training is called "Massed Practice".

There is a large body of research that has compared these two methodologies, virtually all of which shows that Distributed is much more effective than Massed training. However, most training is done as Massed because it's hard to bring users into a training, so, when you do, the thinking is "just get the whole training done at once." ErgoCoach Trainings allow you to create Distributed training that is proven to be more effective and is generally more interesting and pleasant as well.

To learn more about research on the benefits of Distributed vs. Massed practice, see:

  • ScienceDirect Comparative Analysis of Distributed vs Massed Practice
  • Digital Promise (Educational Organization) description of the benefits of Distributed Practice
  • Swinburne University of Technology study of learning methodologies
