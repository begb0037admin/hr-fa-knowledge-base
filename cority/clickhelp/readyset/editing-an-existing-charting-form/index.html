Editing an Existing Charting Form

Edits made to an existing Charting Form, for example to a Radiobutton, the editor should ensure that the new answer is populating by getting the form charted to a person to test the published Charting form.

To Edit an Existing Form

  1. Click the Client Config tab.
  2. On the Left Navigation tab, click on the wrench icon in front of a form.
    The Form displays to the left.

  1. Right click a question on the form, and then click Properties.
    The Question Properties window appears.

All textbox sections that are not greyed out can be edited.

  1. Once all changes are made, click Save.
