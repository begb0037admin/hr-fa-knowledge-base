Viewing Medical Considerations

There are two ways to view the Medical Considerations.

To view Medical Considerations from the Employee Record:

  1. Click on EHS Manager tab.
  2. Click the Participant Management tab in the primary navigation menu.
  3. Search by Last name, First name or by Employee ID.
  4. Click Search.
    This will provide a results list on the right side of the screen. Confirm your search has found your employee.
  5. Click the Employee Edit icon or double-click to open that employee record.
  6. Click View Surveys in the left menu.
    This displays a screen with two panels. The left side panel displays the     Survey List with status display.
  7. Click View Summary to display the Survey Summary with the Medical Considerations.

To view Medical Considerations from the Medical Chart:

  1. Follow steps 1-5 from the above procedure.
  2. Click Medical Chart in the left menu.
    This displays a split screen. Each side has the same available tabs.
  3. Click the Surveys tab from either side.
  4. Click View Summary in the top right corner of the region.
    This will display the     Survey Summary with Medical Considerations.
