Medical Considerations

You can configure medical considerations to appear with an answer. They will appear on the Survey Summary in EHS Manager > Participant > View Surveys > Survey Summary as well as for Event Manager users on the Considerations tab. They are typically medical considerations from a Product Insert or VIS that are important to review.

  1. Within Answer Properties > Details, the Display Consideration box must be selected for the medical consideration that is associated with the answer to appear on the Survey Summary.
  2. To add a medical consideration, click the Considerations tab in Answer Properties.
  3. Click New and fill in the consideration text that should display.
  4. It is recommended you add the Source (which can be an HTML link to a VIS, for example).
  5. Click Save.

Editing and Inactivating Considerations

To edit an existing consideration:

  1. Within Answer Properties > Considerations, click the Edit icon to make changes to the consideration text or source.
  2. Click Save, once complete.
    Changes will appear on the Meta tab under 'Consideration'.

To remove a consideration:

  1. Within Answer Properties > Considerations, select the check box next to the desired consideration.
  2. Click Inactivate.

It is not possible to re-activate a consideration after it has been inactivated. A new consideration must be created.

History

Changes that are made to considerations are available on the Meta tab in Answer Properties.

  1. Right-click the answer and select Answer Properties.
  2. Navigate to the Meta tab.
    Changes related to the answer will display.
  3. Scroll to the Consideration section to view previous consideration text and source information, if it has changed, as well as the time and date stamp and username of the individual who made the change.

Best Practices

These best practices should be followed:

  • Ensure the considerations within your survey are up to date, that they reflect the latest guidance.
  • While the source field is optional, it is highly recommended you use it so you can quickly reference the source to ensure accuracy and transparency.
  • Since considerations are provided to your event users, it is recommended you use the Event tab and provide any important clinical recommendations and information to anyone reviewing the survey summary.
