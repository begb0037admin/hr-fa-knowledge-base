Self-Service Tools: Usernames & Passwords

Users can reset their password and look up their username from the login page.

Self-Service Tools: Retrieving Your Username

To retrieve your username, from the login page, click Forgot Username?. You must have your First and Last Name, Date of Birth, and Organization Code. If you do not know your organization code, contact your administrator.

Self-Service Tools: Retrieving Your Password

To retrieve your password, from the login page, click Forgot Password?. You must have the Username and Date of Birth.

Verifying Security Questions

  • Log in and search for participant using any of the search criteria provided (typically first name, last name).
  • If there are multiple returned, narrow your search by asking for a DOB or another piece of information.
  • Verify answers to security questions they set up prior to giving out a username & resetting their password.
  • Stay on the phone with them until you verify the login is successful.
  • Give them their new password and let them know they will have to change it immediately upon logging in.
  • Password criteria:
    • At least 8 characters and no more than 30 characters in length
    • At least One Upper Case & One Lower Case
    • At least One Number
    • At least One Special Character from the following list:  ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( )

Changing Passwords

Once you update a password using the Help Desk tab, the user will need to know their old password and understand that they will immediately need to create a new one.

Unlocking Accounts

If a user enters the wrong password three times, the account will be locked. To unlock a users account, do the following:

  1. Login and look up the participant.
  2. The window will look the same, however you will see, “Account Locked: Yes” 

  3. Click Unlock and the account is now unlocked.

Other Information

  • “Login Failed! User account is not active”. This means that no one with that username has created an account.
  • Employees are blocked from creating accounts unless their First Name, Last Name, DOB and SSN matches what you are sending Cority in the HR feed.
    • Many times people will mistakenly input the wrong DOB or SSN – They need to use their full legal name, not nicknames
    • For example, if the payroll/HR system has you listed as William, you should not use Bill as your first name when creating an account.
  • New hires will need to create accounts according to the instructions provided by HR/Recruiter or EHS Clinician (My Health Access Letter).
    • When they enroll in one of the new hire programs (e.g. Pre Placement), that is how they are assigned the appropriate surveys. If they enroll in the wrong program, they should call EHS to ensure there aren’t additional surveys they must complete. The EHS Clinicians can assign those.
