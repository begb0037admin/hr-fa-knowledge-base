Enabling Enhanced Notifications

To set up Enhanced Notification preferences for EHS managers, recruiters, supervisors, and participants (via User Settings in My Health):

  1. Once Enhanced Notifications is enabled, click the User Settings tab. 
  2. On the left navigation panel, under My Account, click Notifications.
    The Notifications window opens to the right. 
  3. In the Notifications Options section, select one of the notifications that are enabled:  
    1. Task assigned to user - is available to EHS Managers, Case Managers, or any users who have tasks assigned to them. Click the Task assigned to user drop down textbox and select either Email and Text, Email, or Text.  

      If you do not have text messaging enabled for your notifications the dropdown menu will only display Email as an option. 

    2. New Survey assigned to a Participant – is only assigned to and enabled for participants. This will send a notification to participants when a new survey is assigned to them for completion. Notifications will not be sent for surveys that are assigned via C3 since those are already accompanied by an email.   
    3. New Self Report Incident Created – is available to EHS Managers, and Supervisors who have employees assigned to them or the Incident Tab. If notifications are turned on for this option by EHS Managers with the Incident Tab, they will receive an email and/or text when a new self-reported incident is created. If a supervisor selects this option, they will receive email and text, email, or text when an employee assigned to them fills out a self-reported incident. 

      Alerts and notifications are only generated for EHS Managers and Supervisors that are in the same position as employees.

    4. Appointment cancelled – is only available for recruiters. This sends an email and/or text to recruiters when an appointment has been cancelled for a candidate who is assigned to them.  
    5. Pre-placement complete – is only available for recruiters. This sends an email and/or text to recruiters when a candidate who is assigned to them has completed their pre-placement.  
  4. To have an email notification sent, type in the email address in the Notification email address textbox and Confirm Notification email textbox.

    This email address should be a work email and must be maintained by the user. This will not be updated by your HR (Human Resources) feed.   

  5. To have a text notification sent, type in the phone number in the Notification Phone number textbox.

    The phone number must be a US phone number and must include an area code and a 7-digit phone number.  This field will only be available if the client has an active Twilio account.   

  6. Once all fields are filled in as needed, click Update.
