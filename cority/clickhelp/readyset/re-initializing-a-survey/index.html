Re-Initializing a Survey

Re-initializing a survey does not clear the answers, but rather it allows the new hire/employee to re-answer any question.

  1. Select the participant from the Participant Management on the primary navigation menu.
  2. Choose View Surveys on the left menu. 
    This will display the Survey List with status displayed with green check marks (indicates completed) or the word Incomplete. You can only re-initialize surveys with a green check mark.
  3. To re-initialize, click on the green icon in front of the survey.
  4. Click Yes to the "Re-initialize this survey?" prompt.
    The green check mark changes to Incomplete.
