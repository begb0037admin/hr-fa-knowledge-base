Viewing Surveys

To view surveys:

  1. Navigate to your Client Config tab and select the Survey Config sub-tab.
  2. On the left-hand panel, you will see a list of the active surveys currently available for your organization.
    Keep in mind that 'active' does not mean published. Some of the surveys you see in the list might be unpublished versions. Those cannot yet be ordered for participants.
  3. If you would like inactive surveys included in the list, click the Show Inactive check box.
  4. Navigate the list pages using the arrows or numeric options at the bottom.
  5. Collapse or display the side panel.
