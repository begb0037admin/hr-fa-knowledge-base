Who should have access?

Typically, the Client Config role should be given to only a few individuals within your organization. It is best if the users who have the Client Config role are also Client Admin users, because some configurations require access to both tools.

Users who are given access to Custom Surveys:

  • Will also have access to Custom Forms if/when that tool is enabled for your organization.
  • Should have a strong understanding of your organization's policies for compliance and program requirements.
  • Should have a strong understanding of how your organization uses Standard Reports, Custom Reports, and CorAnalytics dashboards, because survey configurations could alter reporting.
  • Should monitor and be available to adjust medical considerations to reflect updated products, VIS, FDA, and CDC guidance, among other regulatory considerations.

Also, any users who have the Client Config role must complete all training modules for Custom Surveys on Cority Academy.
