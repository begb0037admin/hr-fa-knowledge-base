Adding a Question to a Charting Form

To Add a Question to a Charting Form

  1. Right click the Group created under the New Form.
    A Menu appears.
  2. Click New Question.
    Another menu is displayed to the left with a list of all the different types of questions a user can create.
  3. Click the question type you want to create.
