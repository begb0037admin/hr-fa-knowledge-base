Creating a Correspondence or Email

To create a correspondence: 

  1. Select desired participant.
  2. Choose Correspondence from the menu on the left.
    This opens a Correspondence screen on the right.

To send an email to a participant:

  1. Click the Create Email icon, located at the top of the region.
    This opens a Create Email Correspondence dialog box.
  2. Click on the Notification Type drop-down and select Blank Email Template or any templated options available.
  3. Complete the TO: and CC: fields, Employee and Self are already checked.
  4. You can check the Supervisor option if HR feed is sending this information.
  5. Complete the Subject line.
  6. Type the message.
  7. Click Send.

Record Correspondence with a Participant

  1. Click on New to open the Edit Correspondence dialog box. Required fields have a “*” beside them:
    • Status* – Click the drop-down and select Open or Closed.
    • Resolution – Click the drop-down and select Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
    • Follow-up Required* - Click the drop-down and select Yes or No.
    • Follow-up Date – Click the calendar picker and select the date for follow-up or type in the date, mm/dd/yyyy.
    • Method - Click the drop-down and select the correspondence method.
    • Description* – Click the drop-down and select the item that relates to your correspondence.
    • Assign To – Click the drop-down and select the person that you are assigning this correspondence. Not all EHS clinicians will have permission to assign correspondence.
    • Department – Click the drop-down and select the correct item.
    • Message – Enter free form information.
  2. Click Save and Close.
