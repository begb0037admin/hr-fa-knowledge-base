Influenza Common Reports

Listed below are four common Influenza Reports and how to utilize the ReadySet 4 reporting tab. 

You must have used the system to chart deferrals and deferral dates, exemptions, etc.

How Do I See Who Has Deferred Their Flu Test So I Can Follow Up With Them?

This report displays a list of who deferred, when the deferral was approved, who approved the deferral and deferral until date, along with their Contact information.

  1. On the Report tab, choose the Flu Detail report.
  2. Customize your Report Name.
  3. In the Selection Criteria enter your Start Test and End Test dates.
  4. Select your Program Type (Mandatory or Non-Mandatory).
  5. In the Column Selection:
    1. Un-check the All checkbox.
    2. Check the Participant Identifiers you want (or check the All Participant Identifiers).
    3. Check the Contact Information wanted.
    4. Close all the groups until you get to Deferral Information.
    5. Check the Deferral Information group to select all items in this group.
  7. Click Run Once.
  8. Click Download Reports.
  9. When your customized Flu Detail report is Ready to View, click the View Report icon and download to Excel. 

Who Has a Pending / Approved / Not Approved Exemption Status?

This report displays your list by Exemption Status.

  1. On the Report tab, choose the Flu Detail report.
  2. Customize your Report Name.
  3. In the Selection Criteria enter your Start Test and End Test dates.
  4. Select your Program Type (Mandatory or Non-Mandatory).
  5. Select the applicable Survey Status.
  6. In the Column Selection:
    1. Un-check the All checkbox.
    2. Check the Participant Identifiers you want (or check the All Participant Identifiers).
    3. Check the Contact Information wanted.
    4. Check the General Information wanted.
    5. Close all the groups until you get to Exemption Information.
    6. Check the Exemption Information group checkbox.
  8. Click Run Once.
  9. Click Download Reports.
  10. When your customized Flu Detail report is Ready to View, click the View Report icon and download to Excel.

Who Has Not Taken the Survey?

This report displays the list of survey statuses.

  1. On the Report tab, choose the Flu Detail report.
  2. Customize your Report Name.
  3. In the Selection Criteria enter your Start Test and End Test dates.
  4. Select your Program Type (Mandatory or Non-Mandatory).
  5. Select the applicable Survey Status.
  6. In the Column Selection:
    1. Un-check the All checkbox.
    2. Check the relevant Participant Identifiers.
    3. Check the relevant General Information.
    4. Check the Survey Status checkbox.
    5. If you also want to see whether or not they have had a charting form (test) completed, check the Test Status checkbox.
  7. Click Run Once.
  8. Click Download Reports.
  9. When your customized Flu Detail report is Ready to View, click the View Report icon and download to Excel.

Who Has Already Received / Wants to Receive / Does Not Want the Flu Shot This Year?

This report displays the list of survey statuses.

  1. On the Report tab, choose the Flu Detail report.
  2. Customize your Report Name.
  3. In the Selection Criteria enter your Start Test and End Test dates.
  4. Select your Program Type (Mandatory or Non-Mandatory).
  5. Select the applicable Survey Status.
  6. In the Column Selection:
    1. Un-check the All checkbox.
    2. Check the relevant Participant Identifiers.
    3. Check the relevant General Information.
    4. Check the Survey First Answer checkbox.
    5. You may want to select other checkboxes in the Survey & Test Information group depending on your needs.
  7. Click Run Once.
  8. Click Download Reports.
  9. When your customized Flu Detail report is Ready to View, click the View Report icon and download to Excel.
