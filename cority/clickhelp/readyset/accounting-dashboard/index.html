Accounting Dashboard

The accounting dashboard displays the following information: 

  • Total Billable Participants: Total number of participants that had a status of “Active” or “Leave of Absence” before the billing period started (and still do), that were created and active after the start of the billing period, or have a status of “Terminated” with a termination date before the start of the billing period and their status changed to terminated from active, leave of absence, or a custom status after the billing period start date. If a termination date is not entered, the participant will not be excluded from the count of billed participants.
  • New Billable Participants in Contract Period: Total number of participants who newly had a status of “Active” or “Leave of Absence” after the billing period started. This will also include participants with a status of "Terminated" if no termination date is entered.
  • Contract Billing Period: Displays the date range (mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy), pulling the month and day from the client reference 'Billing Period Start MM/DD' and calculating the year as the current year -1 year for the start.
  • Max Employees: Displays the maximum number of contracted participants for that year. 

The dashboard also displays the following charts:

  • Total Billable Participants by Month: Displays the total number of billable participants for each respective month. The totals displayed in the monthly counts in this chart do not respect the day value of the contract billing period. For example, if the contract billing period is from March 15 2024-March 15 2025, the total billable participant count at the top will respect the date of the 15th, however the monthly counts in the chart will display the monthly count from the 1st day of each month to the last day of each month. The chart displays a rolling 12 months.

  • Total billable participants by...: Displays the total number of billable participants based on the selected filter criteria from the available dropdown. The filter options include: 

    • Cost Center
    • Department
    • Division
    • Facility
    • Job Category
    • Location
    • Population Type
    • Supervisor Name
