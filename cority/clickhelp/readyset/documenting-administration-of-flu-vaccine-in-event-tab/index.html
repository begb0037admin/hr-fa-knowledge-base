Documenting Administration of Flu Vaccine in Event Tab

Follow these simple steps to document the administration of flu vaccine to a participant.

To avoid charting on incorrect records, please remember these tips to search for your patient:

  • Add Employee(s) – Allows you to search by criteria such as Last Name, Dept., Facility, Occupation etc.
  • Remove Selected Employee(s) – Allows you to remove participants from the “send to” group.
  • Remove All Employees – Allows you to remove the entire “send to” group.

    • By following these guidelines, you should locate the patient and prevent charting errors.
