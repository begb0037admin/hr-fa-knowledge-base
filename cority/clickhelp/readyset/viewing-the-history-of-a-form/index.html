Viewing the History of a Form

The history of a form can only be viewed once the form has been published (See Publishing a Charting Form).

To View the History of a Form

  1. On the horizontal navigation panel, click Client Config.
  2. Click the wrench in front of a form. The form window displays to the right.
  3. Click the View history dropdown textbox. The following information is displayed:
  • Name of the form
  • Date and time of when it was published
  • Who published it

To view changes made after the initial publishing of the form

  1. Click Show level.
  2. Click Answers.
  3. Click the History dropdown textbox.
  4. Select the Published on information that appears.
    Form-Published on window displays to the left of the Form window.

  5. You can expand each group and compare what has changed from when it was initially published to what is shown on the form currently.

If users prefer the old charting form over the new one created, the user will have to rebuild the charting form, there is no way to revert to the old charting form.
