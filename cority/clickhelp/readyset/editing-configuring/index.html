Creating, Editing, and Configuring Custom Surveys

Custom Surveys can be be created from the Survey Config tab, under Client Config. From the Survey Config tab, you can do the following:

  1. Click the + New icon at the top of the Survey list to create a new survey. 
  2. To edit or configure an existing survey:
    1. Select the survey
    2. Click the Configure icon or right-click the survey name and select Configure. 
    3. Make any changes needed. 

Changes you make to an existing survey could impact other departments, divisions, regions, or partitions that order the survey.
