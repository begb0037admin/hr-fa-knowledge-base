National Healthcare and Safety Network (NHSN) Report Overview for the Influenza Season

This document highlights some of what you need to know about NHSN reporting requirements and the logic behind the Axion Health Influenza Program that allows you to report to NHSN. The NHSN report was developed to capture vaccination rates of healthcare professionals. See Table 1 and Table 2 for an index of which pieces of the Axion Health Influenza program tie to the NHSN report.

* This is only a brief overview and you are encouraged to review The NHSN Manual for Healthcare Personnel Vaccination Module: Influenza Vaccination Summary ( http://www.cdc.gov/nhsn/PDFs/HPS-manual/vaccination/HPS-flu-vaccine-protocol.pdf ) for more detailed information about the NHSN report and requirements. Note, this manual should be supplemented with the most current information from the NHSN newsletter and website: NHSN website ( http://www.cdc.gov/nhsn )

CDC/NHSN Data Requirements

Below is a highlight of some of the CDC data requirements for reporting to NHSN.

Numerator

To calculate the numerator, NHSN report requires data to be categorized as follows:

  • Vaccinated: number of healthcare workers who received the influenza vaccination at your facility this season
  • Received Vaccination Elsewhere: number of healthcare workers who provided written report or documentation of influenza vaccination outside your facility for the current influenza season
  • Medical Contraindication: includes only persons who report severe allergic reaction to eggs, or other vaccine components, or history of Guillain-Barre Syndrome (GBS) within 6 weeks of a previous influenza vaccination
  • Declined Vaccination: number of healthcare workers who voluntarily declined vaccination for one or more of the following reasons:
    • Declined because of conditions not considered a medical contraindication above
    • Declined vaccination and did not provide additional information
    • Declined because of religious exemptions
    • Deferred vaccination for the entire influenza season (after March 31)
  • Unknown Vaccination Status: number of healthcare workers whose vaccination status are unknown or cannot be determined.

Denominator

The NHSN report denominator rules are as follows:

  • Inclusion of all healthcare workers who physically work in the reporting facility for 1 day or more during the reporting period (October – March of current season
  • Inclusion of both full-time and part-time workers
  • Count each healthcare worker in only one employee type category
  • Only over age 18 are included

To calculate the denominator, NHSN report requires data to be categorized as follows:

  • Employees: all staff on facility payroll regardless of patient contact status
  • Non-Employees:
    • Licensed Independent Practitioners: Includes physicians (MD and DO), advanced practice nurses, physician assistants and post-residency fellows who are not employed by the facility (on the payroll) but are associated with it
    • Adult Students/Trainees and Volunteers: Includes medical, nursing or other health students, interns, medical residents, or volunteers who are aged 18 and older who are not employed by the facility (on the payroll) but are affiliated with it
    • Other Contract Personnel: persons who provide care, treatment or services at the facility and do not fall into any other category

Axion Health Data Requirements and Considerations

Axion Health has incorporated logic into the influenza program to allow your facility to generate NHSN reports right from our system. In order to optimize the quality of your report you must comply with the following data requirements and considerations:

Requirements

  • Mapping of your employee types to the NHSN employee types (denominator) – you will be contacted regarding by a member of the Axion Team this mapping
  • There is only 1 charting form per employee

Considerations

  • All employees regardless of how many facilities at which they work, will only be reported in the report for their primary location as indicated on the demographics page.
  • Pre-employment employees are not included in the report.
  • Employees who are in the system for 1 or more days are included in the report.
  • We recommend you run the NHSN report from our system once a month during the reporting period to ensure proper data output.
  • If you send a termination date in your HR feed, your NHSN data will be more accurate – if you are not sending a termination date and would like to start; contact your implementation manager.
