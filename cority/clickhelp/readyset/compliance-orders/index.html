Compliance Orders 

The Compliance Orders view allows users to define compliance on an individual basis and allows for the supervisor to see notes on a program, like Respirator size or Required to wear a mask during flu season. EHS Managers complete the charting form and then complete the compliance order.


After selecting Submit Final, the compliance flag dialog box displays, and users can activate the flag indicating compliance.


The Compliance Expiration Date options are as follow:

  • Days – options are 1,2,3,4,5,7,21 or 28
  • Weeks – options are 1,2,3,4,5,6,8 or 12
  • Months – options are 1,2,3,4,5,6 or 8
  • Years – options are 1,2,3,4,5,6 or 10 Expired – means not compliant
  • Today
  • Birth Date – pulls the birth date from the demographic record and uses the next year if the date is past.
  • Hire Date – pulls the hire date from the demographic record and uses the next year if the date is past.
  • Not Applicable – removes from the program
  • Custom – allows the user to enter in a date.
  • Permanent – makes the participant compliant permanently.

Viewing Compliance from the Medical Record

Compliance can also be viewed from within a medical record. In the Participant Management menu, click Compliance. This view provides the participant’s overall compliance and individual compliance per program listed. Users can also change the compliance flag from this view by opening the record and editing the charting form.
