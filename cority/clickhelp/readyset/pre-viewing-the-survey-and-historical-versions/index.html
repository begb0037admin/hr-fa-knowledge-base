Previewing the Survey and Historical Versions

You can preview the Survey and any historical versions of a survey in any of the following way:

  • To preview how a survey will look to a participant when opened in MyHealth, while the survey is in Edit or Configuration mode, click the View Survey button at the top.
    A survey preview window will open.
  • To view a historical version of a survey, click its View History drop-down list and select the version you would like to see.
  • Click the arrow in the Unused Questions bar to view questions that have been removed from the survey.
