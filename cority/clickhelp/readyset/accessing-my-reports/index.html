Accessing My Reports

To Access My Reports:

  1. Click the Reports tab on the horizontal navigation panel. 
  2. Click the My Reports tab in the horizontal navigation panel below. 
  3. Under the My Reports sections, click the folder where the report you are looking for was saved under.
    The folder expands with reports below it. 
  4. Click the Report.
