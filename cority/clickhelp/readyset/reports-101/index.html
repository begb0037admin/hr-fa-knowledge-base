General Features of the ReadySet Report Runner

Features and benefits of ReadySet Report Runner:

  • Run reports on demand or pre-schedule
  • Create custom “My Reports” from standard reports
  • Customize report names
  • Customize filters - location, supervisor, date range
  • Customize column outputs - demographics, lot #, compliance status
  • Download Reports area – secure report holding area for up to 30 days
  • Manage reports in online download folder
  • Share reports with ReadySet users securely
