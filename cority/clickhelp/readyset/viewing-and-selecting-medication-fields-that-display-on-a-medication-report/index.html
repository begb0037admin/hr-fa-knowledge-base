Viewing and Selecting Medication Fields that Display on a Medication Report

To View and Select Medication Fields that Display on a Medication Report:

  1. Click the Reports tab.
    A list of sub tabs display horizontally below.
  2. Click the Standard Reports tab.
  3. On the right navigation panel, click Health and Safety.
    The Health & Safety menu expands.
  4. Click Problem List Details.
    The Problem List Details window displays to the right.
  5. In the Columns section, click Medication.

Below the Medication tab, a list of fields that are on the Medication layout tab will display Indicating the fields that would be included in the medication report. You can select and deselect fields as required to display on the Medical report.
