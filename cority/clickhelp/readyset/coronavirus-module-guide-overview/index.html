Coronavirus Module Overview

The Coronavirus (COVID-19) module is a suite of surveys, charting and reporting tools used specifically to record, track, and manage a Coronavirus exposure case or incident. It is designed to integrate with the Incident Management module in order to manage multiple exposure contacts. The Injury, Illness, or Exposure and Case Management modules are also recommended if OSHA and Workers’ Comp reporting are required.

Before You Begin

  • Access to the EHS Manager tab is required.
  • Access to the Client Admin tab is required if setting up communication templates.
  • The terms employee, worker or participant are used interchangeably in this document.
  • The term PUI means "Person Under Investigation".

When to Use the Coronavirus (COVID-19) Module

  • For any suspected COVID-19 virus exposure involving one or more participants at a facility.
  • For one or more exposures where incident reporting (OSHA or Workers’ Comp) is required.
  • A participant has traveled to a Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Level 2 (or higher) alert country.
  • When you need to provide specific, CDC-compliant surveys, charting forms, and reports for assessing and managing a virus surveillance program for EHS.

When considering how to use the ReadySet Coronavirus (COVID-19) module at your facility, think about your workflow and the questions you need answered about the participants and the incident itself:

  • Determination: Who should be surveyed for possible exposure?
  • Evaluation: Do the survey results recommend medical surveillance?
  • Surveillance and Outcomes: Do symptoms and temperature recorded during the specified period recommend invoking containment and hospitalization protocols?

Components

The Coronavirus module comprises the following components:

Surveys

Travel Screening Survey 

  • Assigned through EHS Manager or can be self-reported (My Health)
  • Participant has traveled to a CDC Alert country and wants to self-report.
  • EHS policy at the facility mandates completion for any employee who has traveled in any of the affected areas defined by the CDC.
  • Participant selects this survey from My Health/Report Incident, completes and submits.

COVID-19 Staff Exposure Form

  • Assigned through EHS Manager or Incident Management
  • Participant suspects they have had contact with COVID-19 outside of work and wants to self-report the incident.
  • EHS policy at the facility mandates this survey for any employee who has traveled in any of the affected areas defined by the CDC or possible exposure in their work environment.

Temperature and Symptom Tracker (Participant/EHS Manager)

  • Assigned through EHS Manager or Incident Management
  • EHS Manager can activate this survey at any time.
  • Used for participant temperature and symptom self-monitoring during quarantine or furlough.
  • Participant is responsible for recording symptom data.

Charting Forms

Coronavirus (COVID 19) Employee Under Investigation (PUI) Form (EHS Manager)

  • EHS completes the form after the participant completes the surveillance without recording any symptoms or shows one or more symptoms during surveillance and is quarantined or hospitalized. Chart Return to Work information.

Injury, Illness or Exposure (IIE) Progress Note

  • Used for OSHA reporting.
  • Can be used to document source patient exposure.

Reports

  • COVID-19 Staff Exposure Survey – Provides the survey status, all survey answers, creation date and completion date.
  • Travel Screening Survey – Provides the survey status, all survey answers, creation date and completion date.
  • Coronavirus (2019-nCov) PUI Form Details – Provides all data charted including test status, travel history, contact, quarantine information, symptoms, comorbidity, hospitalization, diagnostic results, return to work and follow-up.
  • Temperature and Symptom Tracker – Outputs all tracked temperatures and symptoms entered by participants.
  • Incident Details – Outputs all details for incidents created under the Incident tab. Use this report only if using the Incident tab to track multiple participants exposed to COVID-19.
  • Incident Summary – Outputs summary details on an individual incident or multiple incidents with no participant information included. Use this report only if using the Incident tab to track multiple participants exposed to COVID-19.
  • Other Reports – Progress Note Details, Progress Note Incident Log
