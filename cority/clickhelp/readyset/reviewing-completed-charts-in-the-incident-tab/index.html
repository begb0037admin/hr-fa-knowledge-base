Reviewing Completed Charts in the Incident Tab

Use the Charting Forms option to track the charting status and view a completed chart.

  1. Click Charting Forms in the left menu.
    This displays the list of participants included in the incident that have a chart(s) to complete.
  2. The Status column displays "Pending" or "Complete".
    If complete, click the     Display Results icon to view the charted information.
  3. Click the Jump to Chart icon to access the participant’s medical record.
