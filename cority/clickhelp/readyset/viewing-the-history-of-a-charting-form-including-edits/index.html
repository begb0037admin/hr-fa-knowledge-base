Viewing the History and edits of a Charting Form

The history and edits of a charting form can viewed from multiple areas. 

To view the history and edits of a charting form from EHS manager:

  1. On the EHS Manager tab, select Participant Manager.
  2. Search for the Participant.
  3. On the Medical History/Results tab, open the charting form to view.
  4. Click the Clock icon shown by the appointment date.
  5. View the history of the charting form, including any edits made to the form.

The history of edits icon is also available in the following areas of ReadySet:

  • EHS Manager > Edit Appointments > Results
  • EHS Manager > Medical Chart > Results Tab and Open Orders
  • Case Management Portal > Case Folder > Medical Chart Tab
  • Client Admin > Merge Duplicates > Results Tab
  • Progress Note > Create Letter > Results Tab
  • Reviewer Portal > Medical Chart > Results Tab and Open Orders
  • Reviewer Portal > Edit Appointments > Results
