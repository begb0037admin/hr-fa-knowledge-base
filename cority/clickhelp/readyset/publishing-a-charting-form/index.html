Publishing a Charting Form

When viewing a charting form, you can publish the form from the configuration window. 

To publish a Charting Form:

  1. On the Charting From window, click Publish.
    If a warning prompt appears, click Continue. The Dynamic Test  Publish window appears.

  1. Click Publish.
    A prompt saying that the form has been published successfully appears.

  1. Click OK.
