Introduction

The following are important when working with Custom Surveys:

  • Custom Surveys can be used to create new surveys and edit existing ones—including adding, removing, and editing questions and answers.

  • Custom Surveys can be provided to Client Config users who have completed all Custom Surveys training modules on Cority Academy. It is best practice for Client Config users to also be knowledgeable Client Admin users.

  • Once added through a Support ticket, Custom Surveys can be accessed using the Client Config tab (next to Form Config).

  • It is best practice to edit an existing survey when the changes are small (so you do not impact the meaning or intent of questions) and you do not want to replace the survey in various other configurations.
