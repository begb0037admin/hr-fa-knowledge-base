Printing Blank Surveys

To print a blank survey there must be an Incomplete Status entered in the survey.

  1. Click on the EHS Manager tab.
  2. Click Participant Management.
  3. Search and select a desired employee.
  4. Click on View Surveys on the left side menu.
  5. Click on the desired survey from the list to open the survey on the right side of the screen.
  6. Click Print Form, located on the upper right corner of the open survey.
  7. Click Print Blank and continue with printer options to complete.

Printing Blank Charting Forms

To print a blank charting form, it must be an unanswered form.

  1. Click on b tab.
  2. Click on Participant Management option.
  3. Search and select a desired employee.
  4. Click Medical Chart on left side menu.
  5. Click on the Open Orders tab, located on the right side.
  6. Select the desired charting form from the list of open orders.
  7. If the desired form is not available, you must order the form. To order a form, do the following:
    1. Click on Order Tests on the left menu.
    2. Click the Protocol drop down and click on the desired option.
    3. Click on the checkbox next to the form(s).
    4. Click the drop down for Location and select.  
    5. Click the drop down for Contact and select.
    6. Click Assign to Above Tests .
    7. Click Order Selected Tests .
      The ordered forms will be in the Open Orders tab.
  8. Click the drop down on the Print icon.
  9. Click Print Blank and continue with printer options to complete.
