Adding a Lookup Question to a Charting Form

To Add a Lookup Question to a Charting Form

  1. Right click the Group created under the New Form.
    A Menu appears.

  1. Click New Question.
    Another menu to the left appears.
  2. Click Lookup.
    The New Question window appears.

The Type field is automatically filled in as Lookup.

  1. Click the Text textbox, and type in a lookup question.
  2. Click he ORM dropdown textbox and select an option that relates to the lookup question.
  3. In the Header textbox, type in instructions that you want to show prior to a question.
  1. In the Footer textbox, type in what you want to show after a question.
  1. Click Save.
