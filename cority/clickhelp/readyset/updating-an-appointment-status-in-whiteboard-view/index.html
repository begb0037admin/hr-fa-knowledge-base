Updating an Appointment Status in Whiteboard View

To Update a Appointment Status in Whiteboard View

  1. Click the EHS Manager tab.
  2. On the secondary horizontal navigation bar, click Appointments.
  3. In the Search section, fill in fields as needed to view appointments either by Appointment Date, or Appointment Status.
    On the Appointment section to the right, a list of appointments will display.
  4. Click an Appointment.
    The Update Appointment window appears.
  5. Click the Status dropdown textbox and select a Status accordingly.
  6. Once completed, close the Update Appointment window. 
  7. Refresh the page and the Appointment Status Indicator icon will update and display to the right of the Appointment Status in the chart.
