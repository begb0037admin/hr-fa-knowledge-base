Searching for Tasks or Correspondence

There are two search options: Task and Correspondence.

Task Search

  1. Click the Task Search tab from the primary navigation menu.
  2. In the Search Type field on the left side of the screen, select Tasks.
  3. Search using any of the criteria listed but you must have at least one criterion.
    Some search fields may have a list of choices.  
  4. Click Search.
  5. In the search results, click the Edit icon to make changes to the task, or the Person icon to go into that participant’s record.
  6. Click the Edit icon  and make any of the following changes:
    Required fields are marked by an “*”.
    • Description – enter your desired information
    • Tasks* - click the drop-down and select a Task
    • Status* - click the drop-down and select Open, Closed or Complete
    • Follow-up Required* - click the drop-down and select Yes or No
    • Follow-up Date – Click the calendar picker and select the date or type in the date, mm/dd/yyyy
    • Correspondence Method - click the drop-down and select Phone, Mail or Email
    • Department - click the drop down and select the correct item
    • Assign To – click the drop down and select the person you are assigning this task.  
    • Notes – Enter free form information
  7. Click Save.

Correspondence Search

  1. Click the Task Search tab from the primary navigation menu.
  2. In the Search Type field on the left side of the screen, select "Correspondence".
  3. Search using any of the criteria listed but you must have at least one criterion. Some search fields may have a list of choices.  
  4. Click Search.
  5. In the search results, click the Edit icon to make changes to the task, or the Person icon to go into that participant’s record.
  6. Click the Edit icon. Required fields are marked by an “*”.
    • Status* - click the drop-down and select Open, Closed or Complete
    • Resolution - click the drop down and select Positive, Neutral or Negative
    • Follow-up Required* - click the drop-down and select Yes or No
    • Follow-up Date – Click the calendar picker and select the date or type in the date, mm/dd/yyyy
    • Method* - click the drop-down and select Phone, Mail or Email
    • Description* – Click on drop down and select the item that relates to your correspondence.
    • Assign To – click the drop down and select the person you are assigning this task. Not all EHS clinicians will have permission to assign tasks.
    • Department – click the drop down and select the correct item

This dialog box allows attaching documents.

     7. Click Save.
