Survey Levels

A survey consists of three main levels:

  1. Question Group:  A grouping of one or more questions
  2. Questions:  The question(s) to which the participant(s)/employee(s) are responding, including any headers and/or footers
  3. Answers:  The options the participant(s)/employee(s) can choose from when answering the questions

Show Level

You can choose the level(s) of the survey you would like to see while configuring by clicking Show Level and selecting the groups, the questions, and/or the answers. Each level shows the level(s) above it in the hierarchy. Use the arrows at the left for expanding / collapsing.

Level Properties

Each level of the survey, including the survey itself, has properties. To view a level's properties, right-click the level and click Properties.

Re-arranging Groups, Questions, and/or Answers

You can arrange groups, questions, and answers within a survey by dragging and dropping into new locations.

  • You can move a question to a different position within a group.
  • You can move a group higher or lower within the survey.
  • You can move a radio button answer higher or lower within a question.
  • You cannot move an answer from one question to another.

Considerations for Re-arranging

  • Be aware of actions that have been configured in answer properties. An action could open a group based on the selection of a particular answer. It could be quite confusing to a participant if the group that opens is above, instead of below, that answer.
  • 'Question in Title' groups are indicated with the letters "QT" in green next to the group title. When re-arranging questions within a group or moving a question to a different group, please be aware that only the first question in a QT group can have an answer configured to re-open that same group.
  • You will receive an error message if you try to make any change that is not acceptable.
