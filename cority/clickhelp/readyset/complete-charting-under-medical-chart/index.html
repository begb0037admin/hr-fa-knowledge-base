Complete Charting under Medical Chart

When a patient comes in for an appointment, charting form data will need to be added to their record. 

  1. From the Home/Calendar view, select the participant or use the Participant Management tab and use the search options to find and open the participant record. 
    Either option will open to the Medical Chart.  
  2. Select Open Orders from either region.
    This will display the charting/test forms listed by date.
  3. Select the Charting Form to open.
  4. To view the survey details or medical considerations select the Surveys tab on the opposite pane. 
    This displays the Survey List.
    1. Select the individual surveys to view complete details.
    2. Click View Summary in the upper right corner to display all completed surveys with the Medical Considerations.
  5. Enter the charting information.
  6. If Compliance Orders are enabled, and the form is associated with a Compliance Program, enter the compliance order information.
  7. When finished, click Submit Final.
    The form will move to the Results tab.
  8. If you click Save Draft, the chart will remain in the Open Orders section for you to complete and submit.

Automated Compliance Orders

For clients that have compliance orders enabled, they can be configured to automatically populate answers from charting forms to associated compliance orders.

To enable Compliance Orders, please contact support.

If enabled and configured, when charting, the Pull Mapped Data option in the Compliance Orders window will be checked by default. This indicates that the compliance order will be automatically filled based on the answers recorded in the charting form.
