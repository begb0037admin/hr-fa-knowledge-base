Complete the Participant’s Seasonal Flu Vaccination Record through Event Manager

If you have never had an Event, contact your Account Executive for the correct procedures needed prior to setting it up.

  1. Log in with the login credentials you have been provided.
    The login will be different from your EHS Manager login.
  2. Click the Event Manager tab on the primary navigation menu
  3. Use the left navigation to search for the participant and either scan their badge number or enter their name or badge number and click Search.
    Their Seasonal Flu Vaccination Record will be displayed.
  4. Select the record by double-clicking the test.
    The charting form will be on the left and you will see the following tabs on the right:
    • Considerations – Participants self-reported issues
    • Survey History – Full survey information
    • Test Results – Access to test result history that relates to this test
    • Tasks – Ability to create a task if needed
  5. Review the Medical Considerations.
  6. Review the full survey, if desired.
  7. Apply the Flu Vaccination and chart the information in the form on the left.
  8. When the charting is complete, click Submit Final.
  9. Repeat for subsequent participants.
