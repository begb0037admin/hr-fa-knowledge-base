Updating an Appointment Status in Calendar View

On the Calendar view, when an Appointment Status is updated that has an associated Appointment Status Indicator, the Appointment Status Indicator icon updates accordingly .

Once the Appointment Status is updated, the Calendar view must be refreshed to update the Appointment Status indicator displayed. 

To Update an Appointment Status in Calendar View

  1. Click EHS Manager tab.
  2. On the secondary horizontal navigation bar, click Schedule.
  3. In the Schedule drop down textbox, select a Clinic.
    All employee-patient schedules will appear. The Appointment Status Indicator for an Appointment Status displays to right of the Appointment name.
  4. To update the Appointment Status, click the Appointment.
    The Update Appointment window appears.
  5. Click the Status dropdown textbox and select a Status accordingly.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Refresh the page and the Appointment Status Indicator will update and display to the right of the Appointment name.

Appointment Statuses can be viewed in any Split Day, whether it is Day, Week, or Month.
