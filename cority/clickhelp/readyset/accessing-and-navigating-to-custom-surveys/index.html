Accessing and Navigating to Custom Surveys

All Custom Surveys training course videos provided by Cority must be completed by all Client Config users in your organization prior to requesting access.

You can find the Custom Surveys training course on Cority Academy. LEARN MORE

  1. Once everyone who has a Client Config user role has completed the training, submit a Support (Helpdesk) ticket.
  2. Once the ticket is closed and you have been granted access, log in and access your Client Config tab.
  3. Click the Survey Config tab to begin.
