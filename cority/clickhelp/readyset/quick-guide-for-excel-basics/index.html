Quick Guide for Excel Basics

You can use these quick basic steps for setting up your Excel spreadsheet views. Remember that you may not have the same Excel version/product.

Add Filters

Filters allow you to drill down and get very specific information.

  1. Highlight the entire worksheet by clicking on the upper left hand box (above Row 1 and left of column A)
  2. Click on Sort/Filter drop down
  3. Click Filters to activate

AutoFit Columns

This resizes all columns' width based on the widest data in the column

  1. Highlight the entire worksheet by clicking on the upper left hand box (above Row 1 and left of column A)
  2. Hover over the line between column A and column B (you will see the cursor change) and Double Click
  3. Select AutoFit Column Width

Freeze Panes

This process will “freeze” the top row(s) allowing you to see the column headers

  1. Highlight Row 1, click the View tab
  2. Click on the Freeze Panes drop down
  3. Select Freeze Top Row

Hide Columns

This process will “hide” but not delete columns in your spreadsheet allowing you to bring the column back when needed

  1. Select the columns you want to “Hide”
  2. Right-click on column and click “Hide”
