Linking Answers to Open Another Group of Questions

To Link an Answer to Open Another Group of Questions

  1. In the form, right click an answer.
    A menu appears.

  1. Click Properties.
  1. Click the Actions tab.
  2. Click the checkbox in front of a Group that you want to link to the question.
  3. Click Save.
    The Question Properties window closes.
  4. To hide a group of questions so that they appear on the active form only when the answer linked to the group is selected, right click the linked Group of questions, a menu appears.
  5.  Click Properties.

The Group Properties window opens.

  1. Click the Hidden checkbox.
  2. Click Save.
    In View test when the form viewed, after the First Answer in Group 2 is selected then the questions in Group 3 will populate.
