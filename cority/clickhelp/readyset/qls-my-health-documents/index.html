My Health Document Management

Users can manage their own documents from their My Health Portal. 

Add documents to My Health 

  1. As a participant, log in to your My Health Account.
  2. Click the My Health tab.
  3. Click Documents.
  4. Click Upload Document.

  5. Click Browse to search for your document or drag and drop the document into the upload window.
  6. Select the Document Type. 
  7. Enter a descriptive name for the file (e.g. Sam’s Immunization Record).
  8. Optionally, enter an Upload Comment.

  9. Click Upload.

Manage documents

Once a document is uploaded, you can do any of the following:

  • To delete a document, select the checkbox next to the document and click Delete Selected.
  • To view the document, click the View   icon.
  • To view document details, click the Edit  icon. Document details cannot be edited.
