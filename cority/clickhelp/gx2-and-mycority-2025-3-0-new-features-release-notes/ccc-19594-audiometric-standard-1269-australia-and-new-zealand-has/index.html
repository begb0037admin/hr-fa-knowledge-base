CCC-19594: Audiometric standard 1269 (Australia and New Zealand) has been updated

Opt-In

GX2 can now automatically calculate the new required thresholds to help organizations for which they are applicable remain compliant with Australia and New Zealand Audiometric regulations.

To support this, in the Audiometric module, new fields can now be added to custom versions of the following layouts:

  • ML-003165
  • ML-001299
  • ML-000503
  • ML-000502
  • ML-000484
  • ML-000240

Those fields are:

  • L3000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • L4000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • L6000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • R3000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • R4000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • R6000 - Retest - numeric integer field (accepts values -10 to 110, in increments of 5)
  • Average Threshold Left - calculated field (average of thresholds at the frequencies 3, 4 and 6 kHz left)
  • Average Threshold Right - calculated field (average of thresholds at the frequencies 3, 4 and 6 kHz right)
  • Mean Threshold - L3000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 3000Hz)
  • Mean Threshold - L4000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 4000Hz)
  • Mean Threshold  - L6000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 6000Hz)
  • Mean Threshold - R3000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 3000Hz)
  • Mean Threshold - R4000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 4000Hz)
  • Mean Threshold - R6000 - calculated field (average of two tests across 6000Hz)

The equivalent columns can be added to custom versions of Audiometric list views.

The new fields can be prohibited.

The new Retest fields are available in Query Builder and can be populated or updated using the import.
