Summary of Safety and Environmental Enhancements

The table below contains design improvements we have made in the Safety and Environmental clouds. The items are organized by component, and the components are arranged in alphabetical order. Descriptions of any larger features follow the table.

ItemComponent(s)Description
CCC-18982Incident and Event Reporting

The following new fields can now be added to custom versions of Safety and Environmental 'Incident' and 'Event Report' layouts, and to Safety 'Injury/Illness' and 'Case' layouts:

  • 10 new alphanumeric (nvarchar, 1000 characters long) UDFs, numbered 25 through 34
  • 10 new Date UDFs, numbered 14 through 23
  • 10 new Time UDFs, numbered 5 through 14

For each new field, the equivalent column can be added to applicable custom list views.

The fields can be used in business rules, display rules, and workflows, and can be reported on using Query Builder and Report Writer.

Each of the new fields can be prohibited from user roles.

Values for the fields can be added or updated through import (Import Utility or REST API).

 

CCC-16393Risk Assessments

Additional sublists are now available for Risk Assessments in myCority.

 

For Safety, the following sublists can now be added to custom Risk Assessment layouts:

  • Notes
  • Interviewed Employees
  • Share with Group
    • Groups can only be created in GX2 and can be viewed in the sublist in myCority.
  • Letters

Letters can only be created in GX2 and can be viewed in the sublist in myCority.
