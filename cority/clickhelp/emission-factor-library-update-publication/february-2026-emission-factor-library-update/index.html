February 2026

CEA 2026 - v21.0 (India) 

New emission factor values from the Central Energy Authority (CEA) are available, with applicable dates from April 1st 2024.  As with version 20 (CEA 2025), Cority applies the Weighted Average Grid Emission Rate (including renewable energy sources).

SEAI 2025 (Ireland) 

New electricity and fuel values from the Energy in Ireland – 2025 Report published by the Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland are available. These are based on 2024 emissions data. No significant methodological changes have been noted.

USEEIO v1.4.0 – Supply Chain Spend GHG Factors (US)

The latest version of the USEEIO spend-based factors has been added to the database. This methodology is no longer published by the EPA and is now maintained under the Cornerstone Sustainability Data Initiative. Consequently, this methodology is now labelled USEEIO 2025 within our system. Existing data sources do not need to be amended; the label change will be visible in the audit dashboard exports and reports. However, when using Emission Factor Source customization for specific data sources, USEEIO 2025 should be selected.

A key change in this release is the transition from IPCC AR5 to IPCC AR6 factors, replacing those used in v1.3. A detailed breakdown of the relative changes between versions v1.3 and v1.4 is available in the supporting documents. These factors are based on 2023 GHG emissions data and are therefore applicable from January 1, 2023, following the logic used in previous releases.

As communicated in September 2025, Cority  will continue to support NAICS categories moving forward. USEEIO reference categories included up to v1.3 will no longer be updated.
