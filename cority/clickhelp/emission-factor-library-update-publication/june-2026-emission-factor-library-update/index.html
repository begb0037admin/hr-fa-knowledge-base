June 2026 Update

The following updates to the CGGI (Canada), eGrid Electricity (formerly EPA) (US), and Fuel Conversions within the Sustainability Performance Management solution are available as of June 2026.

The new factors are not automatically applied to data that was previously uploaded to the system. Existing data files must be reprocessed to apply the updated emission factors. For support on managing this process or any other questions, please contact Cority Support.

CGGI 2026 (Canada) 

CGGI (Canada) emission factors have been updated based on the National Inventory Report 1990 – 2024: Greenhouse Gas Sources and Sinks in Canada (published 2026), effective Jan 1, 2024. Prince Edward Island (PEI) now has its own Consumption Mix for the first time — previously only a Distribution mix was published. The PEI Consumption Mix factor is based on 29.8% own production and 70.2% imports from New Brunswick. As a result, the 2024 Scope 2 value for PEI rises to 0.255 kgCO2e/kWh, up from 0.0070 kgCO2e/kWh under the 2023 Distribution mix. Customers previously using New Brunswick as a proxy for PEI should switch to this more accurate PEI Consumption Mix value. 

We have also adjusted Consumption by Fuel factors tied to emissions control technology for off-road diesel vehicles and equipment, and for vehicle types where “Unknown” is selected for Vehicle Model Year. The impact is minor for vehicles (less than 5% of total CO₂eq./litre of fuel), but diesel and biodiesel equipment factors decrease by 10–14%. 

eGrid2024 (2026) (USA) 

The EPA has not published eGrid factors for 2024. Using the underlying EPA model, the Cornerstone Sustainability Data Initiative has derived these factors and made them available as eGrid2024. For consistency with previous versions, they remain grouped under the EPA methodology in our system, with Publication listed as EPA 2026 (eGrid 2024).  

A key change in this release is the transition from IPCC AR5 to IPCC AR6 global warming potentials, matching the approach Cornerstone has used in its calculations. 

Green-e residual mix factors for 2025 (2023 data), published by CRS are also included in this release. The 2025 residual mixes replace the current 2024 (2022 data) mixes in last year's eGrid 2025 data and are also applied to the new eGrid 2026 data. eGrid 2026 will be updated with new residual factors when they are released by CRS in 2027. 

Fuel Conversions 

The Fuel Conversion table has been updated to include Defra, NGA, and SEAI through 2025, and GHG Protocol v2.0. We've also improved proxies used where the source methodology provides no unit conversion for that combination (e.g., kWh/tonne is available but litre/tonne is not). The most significant number of proxy changes affect biodiesels (where Biodiesel methyl ester is now favored) and waste/secondary gases used as fuels. Conversions have also been added for newly introduced fuels and for fuels that previously lacked them.
