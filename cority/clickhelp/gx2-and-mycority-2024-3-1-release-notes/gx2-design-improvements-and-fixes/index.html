CCC-13499
Inspections, Audits, Observations, Surveys
N/A

For inspections, audits, observations, and surveys, there is a new 'Every Number of Days' recurring frequency. The new frequency has two additional fields for configuration: 'Number of Days' and 'Next Due Based Off Completion".

This will provide more recurrence flexibility, particularly for inspections that must be conducted no later than 'N' days after the completion date of the previous instance.
