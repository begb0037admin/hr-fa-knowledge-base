Summary of Business Intelligence Enhancements

The table below contains a design improvement made within Business Intelligence.

ItemComponent(s)Description
CCC-20992Query Builder / Report Writer

Query Builder and Report Writer now provide the ‘Last 12 Months’ option, which allows organizations to view data and track and analyze trends for any metric over the most recent continuous 12-month period.

‘Last 12 Months’ is a dynamic time period that always includes the most recent 12 full months. It counts backward from the current date to the beginning of the first month in the range.
For example, ‘Last 12 Months’ selected on November 15, 2025 includes the data from November 1, 2024 to November 14, 2025. ‘Last 12 Months’ selected on May 1, 2025 includes the data from May 1, 2024 to April 30, 2025.

The filter recalculates dynamically based on the current system date.

Returned results respect the user’s configured date format (in the 'Short Date Style' system setting).
