Transferring Test Records from the Device into Cority

Once the Device Import Utilities settings are entered and a device is selected, test results from the device can be transferred into Cority.

Before transferring the results, ensure that the device is turned on and connected to your computer.

To facilitate the data transfer, select the appropriate option.

Follow the on-screen red text for each individual device to transfer the data. If you need assistance, contact your Cority Administrator.

The results will be transferred, and the transfer status can be viewed in the status box.

Note: To create a Transfer Log file on your computer, select the Create Transfer Log checkbox while configuring your transfer. The log file will be stored at the root of your C drive.
