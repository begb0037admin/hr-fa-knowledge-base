CCC-15495: For ePrescribing, the drug database has been replaced

Mandatory

The new database is provided by FDB. It retains the ease of use, accuracy, and time, error, and risk reduction of the previous database.

Its benefits include:

  • One-stop simple access to an expansive, reputable repository of deeply researched, peer-reviewed, verified, and continually updated medication information.
  • The ability to quickly and easily identify the drug(s) most appropriate for each employee’s unique need(s).
  • For accurate and quick decision-making, insights about side effects, pediatric / geriatric / pregnancy / lactation precautions, and interaction (drug, food, allergy, and condition) checking, as well as counseling messages and patient information pamphlets.
  • Seamless integration with our existing ePrescription workflows for creating, editing, and electronically submitting digital prescriptions and refill requests.

The existing ePrescription workflows (creating, editing, and cancelling ePrescriptions, printing ePrescriptions and prescription instructions, signing ePrescriptions for controlled substances, audit reporting, etc.) remain essentially the same.
