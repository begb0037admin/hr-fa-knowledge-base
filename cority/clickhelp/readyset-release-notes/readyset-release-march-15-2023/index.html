ReadySet Release – March 15, 2023

Deployment for clients to Test (March 1st):

Cority ReadySet will soon support custom reporting, allowing you to build unique reports that are important to your employee health clinic. This feature will provide the tools needed to keep up to date with the status of ongoing employee health initiatives, helping you analyze employee health data effectively and make informed business decisions to ensure employee health programs are on the right track.  

Easy-to-access, self-serve tools will soon be available for defining the metrics you want to leverage to build your report(s). All ReadySet charting form questions will be reportable, ensuring you can gather and compare data results that are not covered in your standard report offering. Also, this feature will help you avoid manually collecting data and preparing and crunching numbers, ensuring fast, seamless, and error-proof reporting in just a few clicks.
