ReadySet Release – August 13, 2025

Deployment for clients to Test - July 30th 2025

EHS-9099 For Compliance v2, Client Configuration users can now request field mapping to automatically populate answers from charting forms to associated compliance orders.
When mapped, in EHS Manager, under Participant Management, when viewing Compliance Orders, there is now a 'Pull Mapped Data' option. This option will be checked by default if a compliance order has been mapped to the form. When checked, the compliance orders will be automatically filled based on the answers in the charting form.

EHS-10667 For Vaccination and Surveillance Records, Hepatitis A Immunization charting forms now correctly show on the PDF output.

EHS-10905 For Vaccination and Surveillance Records, IGRA tests now correctly show on the PDF  output.

EHS-10906 OSHA CSV reports no longer include quotations around NAICS and EIN codes.

EHS-10944 For Custom Reports, reports can now be generated with date filters applied to both the Charting Form and Survey criteria.
