PartOfBody

This table lists parts of the body injured including the category (e.g. head:mouth, head:teeth, lower ext:leg, lower ext:hip).

Identify the exact part of the body in the PartofBodyReference field. You can either click in the field or use the look-up selector to location the text entry, or click  to select from an image of the body (double-click on the appropriate part on the image). The PartofBodyReferenceImage reflects the selection. This image may be displayed in custom incident and injury layouts.
