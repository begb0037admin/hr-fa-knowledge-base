About the Quality Cloud

The Quality Cloud provides a consolidated location where Quality managers can efficiently record, access and comprehensively manage all of their quality data.

 

This chapter explains how to:

  • manage nonconformance issues (see Managing Nonconformances)

  • record and resolve customer complaints (see Managing Customer Complaints)

  • manage and track information about your organization’s critical suppliers or customers (see Managing Suppliers and Customers).

  • define product specifications, create inspection plans, and capture the results of those inspections (see Managing Inspection Data)

  • record process and design FMEAs (see Recording Failure Mode and Effects Analyses (FMEAs))

  • approve/reject/publish a controlled document (see Viewing or Editing a Controlled Document)

  • view published control documents (see Viewing Published Controlled Documents)

To see the look-up tables used by this module, see Look-up Tables Used by This Module. There may also be system settings that affect your use of this module; for more information, see Changing Your System Settings.
