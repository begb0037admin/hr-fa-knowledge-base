Findings and Actions Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Environmental Actions Report

List of actions from the Environmental Cloud based on due date or date of completion.

Ergonomic Actions Report

List of actions from the Ergonomic Cloud based on due date or date of completion.

IH Actions Report

List of actions from the IH Cloud based on due date or date of completion.

Safety Actions Report

List of actions from the Safety Cloud based on due date or date of completion.

Safety Actions - Percent Completed Report

Record count totals and calculations from the Safety Cloud modules based on the Priority or Risk assigned to an action; can be sorted by source.
