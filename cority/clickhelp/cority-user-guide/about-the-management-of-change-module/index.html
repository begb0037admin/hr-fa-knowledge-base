About the Management of Change Module

The Management of Change module is available in the Environmental, Safety and Quality Clouds. Use this module to manage the process of bringing planned change to an organization. The Change Requests module will help your organization to:

  • Plan for changes to your Environmental, Safety and Quality programs.

  • Automate the paperwork processes/workflows required to roll out the changes.

  • Manage and maintain records related to the changes (i.e. document the changes and related activities).

  • Monitor and evaluate the progress and impact of the changes.

  • Improve your change processes.

 

This chapter explains the change workflow (see How are Change Requests Completed and Approved?), and how to:

  • submit a change request (see Creating a Change Request)

  • approve a change request (see Reviewing Change Requests).

To see the look-up tables used by this module, see Look-up Tables Used by This Module. There may also be system settings that affect your use of this module; for more information, see Changing Your System Settings.
