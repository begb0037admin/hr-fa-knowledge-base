Disposing of a Chemical in Inventory

When you dispose of a chemical, a Waste Management record is created to track the disposition.

  1. In the inventory record, choose Actions»Dispose Chemical.

  2. Enter the Amount you are disposing and select the Unit of Measure, then click OK.

The Chemical Inventory record stays open. A new Waste Management record is created to track the disposition, using the Chemical Product name as the Waste Stream name. For more information, see Waste Management.

To add one or more chemicals to a waste container, select the records in the Chemical Inventory list view, then choose Actions»Add Chemical to Waste Container.
