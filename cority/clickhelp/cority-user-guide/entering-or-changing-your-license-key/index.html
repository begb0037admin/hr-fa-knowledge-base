Entering or Changing Your License Key

If your license key was not entered during implementation, or if your license has changed (e.g. to add more users), you can enter or update your license key through the interface as follows:

  1. In the Administrator menu, click Change License Key.

  2. Enter the license key as provided to you by Cority Inc., then click OK.

  3. If you changed an existing license key, you should refresh the key: open the System Settings, then choose Actions»Refresh License Key from Database.

Your license controls how many users can be assigned to a Cloud or functional area; for example, your license may allow you to assign five users to the Case Management module.

  • To see the limits associated with your current license, open the System Settings and then choose Actions»License Key User Count Limits.

  • To see the active users for each type, create a Report Writer query.
