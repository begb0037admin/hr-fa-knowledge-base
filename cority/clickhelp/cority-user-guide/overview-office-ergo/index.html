Overview

The Office Ergonomics module helps you to understand and mitigate employee health-related risks more effectively as well as better manage cases that arise.

Using the self-guided training and assessments in myCority, employees can take control of their own health and prevent repetitive strain injuries, musculoskeletal disorders, and other workplace injuries from happening. Comprehensive office ergonomics training gives employees the knowledge they need to prevent discomfort and injury while assessment tools allow employees to quickly identify potential ergonomic risks in their work area.

Ergonomists review the self-assessment results in GX2 to monitor the overall risk calculations for individual employees for different risk types, and identify issues that require their assistance to resolve.

GX2 Functions You May Need to Use

Sending Emails

  • Ad hoc (one-time) emails:
    • Send an email to a selected employee in the Manage Employee Risk list view.
    • Send an email to a selected employee in the Training>Online Sessions list.
  • Scheduled emails, e.g. sending a progress report to all supervisors on the first Monday of each month or sending weekly reminders for managers to check-in with employees.
  • Triggered emails, sent as a result of something else happening in the system, e.g. an employee’s risk changing from “low” to “moderate” or a new employee entering a group and receiving an invite email to take the Training and Assessment

See Sending and Viewing Cority Email Messages.

Emails for training assignments can be sent using templates. To create a template, create an email action business rule action.

Business Rules and Actions

Business Rules and Actions are “if-then” statements that can be applied to the Cority system to cause an action to occur based on a trigger. Examples: Employee risk increased, so send an email; or a new employee was added to a group, so automatically invite them to take the self-assessment. Think of the Business Rule as the “if” and the Business Rule Action as the “then”. More than one action can be assigned to the same Business Rule.
See Setting Up Business Rules and Setting Up Business Rule Actions.

Linked Queries

A linked query is a query created in Query Builder that can be used to automatically determine enrollment in a group. To use this feature, simply create a query in Query Builder that uses the field “Employee Number” as the first column. This query will automatically update based on the most recent information in the system. An example of this would be creating a list of employees who work in a specific department. If we use linked queries, we can have a list that updates automatically when employee HR data changes. Another example might be to create a group of people who have completed the training and assessment. You could create this group using a linked query and it would update automatically as employees complete the training.

See Editing or Creating an Ad Hoc Report in Query Builder.

Groups and Teams

Groups or teams of employees can be assigned to an action so that any member can complete the action. A group/team may be populated either manually by identifying the associated employees, or automatically based on the results of one or more linked queries defined in the Query Builder. See the GroupandTeam look-up table. 

Assigning Online Training Sessions

Online training can be assigned as a single ad hoc training session for one or more users, or scheduled automatically for specific groups.

See Managing Online Training Courses.
