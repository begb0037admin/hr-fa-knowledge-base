Drug Testing Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Drug / Alcohol Test Results

List of employee drug test results within a selected date range. A specific date is required on the selection criteria page.

Drug DOT Report

United State's Department of Transportation Drug Testing reports.

Drug Pool Employees

Detailed list of drug pools and their associated employee members, or a summary list of drug pools displaying a count of the number of employee members.

Drug Results: Sample Information

List of employee's Drug and Alcohol test samples with or without test results.

Drug Testing Employee Letters

Print a batch of employee letters without having to open each employee’s record; option to save letters and change Employee Letter Generated flag to Yes in each record.

Drug Tests Report (Without Results Entered)

Drug tests without any matching result records.

Individual Drug Test Results

Drug testing results for a selected employee, or grouped by employee when multiple employees are selected.

Summary Drug Test Results

Summary statistics about drug and alcohol tests during a selected year or quarter.
