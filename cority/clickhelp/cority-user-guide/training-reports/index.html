Training Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Courses Associated With Employees

List of Exposure Group training courses an employee has been associated with.

Courses Associated With Requirements

List of requirements and their associated courses. Also lists all Exposure Groups with the recall frequency for the courses joined by schedule activity codes.

Detailed Forecast Course/Student

Detailed training forecast by course and student, or student and course, grouped by none, department or supervisor.

Detailed Schedule By Course/Student

Detailed training schedule by course and student, or student and course.

Employee Course Attended

Attendees/employees per training session.

Employee Training History

Transcript of training courses an employee has completed.

Summary Forecast, Student/Session Totals, Due Dates

Number of employees scheduled to take a particular course for each month in a 12 month period.

Training Certificate Letter

Training course's details, linked to a Certificate (which is SEG) via schedule activities, presented in form of a letter as a PDF.

Training Certificates

Training course's details, linked to a Certificate (which is SEG) via schedule activities.

Training Class Roster

List of attendees/employees, with option for employee signature per education session. The list may be sorted by employee name, department, trainer, or supervisor; if you choose department or supervisor, each starts on a new page. You may exclude any attendees who have been marked as Attended. A system setting allows you to exclude the Employee Number from the roster, for example if they are private such as SIN/SSN.

Training Compliance Status by Course and Department

Detailed training compliance status by course or department. The option to run by course can be grouped by none, department or supervisor.

Training Profile

List of Exposure Groups associated with courses.

Training Recalls

List of Exposure Group training courses an employee is being recalled for.

Training Session Schedule By Date and Course/Student

Detailed training schedule by date and course, or date and student, grouped by none, department or supervisor.

Training recall dates in these reports are based on the Attended Date in the training record; if the Attended Date was not populated, the Course Date will be used in the calculation of the recall date.
