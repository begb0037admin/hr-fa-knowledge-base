Scheduling Clinic Visit Activities

You can schedule activities related to clinic visits such as making telephone calls, issuing recall letters, training, or attending a medical appointment.

To view a history of scheduled activities for the selected employee, choose Actions»Go to Employee’s Schedule. The Appointments module opens in Employee View. For more information, see Viewing Employee Activities and Recalls.
