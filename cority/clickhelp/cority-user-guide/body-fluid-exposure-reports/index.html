Body Fluid Exposure Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

BFE and OSHA Recordable Counts

Count of BFE record counts and OSHA Recordable counts.

Body Fluid Exposure Questionnaire Out of Range Responses

Numeric questions and employee responses where those responses fall outside the low and high range of acceptable responses in the Body Fluid Exposure module.

Body Fluid Exposure Questionnaire Response Counts

Textual count and percentage report of user responses to questions in questionnaires in the Body Fluid Exposure module. Graphs of responses can also be generated.

Body Fluid Exposure Questionnaire Responses

Questions and user responses to questionnaires in the Body Fluid Exposure module.
