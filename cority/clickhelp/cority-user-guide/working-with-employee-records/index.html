Working with Employee Records

You will only be able to see records whose Health Center you have security access to, or records which do not have a Health Center recorded at all, unless this security is turned off in the system settings.
Exception: Even if you do not have site security access to an employee’s health center, if they are within your GDDLOFB you can view/add/edit records on the “SEGs Related To Individual Employee” list on the Surveillance tab of their record.
