About the Medical Chart and Correspondence Modules

The Medical Chart module provides a central location to view various medical records entered for an employee in other modules.

The Medical Correspondence module provides a central location to view all of an employee's medical correspondence, and for a practitioner to easily email one or more of the medical correspondence items.

This chapter explains how to view different types of information in an employee’s Medical Chart. For information about the reports available for this module, see Medical Chart Reports.
