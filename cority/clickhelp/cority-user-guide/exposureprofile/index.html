ExposureProfile

This table lists profiles, which are summary judgments of whether a risk posed by an exposure is acceptable. This may include risk of adverse health effects, legal risks, community concerns and regulatory requirements. For example:

  • Unacceptable/Critical - implement health hazard controls

  • Uncertain Exposures - collecting additional information

  • Acceptable Exposures - no action or define a routine monitoring program

It also identifies which risk assessment types the profile applies to.
