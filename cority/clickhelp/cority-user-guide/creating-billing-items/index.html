Creating Billing Items

Billing items are typically entered against a clinic visit record, but it is also possible to create a non-visit billing item, such as a service charge or administration fee.

Creating a Billing Item from a Clinic Visit Record

You can automatically create billing items for scheduled exam activites as follows:
- Associate one or more fee schedules with a schedule activity (the Fee Schedule tab must be added to a custom layout of the Schedule Activity look-up table).
- In the Exam Activity tab of a clinic visit record, choose Actions»Add Selected Exam Activities to Billing. The exam activities will be added to the Billing Items tab . If an activity has more than one fee schedule, a billing item will be created for each activity / fee schedule combination.

The following procedure may be completed entirely by the clinician, or it may be initiated by the clinician and finalized by a separate coder.

  1. On the Clinic Visit record, navigate to the Billing tab and click New. This opens a Clinic Visit "Billing Item", which serves as a bill.
  2. Select the Fee Schedule. You will then be prompted to select the Payer Type and Payer. The fee Cost is based on these choices.
  3. Enter the number of Units.
  4. Optionally select a Modifier add-on (e.g. tax, surcharge, etc.). The Total Cost = Fee Cost * Units * Modifier.
  5. Optionally enter any Discount. The Adjusted amount = Total - Discount Total .
    The Outstanding amount initially equals the Adjusted amount, i.e. the total amount to be invoiced. This field will later update to reflect amounts received or written off. 
  6. When the item is ready to be billed, select the Coding Complete checkbox. The item is moved to the Uninvoiced Items module in the Billing solution.

Creating a Non-Visit Billing Item

When reviewing billing items, a biller may create a new billing item that is not related to a specific clinic visit record. In the Billing menu, click Uninvoiced Items, then click New.

The fields are the same as above, and when marked complete, the item is added to the list of uninvoiced items.
