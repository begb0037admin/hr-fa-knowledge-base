Viewing or Editing a Controlled Document

  1. In the My Favorites menu, click Document Control and optionally use the fields to filter the list.

  2. Click a link to edit, or click New.

  3. On the Document Control tab, record information to categorize the document (e.g. Library, Description, Owner, Type) and to reflect the controls (e.g. Status, Review Frequency), and to indicate if the document is saved locally or is available on a remote site.

  4. On the Supporting Documents tab, add any additional supporting document(s) that are part of this controlled document.

  5. Click Save.

  6. To start the review process, choose Actions»Begin Review.

  7. In the Approval History form

    1. Record the Reason for Revision.
    2. If the Document Location (on the Document Control tab) is "Local", locate the “main” document in the Import File field; if the Document Location is "Remote", enter the Doc HTTP Link.
    3. Click Save.

  8. An email is sent to the person identified as the first reviewer for the library associated with the controlled document (in the DocumentLibrary look-up table).

    9. Multiple users may be assigned to a workflow status. Each will receive an email notification informing them of the assignment and any of those users will be able to review, reject, approve, or publish (depending on the workflow status) the document. Once the document has been dealt with by one of its assigned users, the other assigned users will not be able to execute the option(s) for that status and the document will move to the next step/status in the workflow. However, if an assigned user rejects a document, the document owner will be notified of the rejection and any one of the users assigned to the workflow status will again be able to review, reject, approve, or publish (depending on the workflow status) the document.

  9. The reviewer may add Review Comments, and will select Approved or Rejected and click Save.

    • If they approved the document, an email is sent to the person identified as the next reviewer for the library.

    • If they rejected the document, the document's status reverts to its original status and an email is sent to the document owner.

    The Approval History tab displays a history of each revision made to a document. Each workflow email notification includes a link to the most recent Approval History record for the document.

  10. Upon final approval, the person with Publish authority will choose Actions»Publish Document, and the document will be listed in the Published Documents module (see Viewing Published Controlled Documents).

Unpublishing a Document

To unpublish a published document and return it to the review process, open the document in the Document Control module, then on the Approval History tab choose Actions»Unpublish Document.

The document is removed from the Published Documents module, an email is sent to the document owner, and the document’s status is returned to its previous state.
