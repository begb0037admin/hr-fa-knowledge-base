About Contractor Management

The Contractors and Projects modules enable Safety Cloud users to record, manage, and track information about their organization’s contractors and the projects that may be assigned to those contractors. Use these modules to review and approve potential contractors and their projects before they begin their work.

 

This chapter explains how to:

  • record the contractors who do work for your organization (see Managing Contractors)

  • record the projects in your organization that contractors may be working on (see Managing Projects)

See the look-up tables used by this module. There may also be system settings that affect your use of this module; for more information, see Changing Your System Settings.
