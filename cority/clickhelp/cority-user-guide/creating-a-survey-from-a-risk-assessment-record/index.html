Creating a Survey from a Risk Assessment Record

You can link a risk assessment to an existing survey by entering (or looking up) the survey number in the Assessment Details. If the survey does not already exist, create a new survey record from the information contained in a risk assessment.

Once a risk assessment has been linked to a survey, choose Actions»View Survey to open the survey.

To create a survey:

  1. Open a risk assessment record and choose Actions»Create Survey on the Assessment Details tab.

  2. Depending on your system settings, you may be prompted to enter the survey number.

  3. A new survey is created. Some fields are populated based on the risk assessment:

    Risk Assessment

    Survey

    Assessment Details: GDDLOFB information

    Survey tab

    Assessment Details: Material Involved

    Products tab

    Tasks-Hazards-Controls: Task

    Plan tab

    for agents not flagged as Noise in the AgentHazard look-up table:
    Tasks-Hazards-Controls: Agent

    Plan tab

  4. Complete the survey as described in Creating a Survey.
