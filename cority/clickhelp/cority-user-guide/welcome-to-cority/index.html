Welcome to Cority

Cority is the leading provider of EHSQ (Environmental Health Safety and Quality) software for global corporations. Designed by EHSQ professionals, Cority allows companies to streamline, standardize, and integrate organizational data within one innovative, user-friendly, and comprehensive application. The Cority platform spans the main functional areas in EHSQ: Occupational Health, Safety, Industrial Hygiene, Environmental, Quality and Ergonomics. Within each product Cloud are a number of modules for the collection, management and tracking of EHSQ data. Using our powerful Business Intelligence tools, you can analyze and report on data captured within any of the Cority platform functional areas or combine EHSQ data for a complete view of EHSQ performance.

The Cority software Clouds can be implemented as one integrated software package or as standalone products to suit your specific requirements. Deployed as an integrated, enterprise-wide solution, Cority empowers organizations to centralize, streamline and standardize EHSQ data management across their operations.

With Cority, organizations can not only manage EHSQ compliance and regulatory requirements, but can be proactive in driving and streamlining processes that enable them to improve EHSQ performance, increase productivity, and promote effective communication between departments. With its cutting-edge software and diverse customer list of international companies and government organizations from across all industries, Cority is recognized as the world leader in EHSQ software solutions.

Cority interfaces with several third party services; these are trademark of their respective companies.
