Audiometric Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Alberta - Abnormal Shifts and Audiograms

Utilizing the Alberta Audiometry Regulation, this report displays employees' Audiogram test results by Abnormal Audiogram, Abnormal Shift, or Abnormal Audiogram or Shift. The report can be filtered by membership in a Hearing Conservation SEG.

Audiometric Employee Letters

Print a batch of employee letters without having to open each employee’s record; option to save letters and change Employee Letter Generated flag to Yes in each record.

Audiometric Questionnaire Out of Range Responses

Displays numeric questions and employee responses where those responses fall outside the low and high range of acceptable responses in the Audiometric module.

Audiometric Questionnaire Response Counts

Textual count and percentage report of user responses to questions in Questionnaires in the Audiometric module. Graphs of responses can also be generated.

Audiometric Questionnaire Responses

Questions and user responses to questionnaires in the Audiometric module.

Audiometric Recalls

List of active employees who are being recalled for periodic or routine audiometric testing.

Audiometric Shift and Impairment

This report supports OSHA, UK, Alberta, Quebec and South Africa regulations. The sub-reports display employee test results by STS, OSHA Shifts, Binaural Impairment, .5K, 1K, 2K, 3K, 4K, 6K averages in ears greater than/equal to 25 dB, or Shifts greater than 5dB. The report can be filtered by membership in a Hearing Conservation SEG.

Employee Hearing Summary

History of an employee's Audiometric hearing tests and a graph of the most recent hearing test against the employee's baseline test, based on the Audiometric Regulation chosen in system settings.

Speech Frequency Hearing Analysis

Test results with a .5, 1K, 2K average over a specified threshold. Displays the .5, 1K, 2K average, asymmetry, STS and employee audiometric hearing test results.
