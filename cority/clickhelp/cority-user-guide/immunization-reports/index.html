Immunization Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Declined Immunizations

List of employees and the immunizations they have declined.

Employee Immunization History

Historical list of immunizations administered to employees.

Employee Immunizations By Vaccine

Employees who received a specific vaccine-lot number.

Immunization Counts

Count of administered immunizations.

Immunization Questionnaire Out of Range Responses

Numeric questions and employee responses where those responses fall outside the low and high range of acceptable responses in the Immunization module.

Immunization Questionnaire Responses

Questions and user responses to questionnaires in the Immunization module.

Immunization Questionnaire Responses Counts

Textual count and percentage report of user responses to questions in questionnaires in the Immunization module. Graphs of responses can also be generated.

Immunization Recall Letter

Letter depicting specifics about immunization recalls for one or more employees. Select one or more immunization/test to report on; if you do not specify at least one, the report will include all immunizations or tests that are due.

Immunization Recalls

List of active employees who are due to be recalled for, or are delinquent in receiving an immunization.

Immunization Series Status

List of active employees who are due to be recalled for, or are delinquent in receiving an immunization.
