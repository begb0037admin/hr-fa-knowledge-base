Understanding the Safety Culture Score

Cority calculates a Safety Culture Score that helps you to track the effectiveness of organization’s safety culture. This score is recalculated weekly based on the active measures in the SafetyCultureMeasure look-up table. These measures look at factors such as the average number of days to report an event, the percentage of incidents investigated, the average score of responses to the Safety Culture Questionnaire, etc.

To view the score, add the corresponding gadget to your Advanced dashboard. The Safety Culture Score is also reportable in the Query Builder.
