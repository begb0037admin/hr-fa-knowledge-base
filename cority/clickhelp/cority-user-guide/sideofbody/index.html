SideOfBody

This table lists sides of the body (e.g. anterior, left, posterior, right).

Optionally, select the Standardized Value (from the SideofBodyStandardizedValue look-up table) to categorize each record. This value will be used by the predictive Analytics module to compare your data to that of other organizations and determine your relative performance.
