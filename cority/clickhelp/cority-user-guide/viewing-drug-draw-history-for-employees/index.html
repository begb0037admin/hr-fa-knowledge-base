Viewing Drug Draw History for Employees

The Drug Draw Details form displays all employees selected in all drug draws. The list allows you to see how many times, when and through what methodology (forced by month or random) each employee was selected for drug and/or alcohol testing.

In the Occupational Health menu, click Drug Draw Details and use the fields at the top to filter by fields such as employee, pool, alcohol testing, methodology, status (e.g. pending, collected, excused, refused), or draw number.

You may also view an individual’s drug pool history in their demographic record if the Drug Pools Linked to Employee Job and Drug Pools Linked to Individual Employee tabs have been added to a custom Demographics layout.
