Changing or Cancelling an Appointment

To change an appointment’s date, time, or duration, do one of the following:

  • Drag the appointment block to a different time slot on the schedule (if changing the appointment date, be sure that you open a view that shows both dates).

  • Drag the bottom of an appointment block to shorten or extend its duration.

  • Open the appointment and change the Date, Time, and/or Duration as required.

To cancel an appointment, open the appointment and click Delete or choose Actions»Cancel Appointment/Recurrence.

In either case:

  • If the appointment is part of a series, you are asked if the change or cancellation should be applied to just the selected appointment entry or all future instances (any past instances of a series will be unaffected).

  • If an email notification was sent when the original appointment was created, you are asked if you want to send another email notification, to the same recipient(s), to update (or remove) the appointment(s) in their calendar (e.g. Outlook).

To cancel all appointments within a specific time period (if you have appropriate security permission), choose Actions»Cancel Multiple Appointments. You will be prompted to either cancel all future appointments or enter a date range, and optionally specify the scheduling user, activity, and/or health center as well.

Reassigning Appointments From One User to Another

If you need to reassign appointments to another user, either for a specific period or all future appointments starting today:

  1. On the Appointments tab, choose Actions»Reassign Multiple Appointments.

  2. Select the date and time range, or select the Reassign all future appointments checkbox.

  3. Select the Scheduling User whose appointments you are reassigning from, and the user you are reassigning them to.

  4. Click Save.

No email notification is sent to the new scheduling user.
