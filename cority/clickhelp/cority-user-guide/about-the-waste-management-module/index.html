About the Waste Management Module

The Waste Management module allows EHS Managers to maintain compliance with federal, state/provincial, and local waste management regulations and carry out internal waste management organization obligations by tracking waste management at all applicable facilities.

This module is designed to track waste, assign and track waste management compliance actions, manage and track rental containers, track waste at the container level, generate waste labels, and compile and print a Uniform Waste Manifest. Report Writer subqueries are available for association with custom waste-related metrics to analyze and report on waste management data.

Cority provides functionality to track service costs related to waste disposal and transportation processes. This requires the following custom layout changes:
- Vendor look-up table: add the Vendor Services tab to capture a vendor's services and rates; multiple rates may be captured for different effective periods
- Waste Manifest layout: add the Transportation Service Cost tab
- Waste layout: add the Disposal Service Cost tab
Please contact your system administrator for further information.

This chapter explains how to:

  • record waste (see Recording a Waste Record)

  • manage container inventory (see Managing and Disposing of Containers)

  • generate waste labels (see Generating Waste Labels)

  • track waste shipments (see Tracking Waste Shipments)

To see the look-up tables used by this module and reporting options, see Look-up Tables Used by This Module. There may also be system settings that affect your use of this module; for more information, see Changing Your System Settings.
