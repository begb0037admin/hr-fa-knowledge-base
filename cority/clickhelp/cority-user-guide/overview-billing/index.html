Billing

The Billing solution requires a license key. Please contact your Customer Success Representative if you require more information or assistance in setting up this solution.

The Billing solution provides a workflow for healthcare providers to bill for their services. For example, a hospital that is contracted by companies to provide medical services for their employees would have the GX2 system with the Billing solution and Clinic Visit module and manage pools of employees from the different companies.

The Billing solution is typically used by the following types of users:

  • GX2 Administrator - sets up user access by role; maintains fee schedules, payers, modifiers; creates letter templates that are used as invoices
  • Clinician - completes clinical documentation and creates billing items from the clinic visit
  • Coder - reviews billing items to add modifiers and/or discounts, completes coding to send the item to the biller to be invoiced
  • Biller - verifies billing items, generates invoice, track and manages invoice status and due dates
  • Employer - add/edit/update/import employees in the Demographics module, schedule employees for clinic visits, receives invoices, dispute invoices/line items.
