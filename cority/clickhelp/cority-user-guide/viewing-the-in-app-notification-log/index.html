Viewing the In-App Notification Log

Every notification sent by the Cority system to a user’s myCority app is stored in the In-App Notification log, viewable by any user with administrator privileges.

In the Administrator menu, click In-App Notification Log. Click a link to view details of a notification. You cannot resend a notification from the log.
