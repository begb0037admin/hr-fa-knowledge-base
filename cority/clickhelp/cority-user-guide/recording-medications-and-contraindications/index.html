Recording Medications and Contraindications

Cority provides ePrescription (eRx) functionality for Cority-hosted, U.S. clients. A licensed eRx user who is granted the eRx Prescriber role can generate and transmit new medication orders, receive refill requests from the pharmacy, and accept or deny refill requests, electronically from within Cority.

Cority uses the Surescripts clinical network to connect Cority users to pharmacies, and the FDB database to provide users with information on medications and medication interactions.

  • If you are an eRx user, refer to Recording Medications (eRx).

  • If you are dispensing medication locally, or if you are not using eRx to prescribe, refer to Recording Medications (non-eRx).

  • To view or record contraindications, see Recording a Contraindication.
