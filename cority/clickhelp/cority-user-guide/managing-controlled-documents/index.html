Managing Controlled Documents

The Document Control module allows document managers to define a document review workflow and approval hierarchy to store, manage, and share controlled documents. The resulting published controlled documents ensure your organization is aware of and follows the latest documented policies and procedures. Word, Excel, txt, and rtf documents are published as PDFs.
