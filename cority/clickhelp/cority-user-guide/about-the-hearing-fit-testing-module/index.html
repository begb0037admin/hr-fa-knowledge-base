About the Hearing Fit Testing Module

Use the Hearing Fit module to specify and identify whether a hearing protection device is active for an employee; view, track and report hearing fit tests (EARFit or VeriPRO) for active protectors; and assign hearing protection fit test activity to an exposure group and track it to ensure that employees have received their hearing fit tests on time.

 

This chapter explains how to:

  • record hearing fit tests (see Entering Hearing Fit Results Manually)

  • import hearing fit test results (see Importing Hearing Fit Test Results).

Hearing Fit records may also be created as an activity in the Clinic Visit or Appointments/Scheduling modules.

To see the look-up tables used by this module and the reports available, see Look-up Tables Used by This Module. There may also be system settings that affect your use of this module; for more information, see Changing Your System Settings.
