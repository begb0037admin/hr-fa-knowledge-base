Clinic Visit Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Name

Description

Blood Pressure Results (with Graph)

Historical blood pressure readings by employee, displayed in text and line chart format.

Clinic Visit Duration

Calculation of the amount of contact time spent.

Tip: Use the Time Differential calculation in a Report Writer report to report on the waiting time or time taken.

Clinic Visit Ergonomic Tally

Count of ergonomic cases as they relate to restrictions and absences.

Clinic Visit Log

Log of each clinic visit which generates a count by clinic visit and/or by practitioner.

Clinic Visit Progress Notes

Summary of clinic visit notes by employee or case number.

Clinic Visit Unsigned Notes

List of clinic visit notes that have not been electronically signed by the assigned practitioner.

Clinic Visit Utilization

Number of clinic visits by clinic visit reason, health center, practitioner, or activity.

Completed Medical Surveillance Activities

List of completed medical activities by employee including the completed date.

Medical Disposition

Details and a count of medical dispositions associated with clinic visits.

Medical Records

Number of individual reports to document an employee's medical records; can include current, inactive, or all employees. Select the modules to be included.

Medical Surveillance SEG Letters

Print a batch of letters to employees and/or supervisors without having to open each employee’s record; option to save letters and change Employee Letter Generated (or Supervisor Letter Generated) flag to Yes in each record.

Medications

List of medications by employee including the start date and a total count of medications.

Referral

Referral information related to a clinic visit.

Surveillance Information Sheet

Summary of incomplete scheduled activities, recall activities, exposure group enrollments, health clearances and immunizations.
