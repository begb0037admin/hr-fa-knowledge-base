Office Ergonomics Reports

The following Personal Risk reports can be exported to Excel:

  • Employee List
  • Organizational Status
  • Issue Frequency
  • Desktop Statistics
  • Historical Risk
  • Desktop Aggregate

After you configure and save the report, click Export to Excel.

If an email address was provided for the report creator, a link for downloading the report will be emailed to the creator once the report is ready.
If no email address was provided, the report will be accessible from the Ergonomics Reports Export Log.
