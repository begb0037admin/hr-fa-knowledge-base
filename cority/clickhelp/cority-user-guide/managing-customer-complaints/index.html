Managing Customer Complaints

The Complaints module enables Quality Managers to record, review, investigate, and resolve customer complaints.

It will help you to:

  • Streamline the organization’s complaint resolution process

  • Create and assign complaint investigations

  • Initiate corrective or preventive actions and change requests to rectify issues.
