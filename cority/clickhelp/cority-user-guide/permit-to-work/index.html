Permit to Work

The Permit to Work solution requires a license key. Please contact your Customer Success Representative if you require more information or assistance in setting up this solution. 

Overview

The Permit to Work solution enables you to effectively manage any permit to work scenario your organization might face.

A permit to work is a formal, authorized, and controlled document that ensures the hazards and associated risks for a specific high-risk or non-standard process (and its activities) have been assessed and appropriate levels of control have been put in place to reduce the likelihood and severity of potential incidents.

The following types of work typically require a permit to work:

  • Hot work
  • Confined space
  • Lock out tag out
  • Working at heights

A permit to work typically includes information such as: the nature and location of the work being performed, the people involved and their training and/or qualifications, any hazards related to the work activities and the risk of exposure to those hazards, any control measures required and/or precautions to be taken when the work is being performed, the date, time and duration of the work, actions to be taken in the case of an emergency (rescue plans), proof of authorization from the permit issuer allowing the work to commence, and a post-work declaration that the work has been completed and the area is safe to return to for normal production.

The Cority Permit to Work solution allows you to manage your end-to-end permit-to-work lifecycle digitally. It is both a platform and mobile solution: you can complete a permit’s full lifecycle entirely within myCority, entirely within the GX2 platform, or using a combination of them. The solution is integrated with the Cority Safety cloud, which allows you to link related records such as contractor, contractor projects, findings and actions, etc. with ease. A work permit can be linked to a parent work permit, and all child permits for a parent can then be viewed and monitored.

A workflow must be associated with each permit. The workflow will be defined by your organization, and it drives the lifecycle of the permit. The workflow can include such steps as new and/or draft, at which the user can input specific information related to the permit and request approval, and an approval process through which a supervisor or permit issuer can approve the permit, permit activities, a clearance verification, and the completion of the permit.

Setup

To access the Permit to Work solution, in the Safety menu click Work Permit (under Control of Work).

The default Work Permit layout allows you to record typical basic information such as the location or co-ordinates, the permit type, the permit name, the description of the work being performed, start and end times, start and end dates, the approvers (one or several, depending on your process), etc. Your organization will need to create custom versions of the layout to accommodate your own processes and requirements.

The following fields are available:

  • Coordinates – allow users to view the permit on a map
  • Workflow – identifies the workflow step and status
  • Time Permit is Active – records start and end times
  • Contractor, Contractor Project – connected to the Contractors module
  • Employee fields (Employee Contact, Supervisor Contact, User-Defined Employee fields, Created By, Modified By)

    An employee identified through one of the Employee fields will also have access to the permit through myCority ‘My Tasks’.

  • Other UDF fields for organization-specific requirements and configuration.

The following sublists are available to collect/record and view additional permit information:

  • Work permit checklists and/or questionnaires – for gathering certain information (e.g. verification checklists and inspections)
  • Work Plans – for recording the nature and location of the work being performed
  • Permit Actions – for creating findings and assigning actions
  • Authorized Employees – for specifying who will be provided with access to the permit in GX2 or through myCority ‘My Tasks’. This enables you to designate specific users to who will be authorized to perform certain actions within one or more workflow step of a permit. A configurable button will be made available only to the authorized user(s) at the top of the permit during the particular workflow step, e.g. Approve Permit or Reject Permit.
  • Work Permit Risk - to track, monitor, and assess risk related to the work permit (configure the severity and probability options to support the risk calculation in the "Risk Matrix X-Axis (for Record Related Risk)" system setting).
  • Documents – for attaching pictures, SOPs, etc.
  • Letters – for sending email notifications to authorized employees (for example to notify an approver of the approval required in the current workflow step)
  • Permit Conditions – for providing additional permit requirements related to the permit type
  • Conditions and Regulations – for linking to the permit (through the Legal Requirements and Other module) any specific regulations that will govern it
  • Related Incidents – for linking to the permit (through the Incidents module) any related incidents
  • Linked Records - to link other associated records
  • Forms – for creating printable permits
  • Permit Limits – for recording limits for gas or other industrial hygiene measurements taken during permit activities
  • Share with Group – for sharing the permit will all of the employees in an active group
  • Workflow History – for viewing and tracking the permit’s workflow history
  • Related Assets – for linking to the permit (through the Equipment module) any associated equipment
