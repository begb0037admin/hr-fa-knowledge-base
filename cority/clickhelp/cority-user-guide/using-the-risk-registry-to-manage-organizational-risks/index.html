Using the Risk Registry to Manage Organizational Risks

The Risk Registry module helps organizations to document, track and respond to organizational risks or opportunities in order to proactively address risks and maintain compliance with ISO risk assessment standards.

In risk management, some companies like to track risks and opportunities. For example, if it snows today, there is a risk for freezing conditions that can hurt workers, but there is also an opportunity to collect rain water for recycling and using on the process. So a company looking at the risks of a snow storm might also want to track any positive things it can do with the snow event.
