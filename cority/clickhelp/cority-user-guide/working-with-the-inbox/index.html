Working with the Inbox

In the Occupational Health menu, click Inbox. The Inbox opens on the Clinic Visits tab. Only tabs that contain regular lists be highlighted when records are present; the Schedule tab, since it is a custom form that varies day to day, will not be highlighted even if there are appointments today.
