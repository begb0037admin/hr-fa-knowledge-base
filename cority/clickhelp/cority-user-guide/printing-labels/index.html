Printing Labels

You can print standard labels for chemical products, or create custom label templates for secondary GHS container labels that include all OSHA-required fields and hazard warnings for a product.

Printing Standard Labels

Print standard labels from the Chemical Product or Inventory lists, or from a product record.

  1. In a product, choose Actions»Print Labels; or
    In the Product or Inventory list, select the records that you want to print labels for, then choose Actions»Print Labels.

  2. Select the label type (WHMIS, NFPA, or GHS) and size (30, 10, 4 or 2 per sheet; options available depend on the type chosen; Avery label #s are provided as a guide), then click Report.

  3. The labels will appear in a separate report window where you can print or export the file.

Labels are also available from the Report menu; see Chemical Reports.

Creating Custom Label Templates

When creating a label template you define the size, number of labels per sheet, fields to be included, and their placement.

  1. In the Environmental menu, click Label Management.
  2. Enter a Template Name and select the Label Stock to be used.
  3. If appropriate, identify the label as NFPA or HMIS (in the Label System field) to include the NFPA or HMIS object on the label to communicate the acute and chronic hazards of a product to chemical handlers. The diamond object can be moved and resized.
  4. Specify the Font, Font Size, and the Padding to be maintained at the Top and Left of the label.
  5. On the Chemical Products tab, select the fields that should be represented on the label.
  6. The fields selected are shown on the Label Setup preview. Drag the fields to the desired placement on the label.
  7. On the Applicable Locations tab, select the locations for which you want to print labels.
  8. Click Save.

Printing Custom Labels

Print custom labels from the Chemical Product or Inventory list view. You can print custom labels for only one record at a time.

  1. In the Product or Inventory list, select the record that you want to print labels for, then choose Actions»Print Labels from Template.
  2. Select the label template.
  3. Optionally change any of the font or padding parameters as needed, or identify the label as NFPA or HMIS (in the Label System field) to include the NFPA or HMIS object on the label to communicate the acute and chronic hazards of a product to chemical handlers. The product's NFPA or HMIS ratings, entered on the Chemical Products record or on the AgentHazard look-up table, will overlay the diamond object.
  4. The fields defined by the template are shown on the Label Setup preview. Optionally choose any Symbols and/or Phrases to be included on the label.
  5. On the preview area, drag the elements to the desired placement on the label.
  6. Optionally click Save to save the label for future use.
  7. Click Print to print the label.
