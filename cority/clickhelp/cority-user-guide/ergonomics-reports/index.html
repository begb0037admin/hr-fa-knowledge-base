Industrial Ergonomics Reports

For general information on working with Cority reports, see Generating Standard Reports.

The following reports are available:

Report Title

Description

Case Manager Survey Report Aggregated survey results over a user-specified date range.
Can be exported to Microsoft Excel.
Desktop Statistics Report Provides:
  • Breaks data (on break compliance, natural breaks, microbreaks, etc.) over a user-configured or selected preset (such as daily, weekly, monthly, etc.) period of time.
  • Overall computer, keyboard, and mouse-use statistical data over a user-configured or selected preset (such as daily, weekly, monthly, etc.) period of time.
Can be exported to Microsoft Excel.
Historical Risk Report Historical risk data (active risk types, baseline risk, overall risk, desktop risk, break compliance, keyboard usage/strain, mouse usage/strain, etc.) over a user-configured or selected preset (such as daily, weekly, monthly, etc.) period of time.
Can be exported to Microsoft Excel.
Organizational Status Report Current aggregated risk distribution data (baselines for comparison and comparison by <attribute>, where <attribute> can be Geographic Location, Department, Building, Health Center, Job Position, Supervisor, Risk Type, etc.).
Can be exported to Microsoft Excel.
Issue Frequency Report Current issue frequency data (risk inventory issue(s), risk levels and potential benefits of adjustment, issues improved, issues resolved, etc.), as filtered by the user.
Can be exported to Microsoft Excel.
