About the Noise Monitoring Module

This module stores the results of personal dosimetry, sound level measurements and octave band analysis. The system is also configurable for different exchange (doubling) rates and for criteria levels (dB equivalents to 100% dose).

The entry screen captures operation status, sampling strategy, and name of the person conducting the sample. Personal samples include name of the employee (and their demographic data), types of personal protective equipment worn, their exposure groups, tasks performed, and the engineering and administrative controls used. Cority also captures sampling equipment used and pre- and post-calibration data.

A corrective actions tracking feature is included. Any actions can be assigned through Cority and automatic reminders and notifications can be set up to run through the Cority Email Notification Utility.

Notification letters and reports are available to report data to both management and staff. Robust report queries allow for the reporting by numerous factors, including employee, location, process, task, job position and exposure group.

There are three tools available to statistically evaluate data stored in the Noise Monitoring module, LogNorm, LOGAN and the AIHA Spreadsheet.
