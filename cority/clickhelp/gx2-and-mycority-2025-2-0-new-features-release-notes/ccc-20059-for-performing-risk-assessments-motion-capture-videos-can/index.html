CCC-20059: For performing risk assessments, motion capture videos can now be uploaded

Opt-In

In GX2, under Risk Assessment, for any task associated to a job, users can now select the 'Enable Motion Capture' check box to enable motion capture assessments. Once enabled, on the Actions menu, click 'Create Motion Capture Record' to create a new assessment.   

Users can also upload motion capture videos in myCority to create a new motion capture record.

On the 'Create Motion Capture Record' dialog box, the user will upload the motion capture video and provide details of the task and subject in the video. When the video is uploaded, the user will be required to answer additional questions about it.  

When processed, the motion capture assessment:  

  • Adds wireframes to the video over the subject, highlighting the ranges of motion performed in the task to compile an ergonomics risk assessment.
    Note: Administrators can choose to have faces, or the entire subject, blurred for privacy.
  • Allows the user to choose specific areas to analyze, such as neck or shoulders.  
  • Allows the user to choose a standardized assessment type from the following:
    • REBA
    • RULA
    • Inseer Composite Score

All assessments created for a task are listed in the 'Motion Capture Assessments' sublist, where they can be edited or removed.  

To access motion capture features, you must request the new 'Industrial Ergo w/ Motion Capture' license key from Cority HelpDesk, and users must have the new ‘Enable Motion Capture’ action assigned to their role.
