OES Emails

Automated emails are configured to be sent to users on certain event triggers in the system. The automated email is configurable to meet your organization's needs. You can preview the automated emails, which are available in multiple languages.

In addition to the automated emails, OES Administrators can send custom emails to individual users and groups of users using pre-fabricated email text. The text of these pre-fabricated emails may be customized for your system, and is available in multiple languages.
