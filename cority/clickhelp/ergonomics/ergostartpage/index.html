Ergonomics

Cority RSIGuard and Office Ergonomics Solution (OES) software is designed to help ergonomists and health & safety program managers understand and mitigate employee health-related risks. The integrated Case Manager allows professional ergonomists and physical therapists to efficiently manage cases that arise.

Documentation is available here for these types of users:

  • OES Admin: For OES administrators who are responsible for managing their organization's OES program and users.
  • Case Manager Admin: For administrators responsible for managing cases and scheduling follow-ups.
  • Case Manager Evaluator: For those responsible for performing workstation evaluations and initial case documentation.

RSIGuard documentation is available separately from that product and the RSIGuard help pages at rsiguard.com.
