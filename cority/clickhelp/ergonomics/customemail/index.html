Custom Emails

To send a custom email to an individual:

  1. From the Employee Summary, click Email Employee from either the left menu, or the Recent Email log.

  2. Enter the subject line for the email.

  3. Enter the text for the email.

    Optional:

    • Add additional people to receive copies of the email by adding email addresses to the Cc: box. Separate each email address with a semi-colon (;) or a comma (,).
    • Include an attachment: Click Browse and select a file from your computer or network.
    • Attach a "Helpful File" from the OES system (dependent on your system setup): Select the file from the drop-down list and click Add.

  4. Click "Send To:…" to send the email.

    The email will be sent from your email address.
