Health & Safety (OES and Case Manager) Release Notes

Version 1.8.27 Release Date: 7/22/2021

Version 1.8.27 is focused on the resolution of customer-reported product issues.


General Improvements and Bug Fixes

AppTicketDescription
CMHS-6805

In the 'Ergonomics Case and Task Status Report', HR data was missing for cases where the case type was changed (for example, if the case started out as a Walk-By and got changed to a Full Evaluation). All expected HR data will now be in the report.

OESHS-4585

For some users, risk and discomfort levels were not showing in the 'Employee List Report' or 'Employee Summary Page'. This has been resolved.


OESHS-6245

We have added a 'Started Online Program' logpoint. This logpoint will be triggered for anyone who clicks the 'Start' button. This will enable you to report on users who have started the assessment and training but have not yet reached the 'Gotten Past Initial Assessment' logpoint.


OESHS-6853

The discomfort score in 'Employee List Reporting' was not recalculating. The fix will recalculate the discomfort score for all users and ensure recalculations occur in the future.
