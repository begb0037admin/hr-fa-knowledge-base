Emailing a Group of People

You can send an email to a group of people by going into an Employee List Report and checking the box next to each user's name.

  1. Select the users you wish to email.

  2. Click Send Email.

  3. Enter a subject line for the email.

  4. Enter the text for the email.

    Additional Options:

    • In the text you can include a link to the login page to direct the user to their OES dashboard. (https://login.remedyint.com/[company]
    • Add additional people to receive copies of the email by adding email addresses to the Cc: box. Separate each email address with a semi-colon (;) or a comma (,).
    • Include an attachment: Click Browse and select a file from your computer or network.
    • Attach a "Helpful File" from the OES system (see Helpful Files and Links) by selecting the file from the drop-down list and click Add.
    • To automatically include the user's full name from their OES account, select "Recipient Name" from the Fields dropdown.

  5. Click Send To:… to send the email.

    The email will be sent from your email address.

    When sending emails from within OES, DO NOT use the option to add a Reassessment Link to the email.

    When sending emails in bulk, ALWAYS double check the number of users being affected prior to sending the email.

    Important:  Once sent, the emails cannot be recalled.
