Appendix B: Automated emails

A set of automated emails are initially available in the system for your use. Other custom pre-fabricated emails may be set up as requested. Email types can be enabled or disabled for your system as required.

The following table shows default email and text. Email text may be edited and customized for your system.

Email

Email text

Invitation email

Dear [Name],

As part of the company injury prevention program, we would like to share with you our interactive online ergonomics assessment and training, the OES from Remedy Interactive.

OES will assess your personal risks for injury in the workplace and teach you how to setup your workstation for a more comfortable and healthy environment. After completing the assessment and training, you will receive an email with specific feedback about your personal areas of risk. Depending on your risk level, OES may email you in a couple of weeks to reassess how you're doing and whether or not further suggestions can be made.

The assessment and training should take about 35 minutes. The system will automatically save your place in the program, so feel free to take a break and come back to the training soon after.

Click on the instant login link to begin. The link is personalized for you, so please do not share or forward it to other people.

If you have any questions, please contact your Health and Safety team.

Thank you,

Your Health and Safety Team
