Workstation Self-Assessment Review Email

The Workstation Self-Assessment Review Email prompts users to visit their Issue Resolution tab to address any open issues.

Prior to sending this email, please ensure:

  • The user is in conformance with the 2-year reassessment window (i.e. the user has completed the Full Self-Assessment & Training within the last 2 years).
  • The user has open issues on their Dashboard To-Do List. You may view the user's To-Do List by selecting View User's Dashboard from the Employee Summary.

To send the email to a user, select the Workstation Self-Assessment Review email from the pre-fabricated email dropdown box.

The Workstation Self-Assessment Review email will be sent to the user from your email address.
