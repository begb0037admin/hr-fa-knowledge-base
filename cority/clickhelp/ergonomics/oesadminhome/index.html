Administration Home Page

Download a print version of this guide here: OES Admin Guide (PDF)

As an OES Administrator, when you log into the OES you see the Administration Home page. From here you can view the organization-wide dashboard, manage your system, and manage users.

The tabs available include:

  • Home: Shows organizational announcements and organization-wide risk and progress charts.
  • People: Manage users and search user database.
  • Reporting: Run OES reports.
  • My Settings: Update your password and manage My Saved Criteria.

Note:  Your administrative access is determined by the specific Administrator Role to which you are assigned. See OES Roles.

From the menu on the left you can:

  • Access Helpful Files and Links
  • Preview OES Emails
  • Find users, view user information, and add new users. Users you viewed recently are shown for quick access (My Recent Items). See Manage Users.

The OES Administrator's dashboard contains a set of panels:

  • Company announcements. This panel may be modified by any administrator by clicking the link "modify this announcement".
  • Program Highlights: Includes a summary of OES program highlights across your company, and shows the date of the latest HR data upload.
  • Current Organizational Risk Status shows organization-wide risk in a pie chart, updated nightly.
  • Organizational Risk Progress: The bar chart compares initial aggregate assessment risk to current risk, updated nightly. For definition of Risk Levels see Risk Levels.
