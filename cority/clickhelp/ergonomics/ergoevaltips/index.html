Ergonomic Evaluation Tips

Using the Ergonomic Worksheet:

  • If you're linked to their OES data, you can save time by looking for blue marks in Not Corrected buttons and focus on these issues first.
  • If you see an issue during the observation period (e.g., wrists are bent while mousing), and you know that you will make a recommendation to correct it before you finish your evaluation (e.g., relax fingers and adjust mouse surface), mark this as Corrected.
  • If you see an issue during the observation period that you cannot correct during the evaluation (e.g., elbow is away from the side of the body) and you know that you will make a recommendation that will require a corrective action (e.g., purchase a short keyboard), mark this as Not Corrected. The Not Corrected items will be the focus of the Follow-Up.
  • Use the comments field to document useful information for the current readers and for subsequent ergonomists' review. Remember, these comments reside on the worksheet only, not on the Evaluation Report.
  • If a delay occurs during your evaluation (e.g, the person has to take a phone call or someone walks up and interrupts your meeting), Case Manager will automatically save the data to secure your work.
