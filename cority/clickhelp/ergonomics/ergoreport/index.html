Ergonomic Report

After completing the evaluation worksheet, recommendations are pulled into the report writer. The report writer interface divides the recommendations into report sections. Each recommendation can be further edited and stylized to provide emphasis in the final evalation report that is delivered.

The report can be previewed before it is completed and sent to employees, supervisors and other in your health & safety team.
