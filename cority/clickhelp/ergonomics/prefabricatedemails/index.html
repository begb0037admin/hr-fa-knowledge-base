Pre-fabricated Emails

Pre-fabricated emails are available to be sent manually as needed by a OES Administrator. A variety of default and custom pre-fabricated emails may be enabled for your system.

To send a pre-fabricated email:

  1. From the Employee Summary, click Email Employee (or Send Message to Employee) from either the left menu, or the Recent Email log summary.

  2. Check the Send a pre-fabricated email box and choose the email type to send from the drop-down list.

    Optional:

    • Add additional people to receive copies of the email by adding email addresses to the Cc: box. Separate each email address with a semi-colon (;) or a comma (,).
    • Include an attachment: Click Browse and select a file from your computer or network.
    • Attach a helpful file from your Helpful Files and Links. Files that have been made available for email attachment are shown in the drop-down list. Select the file to attach and click Add.
  3. Click the Send To: button to send the email.
