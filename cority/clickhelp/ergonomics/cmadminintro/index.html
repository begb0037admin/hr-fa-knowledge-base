Cority's Office Ergonomics Suite (OES) Case Manager System is an integrated system designed to document Ergonomic Workstation Evaluations and Follow Up Reports.

Worksheets and employee reports can be completed and viewed according to each Administator's, Evaluator's, and Scheduler's access role.

Case Manager was designed specifically for ergonomists and health & safety program managers. Thousands of hours of interviews in the field went directly into this program, and continue to do so.

Case Manager provides ergonomic professionals with these benefits:

  • Increases administrative efficiency. Case Manager replaces unwieldy clipboards and reams of paper with a online questionnaire that eliminates questions not relevant to your case.
  • Ensures consistent data collection and reports. By standardizing forms and communications, the system saves time and maintains data uniformity.
  • Creates a centralized database. Employees' OES and RSIGuard profiles are fully integrated, so you don't have to look for scattered pieces of information for every evaluation.
  • Delivers instant access to standardized information making audits of employee behavior and compliance easier.
  • Custom configuration. Data collection or question and answer fields can be adapted to your organization. Case Manager can be configured to enforce your business rules around scheduling, access to information and performance of evaluation.
  • Imposes structure and deadlines. The program guides users through the steps of completing an evaluation successfully.
  • Completely transparent process. The system allows managers to assess and optimize team performance from case opened to case closed.
  • Instills accountability. Team members have shared timelines, automated communications and are monitored for task completion.
