Shingles Survey (EHS-9660)

Have you ever had the herpes zoster virus (shingles) infection? (Shingles)

New: YES= ZVL (Zostavax) recipients will likely benefit from vaccination with RZV (Shingrix).

New: NO= RZV (Shingrix) may be used in adults aged ≥50 years, irrespective of prior receipt of varicella vaccine or ZVL (Zostavax) and does not require screening for a history of chickenpox (varicella). 

New: DON’T KNOW= RZV (Shingrix) may be used in adults aged ≥50 years, irrespective of prior receipt of varicella vaccine or ZVL (Zostavax) and does not require screening for a history of chickenpox (varicella).  
ZVL (Zostavax) remains a recommended vaccine for prevention of herpes zoster in immunocompetent adults aged ≥60 years (6). Care should be taken not to confuse ZVL (Zostavax), which is stored in the freezer and administered subcutaneously, with RZV (Shingrix), which is stored in the refrigerator and administered intramuscularly.

Have you ever received a herpes zoster (shingles) vaccine (Zostavax)? (Shingles)

New: YES= Age and time since receipt of ZVL (Zostavax) may be considered to determine when to vaccinate with RZV (Shingrix). Studies examined the safety and immunogenicity of RZV (Shingrix) vaccination administered ≥5 years after ZVL (Zostavax).

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to gelatin? (Shingles)

New: YES= Severe allergy to gelatin. CONTRAINDICATION for Zostavax which contains porcine gelatin. RZV (Shingrix) does not contain porcine gelatin.

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to the antibiotic "neomycin"? (Shingles)

New: YES= Confirm severe allergy to neomycin. CONTRAINDICATION for Zostavax. RZV (Shingrix) does not contain antibiotics.

Have you ever had a life threatening allergic or other reaction to a herpes zoster (shingles) vaccine (Zostavax)? (Shingles)

New: YES= RZV (Shingrix) should not be administered to persons with a history of severe allergic reaction, such as anaphylaxis, to any component of this vaccine changes.

Do you have an immune deficiency? For example, do you have an immune system disease or take immunosuppressive medicine? (Shingles)

New: YES= As with ZVL (Zostavax), the ACIP recommends the use of RZV (Shingrix) in persons taking low-dose immunosuppressive therapy (e.g., <20 mg/day of prednisone or equivalent or using inhaled or topical steroids) and persons anticipating immunosuppression or who have recovered from an immunocompromising illness. CONTRAINDICATION for persons taking high-dose immunosuppressive therapy.

Do you currently have an acute illness or fever? (Shingles)

New: YES= Current acute illness. WARNING for Zostavax/Shingrix and should consider deferring vaccination.  Adults with chronic medical conditions (e.g., chronic renal failure, diabetes mellitus, rheumatoid arthritis, and chronic pulmonary disease) should receive RZV (Shingrix).

Are you pregnant at this time or do you plan to become pregnant in the next 4 weeks? (Shingles)

New: YES= Pregnant or planning pregnancy in the next 4 weeks. CONTRAINDICATION for Zostavax. Do not give vaccine to pregnant women. Advise worker that pregnancy should be avoided for 4 weeks after vaccination. There are no available data to establish whether RZV is safe in pregnant or lactating women and there is currently no ACIP recommendation for RZV use in this population. Consider delaying vaccination with RZV in such circumstances.

How old are you? (Shingles)

New: 49 or younger= Consult PCP for vaccination approval of healthy adults less than 50 years old.

New: 50 to 59= With high efficacy among adults aged ≥50 years, and modest waning of protection over 4 years following vaccination, RZV (Shingrix) has the potential to prevent substantial herpes zoster disease burden. Vaccinating adults starting at age 50 will prevent disease incidence in midlife, and the vaccine will likely continue to provide substantial protection beyond 4 years as recipients age.
