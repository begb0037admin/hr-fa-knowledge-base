MMR Survey (EHS-9631)

Have you ever had measles? (MMR)

New: NO= Ensure that all HCP have presumptive evidence of immunity to measles. Consider vaccinating HCP born before 1957 who do not have other evidence of immunity to measles.

New: Don’t Know= Consider vaccinating HCP born before 1957 who do not have other evidence of immunity to measles.

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to eggs? (MMR)

New: YES= People with egg allergy of any severity can receive any IIV, RIV, or LAIV that is otherwise appropriate for the patient’s age and health status; ccIIV and RIV do not contain egg antigen. When administering an influenza vaccine other than ccIIV or RIV to a person with a history of severe allergic reaction to egg, or who required emergency medical intervention (e.g., epinephrine), vaccination should occur in a clinic, health department, or physician office; vaccine administration should be supervised by a healthcare provider who is able to recognize and manage severe allergic conditions.

Have you ever had a life threatening allergic or other reaction to a MMR, measles, or rubella vaccine? (MMR)

New: YES= History of anaphylactic reaction (see question 2) to a previous dose of vaccine or vaccine component is a contraindication for subsequent doses. Under normal circumstances, vaccines are deferred when a precaution is present. However, situations may arise when the benefit outweighs the risk (e.g., during a community pertussis outbreak).

Do you have an immune deficiency? For example, do you have an immune system disease or take immunosuppressive medicine? (MMR)

New: YES= Live virus vaccines (e.g., LAIV, MMR, VAR) are usually contraindicated in immunocompromised people. However, there are exceptions. For example, MMR vaccine is recommended, and VAR vaccine may be considered for adults with CD4+ T-lymphocyte counts of greater than or equal to 200 cells/µL. Immuno-suppressed people should not receive LAIV.

Do you have a cancer affecting the bone marrow or blood, leukemia, lymphoma, or other blood abnormality? (MMR)

New: YES= A history of thrombocytopenia or thrombocytopenic purpura is a precaution to MMR vaccine. LAIV is not recommended for people with anatomic or functional asplenia, complement component deficiency, a cochlear implant, or CSF leak. Underlying health conditions of the heart, lung, kidney, or metabolic disease (e.g., diabetes) and asthma are considered precautions for the use of LAIV. Aspirin use is a precaution to VAR.

Updates as of May, 31st

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to the antibiotic "neomycin"? (MMR) 

New: YES= Remove: CONTRAINDICATION for MMR-II, Mumpsvax, Attenuvax, and ProQuad. Keep: Reaction to neomycin. Determine if reaction was anaphylaxis.  Contact dermatitis from neomycin is not a contraindication.

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergy or other reaction to gelatin? (MMR) 

New: YES= Remove: CONTRAINDICATION for MMR-II, Attenuvax, Mumpsvax, and ProQuad. 

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to eggs? (MMR) 

New: YES= Allergy to eggs. Warning for MMR-II and ProQuad. Although measles and mumps components of the vaccine are grown in chick embryo fibroblast tissue culture, allergy to egg is not a contraindication to vaccination. Among persons who are allergic to eggs, the risk for serious allergic reactions, such as anaphylaxis after administration of MMR vaccine, is exceedingly low. Use caution when administering M-M-R II and ProQuad to individuals with anaphylaxis or immediate hypersensitivity following egg ingestion. 

Do you have a cancer affecting the bone marrow or blood, leukemia, lymphoma, or other blood abnormality? (MMR) 

New: YES= A history of thrombocytopenia or thrombocytopenic purpura is a precaution to MMR vaccine. LAIV is not recommended for people with anatomic or functional asplenia, complement component deficiency, a cochlear implant, or CSF leak. Underlying health conditions of the heart, lung, kidney, or metabolic disease (e.g., diabetes) and asthma are considered precautions for the use of LAIV. Aspirin use is a precaution to VAR. Add: Use caution when administering M-M-R II and Proquad to individuals with a history of thrombocytopenia. Thrombocytopenia and thrombocytopenic purpura have been reported following vaccination with PRIORIX.

Are you pregnant or planning a pregnancy in the next 4 weeks? (MMR) 

New: YES= Add: Do not administer M-M-R II to females who are pregnant. Pregnancy should be avoided for 1 month following vaccination with M-M-R II.  


Do not administer ProQuad to females who are pregnant. Pregnancy should be avoided for 3 months following vaccination with ProQuad.  


Do not use during pregnancy. 
Avoid pregnancy for 1 month following vaccination with PRIORIX. 

Do you have a low platelet count (thrombocytopenia)? (MMR)

New: YES= Thrombocytopenia can be worsened if vaccinated. Use caution when administering M-M-R II and Proquad to individuals with a history of thrombocytopenia. Thrombocytopenia and thrombocytopenic purpura have been reported following vaccination with PRIORIX. Consider checking serologic status to determine if vaccination is needed. 

Have you recently received an injection of immunoglobulin? (MMR) 

New: YES= Recent immunoglobulin injection. DRUG INTERACTION for MMR-II, Priorix, and ProQuad. Immunoglobulin is not to be given concurrently with live vaccines. Add: Administration of immune globulins and other blood products concurrently with M-M-R II, Priorix, and Proquad vaccine may interfere with the expected immune response.
