TDAP Survey (EHS-9658)

Have you ever had a life threatening allergic or other reaction to a Tdap vaccine? (TDAP)

New: YES= Previous life-threatening reaction to a tetanus vaccination. CONTRAINDICATION for all tetanus vaccines. Do not give Tdap or Td to a person who has experienced a serious systemic or anaphylactic reaction to a prior dose of either vaccine or to any of its components.

Do you have a neurologic disorder (such as epilepsy or recent stroke)? (TDAP)

New: YES= Neurologic condition. For Tdap only: progressive or unstable neurologic disorder, uncontrolled seizures or progressive encephalopathy until the patient’s treatment regimen has been established and the condition has stabilized. These conditions may be considered reason to defer Tdap vaccination.

Have you ever had a decreased level of consciousness, coma or prolonged seizures within 7 days of receiving a vaccine that included pertussis? (TDAP)

New: YES= Do not give Tdap to a person who has experienced encephalopathy within 7 days following DTP/DTaP/Tdap not attributable to another identifiable cause.
