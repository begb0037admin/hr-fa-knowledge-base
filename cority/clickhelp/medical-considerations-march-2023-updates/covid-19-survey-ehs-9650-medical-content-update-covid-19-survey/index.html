COVID-19 Survey (EHS-9650)

Please indicate your age range: (COVID AGE RANGE)

New: Under 16= No longer an issue. Recent MMWRs recommend MRNA (Pfizer and Moderna) for children 6 months -5 yrs. 

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to any vaccine? (COVID ALLERGIC REACTION)

New: YES= Contraindication. Severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) after a previous dose or to a component of the COVID-19 vaccine.

Do you currently have an acute illness and/or high fever? (COVID FEVER)

New: YES= Defer vaccination until person has recovered from acute illness and criteria have been met for them to discontinue isolation.

Do you have current or planned immunosuppression? [F] (COVID IMMUNOSUPPRESSION)

New: YES= Whenever possible, COVID-19 vaccines should be administered at least 2 weeks before initiation or resumption of immunosuppressive therapies.

Do you have a bleeding disorder (such as hemophilia, low platelets) or are you taking a blood thinner? (COVID BLEED DISORDER)

New: YES= It is contraindicated to administer Janssen COVID-19 Vaccine to persons with a history of TTS following receipt of the Janssen COVID-19 Vaccine or any other adenovirus vector-based COVID-19 vaccines (e.g., AstraZeneca’s COVID-19 Vaccine)

Have you received any other vaccinations in the past 30 days? (COVID OTHER VACCINES)

New: YES= COVID-19 vaccines may be administered on the same day as other vaccines. Administer each injection in a different injection site.

Have you received a confirmed positive COVID-19 Antibody lab test in the past 3 months? (COVID POSITIVE AB)

New: YES= COVID-19 vaccination is recommended for everyone ages 6 months and older, regardless of a history of symptomatic or asymptomatic SARS-CoV-2 infection. People who recently had SARS-CoV-2 infection may consider delaying their next COVID-19 dose by 3 months from symptom onset or positive test (if infection was asymptomatic)

Are you pregnant at this time or do you plan to become pregnant in the next 2 months? (COVID PREGNANT)

New: YES= Are recommended to be vaccinated according to the recommended schedule.

Are you currently breastfeeding? (COVID BREASTFEEDING)

New: YES= Are recommended to be vaccinated according to the recommended schedule.
