Meningitis Survey (EHS-9630)

Are you exposed to N. meningitidis as part of your work? For example, work in microbiology. (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Determine if worker routinely works in microbiology with the bacterium N. meningitidis or at increased risk during an outbreak, i.e. hospital settings such as ER, Urgent Care, Clinics, etc.)

If yes, has it been more than 5 years since you had a meningococcal vaccine? (MENGCAL)

New: Revaccination after 5 years with Serogroups A, C, W, Y meningococcal conjugate vaccines: MenACWY-D (Menactra, Sanofi Pasteur), or MenACWY-CRM (Menveo, Glaxo Smithkline)for ages 15-55 yrs old, or MenACWY-TT (MenQuadfi, Sanofi Pasteur)for ages >= 15 yrs. old, is recommended for adults who were previously vaccinated with either MCV4 (conjugate vaccine) or MPSV4 (polysaccharide vaccine) if they remain at increased risk for infection (e.g. adults with anatomic or functional asplenia.) Booster doses administered outside of these ages or administration of >1 booster dose are considered off-label. During an outbreak, a single MenB booster dose was recommended if it had been ≥1 year since primary series completion (interval of ≥6 months may be considered if recommended by public health officials).

Are you allergic to thimerosal (a mercury derivative)? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Remove; Menomune: Package Insert / Prescribing Information - Drugs.com/Discontinued

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to latex? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Allergy to latex. CONTRAINDICATION and warning for Menactra and Bexsero.

Do you have any of the following medical conditions: Complement component deficiency, asplenia (no spleen), "functional asplenia" (a spleen that does not work normally). (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Medical condition that affects ability to fight infection. Consider revaccination with MenACWY (conjugate) if more than 5 years since last vaccination.

Has a doctor ever told you that you had "Guillain-Barre Syndrome" (GBS)? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Past Guillain-Barre Syndrome. Contraindication and previous history of Guillain-Barré syndrome is listed as a precaution for MenACWY-D (Menactra) in the package insert.

Have you ever had a life threatening allergic or other reaction to a meningococcal vaccine? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Previous life-threatening reaction to a meningococcal vaccine. CONTRAINDICATION for Menactra, Menveo, MenQuadfi, Trumenba, and Bexsero.

Have you ever had a life-threatening allergic reaction to kanamycin? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Life threatening allergic reaction to Kenamycin. Bexsero contains <0.01mcg of Kenamycin.

Are you 56 or older? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Vaccination of persons aged ≥56 years is considered off-label.

Do you currently have an acute illness? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= People who have a moderate or severe illness should probably wait until they recover. 

Do you have a bleeding disorder (such as hemophilia, low platelets) or are you taking a blood thinner? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Bleeding disorder. Risk of bleeding/bruising from intramuscular injection.

Are you pregnant or do you think you might be pregnant? (MENGCAL)

New: YES= Pregnant and lactating women should receive MenACWY vaccine if indicated. Because limited data are available for MenB vaccination during pregnancy, vaccination with MenB should be deferred unless the woman is at increased risk and, after consultation with her health care provider, the benefits of vaccination are considered to outweigh the potential risks.
