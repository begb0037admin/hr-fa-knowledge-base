Seasonal Flu Survey (EHS-9651)

Please select one of the following: (SFLUFLU11)

New: I AGREE to receive the seasonal flu vaccination [Open -> MULTIPLE (2)] = Syncope (fainting) can occur in association with the administration of injectable (IM) vaccines. Procedures should be in place to avoid falling injuries and to restore cerebral perfusion following syncope. Routine annual influenza vaccination is recommended for all persons aged ≥6 months who do not have contraindications.

Please indicate your age range: (FLU AGE RANGE)

New: 65 or older= ACIP recommends that adults aged ≥65 years preferentially receive any one of the following higher dose or adjuvanted influenza vaccines: quadrivalent high-dose inactivated influenza vaccine (HD-IIV4), quadrivalent recombinant influenza vaccine (RIV4), or quadrivalent adjuvanted inactivated influenza vaccine (aIIV4).

[H] Are you currently on aspirin therapy? (FLU ASPIRIN THERAPY)

New: YES= Children and adolescents receiving concomitant aspirin- or salicylate-containing medications, because of the potential risk for Reye syndrome (a labeled contraindication noted in the package insert).

Hives (FLU HIVES)

New: HIVES= Persons with a history of egg allergy who have experienced only urticaria (hives) after exposure to egg should receive influenza vaccine. Any licensed, recommended influenza vaccine (i.e., any IIV4, RIV4, or LAIV4) that is otherwise appropriate for the recipient’s age and health status can be used.

Cardiovascular changes (FLU CARDIOVASCULAR CHANGES)

New: Cardiovascular Changes= Egg allergy reaction - Cardiovascular changes. RIV4 or ccIIV can be administered but in such an instance, vaccination should occur in an inpatient or outpatient medical setting and should be supervised by a health care provider who is able to recognize and manage severe allergic reactions. 

Breathing difficulty (such as wheezing) (FLU BREATHING DIFFICULTY)

New: Breathing Difficulty (such as wheezing) = Egg allergy reaction - breathing difficulty. Administer RIV4 if patient is 18-49. Refer to a physician with expertise with management of allergic conditions per CDC/ACIP recommendations.

Nausea/vomiting (FLU NAUSEA)

New: Nausea/vomiting= Egg allergy reaction - Nausea/Vomiting. RIV4 or ccIIV can be administered but in such an instance, vaccination should occur in an inpatient or outpatient medical setting and should be supervised by a health care provider who is able to recognize and manage severe allergic reactions. 

Reaction requiring epinephrine shot (FLU EPI SHOT)

New: Reaction requiring epinephrine shot= All egg-based IIV4s are contraindicated in persons who have had a severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) to a previous dose of any influenza vaccine (any egg-based IIV, ccIIV, RIV, or LAIV of any valency). Use of ccIIV4 is contraindicated in persons who have had a severe allergic reaction (e.g., anaphylaxis) to any ccIIV of any valency.
