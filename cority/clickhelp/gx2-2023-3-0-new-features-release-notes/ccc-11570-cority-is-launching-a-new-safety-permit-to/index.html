CCC-11570: Cority has a new Safety ‘Permit to Work’ solution

This solution requires a license key.

In GX2, the Permit to Work solution is located in the Safety cloud menu, under ‘Control of Work’.

In myCority, it can be accessed in the Create New section (‘New Work Permit’), if you are creating a new permit, or if the permit is already in progress or completed, it can be found under My Tasks, when filtered by ‘Work Permit’. For My Tasks ‘Work Permit’, the grey circle at the top right corner indicates the number of permits that are currently active.

A permit to work is a formal, authorized, and controlled document that ensures the hazards and associated risks for a specific high-risk or non-standard process (and its activities) have been assessed and appropriate levels of control have been put in place to reduce the likelihood and severity of potential incidents.

The following types of work typically require a permit to work:

  • Hot work
  • Confined space
  • Lock out tag out
  • Working at heights

A permit to work typically includes information such as: the nature and location of the work being performed, the people involved and their training and/or qualifications, any hazards related to the work activities and the risk of exposure to those hazards, any control measures required and/or precautions to be taken when the work is being performed, the date, time and duration of the work, actions to be taken in the case of an emergency (rescue plans), proof of authorization from the permit issuer allowing the work to commence, and a post-work declaration that the work has been completed and the area is safe to return to for normal production.

The Cority Permit to Work solution allows you to manage your end-to-end permit-to-work lifecycle digitally. It is both a platform and mobile solution. You can complete a permit’s full lifecycle entirely within myCority, entirely within the GX2 platform, or using a combination of them. The solution is integrated with the Cority Safety cloud, which allows you to link related records such as contractor, contractor projects, findings and actions, etc. with ease.

The Cority ‘Permit to Work’ solution enables you to effectively manage any permit to work scenario your organization might face.

A workflow must be associated with each permit. The workflow will be defined by your organization, and it drives the lifecycle of the permit.

The workflow can include such steps as new and/or draft, at which the user can input specific information related to the permit and request approval, and an approval process through which a supervisor or permit issuer can approve the permit, permit activities, a clearance verification, and the completion of the permit.

The out-of-the-box ‘Work Permit’ layout (ML-003763) is basic, so most organizations will want to create custom versions of it with additional fields that will accommodate their own processes and requirements. Note: The only required field on the out-of-the-box version is the Code field, and its value must be unique.

On the out-of-the-box layout, you can record typical information such as the location or co-ordinates, the permit type, the permit name, the description of the work being performed, start and end times, start and end dates, the approvers (one or several, depending on your process), etc.

The layout editor can be used to configure one or more custom layouts for your organization.

With the layout editor, you can add fields such as:

  • Co-ordinates – allow users to view the permit on a map
  • Workflow – identifies the workflow step and status
  • Time Permit is Active – records start and end times
  • Contractor – which is connected to the existing Contractors module, for linking contractors
  • Contractor Project – which is connected to the existing Contractors module, for linking contactor projects
  • A number of Employee fields:
    • Employee Contact
    • Supervisor Contact
    • User-Defined Employee fields 1 – 9
    • Created By
    • Modified By
      Note: An employee identified through one of the Employee fields will also have access to the permit through myCority ‘My Tasks’.
  • Other fields for organization-specific requirements and configuration:
    • 9 Text UDFs
    • 9 Look-up UDFs
    • 12 Number UDFs

    In addition to the above information, several sublists are available that enable you to collect/record and view additional permit information:

    • Work permit checklists and/or questionnaires – for gathering certain information (examples include: verification checklists and inspections)
    • Work Plans – for recording the nature and location of the work being performed
    • Permit Actions – for creating findings and assigning actions
    • Authorized Employees – for specifying who will be provided with access to the permit in GX2 or through myCority ‘My Tasks’
      This enables you to designate specific users to who will be authorized to perform certain actions within one or more workflow step of a permit. A button, completely configurable by your organization, will be made available only to the authorized user(s) at the top of the permit during the particular workflow step. The button will be used to take the action. For example, Approve Permit or Reject Permit.
    • Documents – for attaching pictures, SOPs, etc.
    • Letters – for sending email notifications to authorized employees (for example to notify an approver of the approval required in the current workflow step)
    • Permit Conditions – for providing additional permit requirements related to the permit type
    • Conditions and Regulations – for linking to the permit (through the existing Legal Requirements and Other module) any specific regulations that will govern it
    • Related Incidents – for linking to the permit (through the existing Incidents module) any related incidents
    • Forms – for creating printable permits
    • Permit Limits – for recording limits for gas or other industrial hygiene measurements taken during permit activities
    • Share with Group – for sharing the permit will all of the employees in an active group
    • Workflow History – for viewing and tracking the permit’s workflow history
    • Related Assets – for linking to the permit (through the existing Equipment module) any associated equipment

    The sublists are completely configurable by your organization.

    Also, a work permit can be linked to a parent work permit, and all child permits for a parent can then be viewed and monitored.

    For information on creating custom layouts, or working with sublists, look-up tables, or the Incidents, Legal Requirements and Other, or Equipment modules, see the GX2 User Guide.
