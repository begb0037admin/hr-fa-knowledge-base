Edit Discharges to Streams or Water Bodies

In Part II Section 5.3, you must enter discharges of a chemical to streams or water bodies.

To edit a discharge to a stream or water body, or view existing discharge information:

  1. Select the radio button associated with the stream/water body. Click Edit Stream.
  2. Enter changes and click Save.

To add a discharge to a stream or water body:

  1. Click Add Stream. (Uncheck NA if necessary first.)
  2. Select the stream or water body from the drop-down menu.
  3. Enter the release amount and the percent from stormwater for each business unit.
  4. Select the Basis of Estimate from the list.
  5. Click Save.

To delete a discharge to a stream or water body:

  1. Select the radio button associated with the stream/water body. Click Delete Stream.
  2. Click OK to confirm the deletion.
