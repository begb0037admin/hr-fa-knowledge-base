Section 8.8 Detail Values

Click the Detail 8.8 Values button to display the Section 8.8 Details sheet and review the distribution values that are calculated.

You may edit the distributed values on this sheet if necessary. Total distribution value for any box may not be more than the calculated total for all items in that section.

After editing any values, click Save. After saving, you will be returned to FormR, Section 8.

8.8(i) Quantity released due

Section 8.8(i) declares the total quantity released due to catastrophic events. The amount is divided between four fields to correspond with Subsections 8.1a, 8.1b, 8.1c and 8.1d, to allow for automatic calculation of the Section 8.1 fields.

8.8(i)(a): Onsite disposal to UIC Class I Wells, RCRA Subtitle C landfills, and other landfills

This is subtracted from Section 5 amounts and used to calculate the quantity for 8.1a as follows:

8.1a = 5.4.1 + 5.5.1A + 5.5.1B – 8.8(i)(a)

8.8(i)(b): Other onsite disposal or other releases

This is subtracted from Section 5 amounts and used to calculate the quantity for 8.1b as follows:

8.1b = 5.1 + 5.2 + 5.3 + 5.4.2 + 5.5.2 + 5.5.3A + 5.5.3B + .5.4 – 8.8(i)(b)

8.8(ii): Quantity used for energy recovery offsite due to remedial/catastrophic events

This field corresponds to section 8.3 as follows:

8.3 = 6.2 (M56, M92) – 8.8(ii)

Enter the sum of the quantities used for energy recovery offsite that were from remedial actions, catastrophic events, or one-time events not associated with production processes. These quantities would have been included in the following section (along with releases from normal, routine operations):

6.2, Waste Treatment Method codes M56 and M92

8.8(iii): Quantity recycled offsite due to remedial/catastrophic events

This field corresponds to section 8.5 as follows:

8.5 = 6.2 (M20, M24, M26, M28, M93)– 8.8(iii)

Enter the sum of the quantities recycled offsite that were from remedial actions, catastrophic events, or one-time events not associated with production processes. These quantities would have been included in the following section (along with releases from normal, routine operations):

6.2, Waste Treatment Method codes M20, M24, M26, M28, and M93

8.8(iv): Quantity treated offsite due to remedial/catastrophic events

This field corresponds to section 8.7 as follows:

8.7 = 6.1 (Non-Metals) + 6.2 (M40, M50, M54, M61, M69, M95) – 8.8(iv)

Enter the sum of the quantities treated offsite that were from remedial actions, catastrophic events, or one-time events not associated with production processes. These quantities would have been included in the following sections (along with releases from normal, routine operations):

6.1 (Non-Metals)

6.2 (Waste Code Methods M40, M50, M54, M61, M69, M95
