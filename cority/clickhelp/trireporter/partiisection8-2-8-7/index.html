Part II Section 8.2 - 8.7

Sections 8.2 and 8.3: Energy Recovery

These relate to an EPCRA Section 313 chemical or a mixture containing an EPCRA Section 313 chemical that is used for energy recovery on-site or is sent off-site for energy recovery, unless it is a commercially available fuel (e.g., fuel oil no. 6). For the purposes of reporting on Form R, reportable on-site and off-site energy recovery is the combustion of a waste stream containing an EPCRA Section 313 chemical when:

(a) The combustion unit is integrated into an energy recovery system (i.e., industrial furnaces, industrial kilns, and boilers); and

(b) The EPCRA Section 313 chemical is combustible and has a significant heating value (e.g., 5000 BTU)

Note:  Metals and metal category compounds cannot be combusted for energy recovery. For metals and metal category compounds, you should enter NA in Sections 8.2 and 8.3.

Quantities used for energy recovery off-site that are reported in Section 8.8 are excluded from Section 8.3.

8.2 Energy Recovery on-site

If you checked 7B "Not Applicable" 8.2 is marked "NA" and cannot have any release values. 7B "Not Applicable" must be unchecked before you can enter values for 8.2.

Section 8.2 is not related to Sections 5 or 6.

8.3 Energy Recovery off-site

The value for this field is calculated as follows:

8.3 = 6.2 (energy recovery codes M56, M92) - 8.8(ii) (offsite energy recovery)

Sections 8.4 and 8.5: Recycling

These relate to an EPCRA Section 313 chemical in a waste that is recycled on-site or is sent off-site for recycling. Quantities recycled off-site that are reported in Section 8.8 are excluded from Section 8.5.

8.4 Recycling on-site

If you checked 7C "Not Applicable" 8.4 is marked "NA" and cannot have any release values. 7C "Not Applicable" must be unchecked before you can enter values for 8.4.

Section 8.4 is not related to Sections 5 or 6.

8.5 Recycling off-site

The value for this field is calculated as follows:

8.5 = 6.2 (recycling codes M20, M24, M26, M28, M93) - 8.8(iii)

Sections 8.6 and 8.7: Treatment

These relate to an EPCRA Section 313 chemical (except for most metals and metal category compounds) or a waste containing an EPCRA Section 313 chemical that is treated for destruction on-site or is sent to a POTW or other off-site location for treatment for destruction. Most metal and category compounds are not reported in this section because they cannot be destroyed (see Appendix B). Quantities treated off-site that are reported in Section 8.8 are excluded from Section 8.7.

8.6 Treatment on-site

If Section 7A (on-site treatment) is NA, then Section 8.6 is NA; however, the reverse is not necessarily true. Since EPA requires that waste treatment methods, applied on-site to waste streams that contain the chemical (rather than treatment of the chemical itself), be reported in Section 7A, it is possible that a waste stream could be treated on-site, but the chemical itself is not being treated. Therefore, Section 8.6 could be NA when there is a value in Section 7A.

Section 8.6 is not related to Sections 5 or 6.

8.7 Treatment off-site

The value for this field is calculated as follows:

8.7 = 6.1 (Percentage Treated) + 6.2 (treated codes M40, M50, M54, M61, M69, M95) - 8.8(iv) (offsite treatment)

Some chemicals in addition to metals and metal category compounds might not be treated for destruction at a POTW. If you know that some or all of a chemical is not treated for destruction at the POTW, that quantity should be reported in Section 8.1 instead of Section 8.7.
