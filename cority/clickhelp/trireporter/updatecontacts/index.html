Update Contacts

You can easily update contact information across a large number of FormRs at the same facility, for the same year.

To save the same contact information for all FormRs at a facility:

  1. Complete the following fields in any FormR for the current year:

    Part 1, Section 3 Certification – Name and Title

    Part 1, Section 4.3 - Technical Contact Name, Phone and Email

    Part 1, Section 4.4 - Public Contact Name, Phone and Email

  2. Check the box in Section 5 at the bottom of Part I: "Use the above contact information on all FormRs at this facility."

  3. Click Save.

    The fields will be updated on all FormRs for the facility.
