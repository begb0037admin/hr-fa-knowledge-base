Part I

Section 1: Reporting Year

The reporting year is the calendar year to which the reported information applies, not the year in which you are submitting the report. Information for the 2016 reporting year must be submitted on or before July 1, 2017.

Section 2: Trade Secret Information 

Answer these questions only after you have completed the rest of the report.

If you are not making a trade secret claim, select "no," and do not complete Section 2.2.

If you are making a trade secret claim, select "yes". Only answer "yes" if you manufacture, process, or otherwise use the EPCRA Section 313 chemical whose identity is a trade secret.

If you are making a trade secret claim, in Section 2.2, check "sanitized" if this copy of the report is the public version that does not contain the EPCRA Section 313 chemical identity but does contain a generic name that is structurally descriptive in its place. Otherwise, check "unsanitized."

Section 3: Certification

The certification statement must be signed by a senior official with management responsibility for the person or persons completing the form. A senior management official must certify the accuracy and completeness of the information reported on the form by signing and dating the certification statement. This certification statement applies to all the information supplied on the form and should be signed only after the form has been completed.

The contact information in Section 3 and Section 4 (4.3 and 4.4) can be automatically updated to apply to all FormRs at the facility. See Update Contacts.

Date Submitted: It is recommended that you leave the Date Submitted field blank. The current date will be populated automatically when you print the FormR or create EFiles.

You may enter a date in the future for your printed FormR if you wish. It is not possible to create FormRs or EFiles with past dates; past dates will be converted automatically to the current date.

Section 4: Facility Identification

The Facility identification fields are automatically completed from the information stored for the location. To edit these fields, see Edit Locations.

The contact information in Section 3 and Section 4 (4.3 and 4.4) can be automatically updated to apply to all FormRs at the facility. See Update Contacts.

Section 4.1 Facility Name, Location, TRI Facility Identification Number and Tribal Country Name

If your facility is located in Indian country as defined by 18 USC §1151 you must enter the three digit Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) tribal code in the "BIA Code" field.

Section 4.6 Dun & Bradstreet Number

All facilities should verify the accuracy of facility and parent company information (e.g., DUNS number, parent company name). (See next section.)

Section 5: Parent Company Information

To verify the accuracy of your facility and parent company Dun and Bradstreet number and name, go to: https://www.dnb.com/product/dlw/form_cc4.htm or call 1-888-814-1435 to verify your information.

Callers to the toll free phone number should understand that the Dun and Bradstreet support representatives will need to verify that callers requesting the DUNS numbers are agents of the business. Dun and Bradstreet recommends knowing basic information such as when the business originated, officer names, and the name, address, and phone number for the facility. Facilities reporting to TRI should also make sure they are providing the parent company name and Dun and Bradstreet number as of December 31st of the current reporting year.
