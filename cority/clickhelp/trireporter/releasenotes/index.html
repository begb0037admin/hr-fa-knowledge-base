EMS TRI Reporter Release Summary

TRI Reporter RY 2019 Release date: 4/20/2020

The TRI (Toxic Chemical Release Inventory) Reporting Forms and Instructions for EPA are available for completing and generating electronic files for the RY 2019.

TRI Reporter RY 2018 Release date: 5/10/2019

Addition of Nonylphenol Ethoxylates Category

  • Per EPA’s finalized rule from June 7, 2018, a category of 13 specific nonylphenol ethoxylates (NPEs) is added to the Toxic Release Inventory (TRI) list of reportable chemicals.

TRI Form and Schema Update

TRI Reporter RY 2017 Release date: 3/14/2018

RY Regulatory Updates

  • Adoption of 2017 NAICS Codes for TRI Reporting: Per EPA’s finalized rule from December 2017, all facilities that meet TRI reporting requirements are not required to use 2017 NAICS codes on TRI reporting forms.
  • FormR and Schedule 1 New Form updates

Export Offsite Locations: Users can now export offsite facility information to Excel.

General Improvements and Bug Fixes:
