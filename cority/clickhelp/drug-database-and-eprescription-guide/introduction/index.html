Introduction

 

Cority now provides an expansive drug database and ePrescription functionality for Cority-hosted, U.S. clients. An eRx licensed user who is granted the appropriate role will have access to the new drug database, and if certain other conditions are met, will be able to generate, transmit and manage prescriptions electronically. Put in more practical terms, the drug database can be used to perform extensive research on drugs (including their side effects, and any interactions or warnings), and medical providers can now use Cority to transmit employee medication orders to pharmacies electronically.

These features are integrated into the familiar Cority user interface.

To support this functionality, Cority licenses technology from both Lexicomp® and Surescripts. Cority uses the Lexicomp database to provide users with detailed information on drugs and drug interactions and the Surescripts clinical network to connect Cority users to pharmacies.
