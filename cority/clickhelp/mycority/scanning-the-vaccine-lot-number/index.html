Scanning a Vaccine Lot Number

  1. Enter a health center. 
  2. Click the Scan icon in the Vaccine Lot Number field.
    The Vaccine Lot Number window opens providing access to your camera.
  3. Scan the vaccine lot number. If the system finds a match, the Vaccine Lot Number window closes and you are taken back to the New Vaccination window with all of the vaccine information auto-populated.
