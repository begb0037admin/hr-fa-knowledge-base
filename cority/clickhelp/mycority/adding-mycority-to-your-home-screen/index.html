Adding myCority to your Home Screen

Android Devices

  1. On your device, select Settings > Add to Home screen.
  2. You will be prompted to add myCority to your home screen. Click Add.

Apple Devices

  1. Using an Apple device, open the Safari browser and enter your myCority URL.
  2. From the menu bar, click  .
  3. Select Add to Home screen.
