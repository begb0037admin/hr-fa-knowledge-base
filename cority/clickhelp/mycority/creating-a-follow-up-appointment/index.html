Creating a Revisit (Follow-up) Appointment

If the clinician needs to gather additional information from the employee to continue the case, the clinician will:

  1. Navigate to the Case record.
  2. Select the Schedule Referral action. The Appointments calendar will open.
  3. Create a new appointment (schedule entry).
  4. On the Schedule Entry record for the appointment, fill in all required information.

The employee’s name and the case number will be auto-populated.

  1. Click Save.
  2. If the Activity field value is of the Clinic Visit type, click Actions and then click Link. This will open a new Clinic Visit record linked to the case. The system now treats this as a revisit.
  3. Click Save. A prompt will alert the user that he or she is creating a revisit for the employee.
  4. Click Yes. The new Clinic Visit record will save.
  5. Under Case Management, select the Clinic Visit module. On the Clinic Visit list view, search for the case number. Both the new and original Clinic Visit records will be listed.
  6. Once all required information is gathered and complete, finish the assessment and send it to the employee with the status of the questionnaire set to Awaiting Consent. The employee can then choose to provide his or her consent to share the assessment with the referring Manager. For information on providing employee consent, see Providing Employee Consent for Assessment Disclosure.
