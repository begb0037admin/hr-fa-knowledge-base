Accessing myCority

You can access myCority from a web browser on your desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile device. To access myCority, launch a web browser and enter the URL of your company’s myCority site (https://mycompany.my.cority.com).

You may need to whitelist ‘my.cority.com’ in order to access the site.
