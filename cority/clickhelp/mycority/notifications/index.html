Notifications

The Notifications list in myCority displays recent communications from your supervisor or administrator, such as new assignments, upcoming appointments, or pending event reports.


To access your notifications, expand Account at the bottom of the side menu and click Notifications.

Unread notifications will display in bold text. Click View to mark the notification as ‘read’. If you click Dismiss, the notification will be deleted from the list.  If you click Clear All, all of your notifications will be deleted. Click Load More to see additional notifications.

If the notification is not linked to a record, the View button will not display. Click View to open the record, or X to close the Notifications list.
