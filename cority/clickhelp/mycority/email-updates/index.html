Email Updates

Your supervisor can request email updates each time you submit or modify a record. If you have performed an action for which email updates have been requested, the Send an email form will display. On the form, the subject and body text will be automatically populated.

Enter the recipient’s email address and enter any additional information. Click Select files to attach a file. When you are finished composing the email, click Send.
