Creating or Viewing an Asset

After you scan or upload a code, the myCority Asset View screen will open, displaying the equipment code, equipment type, and other details associated with the asset.

If there is no asset associated with the code, a message will display asking if you want to create a new asset. Select Create.

The myCority Asset View screen will display. Complete the form to the best of your ability. When you are finished, click Save.
