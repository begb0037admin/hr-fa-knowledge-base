Adding a Vaccine Lot Number

There are two ways to add a vaccine lot number:

  • Scanning a Vaccine Lot Number
  • Entering a Vaccine Lot Number

From the Home Screen, in the Create New section, click New Vaccination. The New Vaccination form opens.

The first time you use this form, myCority asks to access your camera. Click Allow.

For vaccine lot numbers that are not associated with the selected Health Center, a pop-up displays asking if the user wants to update the Health Center value on the record. If you click Yes , the Health Center will update to match the selected Vaccine lot health center. If you click No, then you will have to pick a different vaccine lot number.
