Printing or Exporting a Record

  1. Select a record from the My Records view.
  2. Click the vertical ellipsis at the top right.
  3. Click Print or Export. A read-only view of the record will display. The view will have Print, Export to PDF, and Back to previous view buttons.
  4. To print the record, click Print. If your device is connected to a printer, a Print dialog will display. Select your desired printer settings and click Print.
  1. To export the task to PDF format, click Export to PDF. A PDF copy of the record will download to your device.
  2. Click X to return to the record.
