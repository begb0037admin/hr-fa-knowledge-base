Creating a Manager Referred Appointment

After a manager or supervisor submits a questionnaire through myCority to refer an employee to a clinic, with the auto-post to case feature configured, a case will automatically be created in GX2. From the Case record, a clinician can create a manager referred appointment. 

For the clinician:

  1. In GX2, navigate to the case and review.
  2. If further information is required, contact the referral manager using the Messages tab.

The referral manager can also use the Messages tab, to inquire with the clinician about status, et.

  1. From the case, create a Schedule Entry (appointment).

The appointment must be created from the case to avoid breaking the link between the appointment and case.

If case type security is necessary, then the security action 'schedule.applycasetypesecuritytoscheduleentry' must be granted to the user’s role. Otherwise, the Schedule Entry section will appear as usual.

  1. On the Schedule Entry, fill in all the required information.

On the Schedule Entry, the employee’s name and case number are auto-populated.

  1. Click Save.
  2. If the Activity field value is of the Clinic Visit type, click Actions, and then click Link. This will take you to the Clinic Visit record.
  3. Ensure all the content on the Clinic Visit record has been updated based on the information in the Schedule Entry (for example: case number, employee name, scheduled date, etc.)
  4. Click Save.
  5. Click Yes. The Clinic Visit record saves and links to the Schedule Entry.
  6. Under Case Management, navigate to the Clinic Visit list view, and search for the Clinic Visit record by case number.
  7. Click Schedule History. The appointment created is listed in the Schedule History.
  8. After the appointment, if the information gathered during the appointment is incomplete and a revisit required, create a revisit appointment (see Creating a Revisit).
  9. If the information gathered during the appointment is complete, finish and send the assessment to the employee with the status of the questionnaire set to Awaiting Consent. On the assessment, the employee can then choose to provide consent to share the assessment with the referring manager. (See Providing Employee Consent for Assessment Disclosure).
