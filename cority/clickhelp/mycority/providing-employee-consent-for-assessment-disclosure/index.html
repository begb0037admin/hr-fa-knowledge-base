Providing Employee Consent for Assessment Disclosure

Adding employee consent to an Occupational Health assessment, provides employees the option of whether they accept the assessment (questionnaire) and are willing to share the information in the assessment to the manager of supervisor who referred them to the assessment. If the employee does not provide consent and it is applicable, the OH team (or nurse) can make changes to the assessment and send the updated assessment for consent. This questionnaire is completed by the OH team (or nurse) to assess whether the employee is fit to work with restrictions.

 Providing employee consent is not used with public questionnaires.

The following new fields can be added to custom versions of Questionnaire layouts:

  • Consent Status – is a drop-down field with the following options:
    • Awaiting Consent;
    • Consent Declined; and
    • Consent Provided
  • Date Consent Provided – is a read-only field that is populated automatically if the employee provides consent
  • Date Consent Declined – a read-only field that is populated automatically if the employee declines consent
  • Declination Reason – a text field in which the employee can provide a reason for declining consent
  • Share with Supervisor – a checkbox that will share the assessment with the employee’s supervisor

Once information gathered during the clinic visit appointment for the assessment is complete, the OH team (a nurse) completes the assessment and has it sent to the employee with the status of the questionnaire set to “awaiting consent”. From here the employee can choose to provide their consent.

If the employee is not comfortable with sharing or disagrees with the assessment.

The employee can:

  1. Select Consent Declined. The Date Consent Declined field populates automatically.
  2. Fill in the Declination Reason textbox.

The employee does not have access to make changes to the questionnaire itself. The employee can only change the Consent Status on the assessment.

  1. Click Save. The status of the assessment is changed to Consent Declined.

The OH team (a nurse) will:

  1. Review the employees’ reason for declining consent determine if the declination is applicable, and then update the assessment.
  2. Update the consent status to Awaiting Consent and send the assessment back to the employee.

If the employee is comfortable with sharing and agrees with the assessment.

The employee can:

  1. Select Consent Provided. The Date Consent Provided field populates automatically, and the status of the assessment updates to consent provided for the assessment on the side.
  2. Click Save. The status of the assessment is changed to Consent Declined.

To share the assessment with the Manager or Supervisor, the OH team (a nurse) will:

  1. Once the status of the assessment is updated to Consent Provided on your side, click the Share with Supervisor checkbox.
  2. Click Save.

The assessment is shared with the Manager or Supervisor of the employee requesting the assessment in either GX2 or in myCority if the supervisor is configured to be a myCority user.

You can also email the assessment to the Manager or Supervisor.

To email the assessment to the Manager or Supervisor, the OH team (a nurse) will:

  1. On the list of assessment page, click the View drop down textbox at the top right of the page, and then click Consent status. The first column in the table will be set to Consent Status.
  2. Click the checkbox in front of a Consent Provided assessment.
  3. Click Action at the top of the screen, a drop-down menu appears.
  4. Click Email. The Email window displays with the from section auto populated.

Only one Consent Provided assessment can be selected at a time to email. If more than one Consent Provided assessment is selected a “Cority Message” prompt displays “Selected action cannot be applied to multiple records”. Click OK, and ensure you have only selected one assessment, then click Action, and then click Email.

 If a checkbox in front of an assessment that consent is not provided is selected, when you click Action, and then click Email. A confirm prompt appears.

  1. On the Email window, fill in the To, Subject, and Body sections.
  2. Click Send. A “Cority Message” prompt displays “Email sent Successfully”.
  3. Click OK.
