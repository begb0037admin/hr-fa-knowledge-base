How do I process a leaver in PeopleXD?

To process a leaver in PeopleXD, follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to Employee → Leavers in PeopleXD CoreHR.
  2. Search for the employee and open their record.
  3. Enter the Leaving date and select the Reason for leaving.
  4. Complete the Exit questionnaire if required by your HR policy.
  5. Submit — payroll will be notified automatically for the final pay run.
